### 基本的 1♣（16+ 或 17+ balanced）

-   1♦: 0-7
    -   1♥: 4+ card major (MAFIA)，forcing 1 round
        -   1♠（在 1♥ 之后）: 4+ spades，denies 4 hearts，forcing
            -   1NT: 1444「很可能」，NF
            -   2m: minor 中有 4+，可能是 5/4 或 4/5，NF
            -   2♥: 6+ hearts
            -   2♠: 4 spades，16-17 或 3 spades，16-19
            -   2NT: 21-22，很可能有 0-1 spades
            -   3m: 5m/4♥，GF
            -   3♥: 「long good suit」，invitational（某种程度上比 2♥ 更好）
            -   3♠: 3 spades，20-21 或 4 spades，18-19
            -   3NT: to play
            -   4♥: to play
            -   4m: splinter (shortness)；4+ spades，20+
        -   1NT: 0-5 HCP，denying 4 card support，denying 4 spades
        -   Pass: 可能/很可能是 1444，NF
            -   2m: minor 中有 4+，可能是 5/4 或 4/5，NF
            -   2♥: 通常有 6+ hearts
            -   2♠: 类似 reverse。21+ HCP，5+ hearts，4 spades
            -   2NT: 22-23，NF
            -   3m: 5+m/4♥，GF
            -   3♥: 「long good suit」，invitational（某种程度上比 2♥ 更好）
            -   3♠: 3 spades，20-21 或 4 spades，18-19
            -   3NT: to play
            -   4♥: to play
            -   4m: splinter (shortness)；4+ spades，20+
    -   1NT: 17-19
        -   与 1NT opening 时的 responses 相同
    -   2NT: 22-24（若有 20-21，就会 opened 2NT）
        -   与 2NT opening 时的 responses 相同
    -   3NT: 25-27

### 在 1♦-2♣/2♦ 之后

如果是 balanced，opener 的 priority bid 是 2♥，以便在 3NT 的情况下 hide info（1♦-2♦-2NT/3NT 除外）。如果 responder indicates slam interest，之后可以 reveal support。

如果是 unbalanced (singleton/void)，opener 应该 bid 2♠ 或 3♣，或（极少数情况下，在适用时）3M。

-   2♦（在 2♣ 之后）: 5 或更多 diamonds，unbalanced，denies 4+♣
    -   2M: semi-natural，confirms GF
    -   3♣/3♦: non-forcing，推测是 natural
-   2♥: 11-13 balanced。
    -   2♠: puppet 至 2NT
        -   2NT: forced；当 responder 的 majors 中有 junk 时，responder 希望这样，以 protect majors 免受 initial lead 的影响
            -   3m: GF，slam interest
            -   3M: minor 中有 6+，major 中有 exactly 4（如果 minor 或 major 中有 5，initial response 是 1M；如果有 6/5，就会用 major 来 responded 2H，而不会 puppeted 至 2NT）
            -   3NT: to play，希望由 opener 来 declare
    -   2NT: slam interest，balanced，asks for 4-card major
    -   同一 minor 的 3（或 2♦ 之后的 3♣）: invite，non-forcing
    -   major 的 3: minor 中有 6，major 中有 5，GF
    -   3NT: to play
-   2♠: 4-card support，针对 responder 的 minor 的 undisclosed splinter，GF（不能是 short diamonds）
    -   2NT: 针对 shortness 的 asking bid
        -   3♦: short ♠
        -   3♥: short ♥
        -   3♠: short ♣
-   2NT（在 2♣ 之后）: 4441，minimum
-   3NT（在 2♣ 之后）: 4441，maximum
-   2NT（在 2♦ 之后）: 6 diamonds balanced，minimum
-   3NT（在 2♦ 之后）: 6 diamonds balanced，maximum
-   3♣: responder 的 minor 中有 3，另一个 minor 中有 5，4-card major。GF
    -   3♦: asks for major
-   3♦: 6+♦，very good suit，GF
-   3M: 6♦，major 中有 5

如果 responder 是 passed hand（limited to 10HCP）：

-   Pass: to play，甚至可能在 misfit 时也这样做
-   2♦（在 2♣ 之后）: to play，甚至可能在 misfit 时也这样做
-   2NT: 不能 tolerate ♦/♣，to play
-   其他任何叫法：natural，game interest

### 在 1♦-2♥/2♠ (reverse Flannery) 之后

2♥: 5♠，4 或 5♥，6-9HCP。2♠: 5♠，4 或 5♥，invitational。作为 responder，持有 GF hand 时不要使用 Flannery。而应 respond 1♠，然后照常继续。

-   Pass: to play
-   2♠（在 2♥ 之后）: to play
-   2NT: game interest，asking for shape 和 points
    -   3♣: 5♠-4♥，min
    -   3♦: 5♠-4♥，max
    -   3♥: 5♠-5♥，min
    -   3♠: 5♠-5♥，max
-   3♣: 5-5 minors，asking responder 来 set contract。没有 game interest
-   3♦: showing diamonds，推测是 6+？
-   game contract: to play

### 在 2♣-2♦ 之后

-   2♥: 有 4-card major
    -   2♠: asking for 4-card major（structure 是 ♥low/♠low/♥max/♠max）
        -   2NT/3♣: hearts/spades，10-13HCP
            -   Pass: sign off
            -   3♣（在 2NT 之后）: sign off
            -   opener 的 major 的 3: game invite
            -   另一个 major 的 3: 在 opener 的 major 中进行 slam try
            -   game: to play
        -   3♦/♥: hearts/spades，14-15HCP，GF
            -   opener 的 suits 的 3: slam try
            -   game: to play
    -   2NT: natural invite
    -   3♣: natural invite
    -   3♦: 在 clubs 中进行 slam try
-   2♠: medium 或 maximum，没有 4-card major
    -   2NT: asking bid
        -   3♣: medium
        -   3♦: maximum，singleton/void diamonds
        -   3♥: maximum，singleton/void hearts
        -   3♠: maximum，singleton/void spades
        -   3NT: maximum，balanced（既然是 2♣ opening，就不可能是 short clubs）
    -   3♣: sign off，to play。Opener 必须 pass
    -   3♦: 在 clubs 中进行 slam try
    -   3M: 5 cards exactly
-   2NT: maximum，没有 4-card major，两个 majors 中都有 stoppers
    -   3♦: 在 clubs 中进行 slam try
    -   3M: 5 cards exactly
-   3♣: minimum hand，没有 4-card major
    -   3♦: 在 clubs 中进行 slam try
    -   3M: 5 cards exactly
-   3♦/3M: 5-card suit（此外还有 6+ clubs），GF

3M responses 是 exactly 5，因为若有 6+，responder 就会用 3♣/3♦/3♥ invitational transfers 来 responded opening bid。

### 在 2♣-2NT 之后

-   3♣: 必须如此（2NT 是 puppet，要么是 pre-emptive raise，要么是 5-5 GF hand）
    -   3♦: 5-5 hearts-spades
    -   3♥: 5-5 diamonds-spades
    -   3♠: 5-5 diamonds-hearts

没有必要 show 5 clubs，因为持有 5-club GF hand 时，responder 就会先 bid 2♦，接着 bid 3♦，以便在 clubs 中进行 slam try

### 在 2♦-2NT 之后

2NT 是 asking bid。持有某个 major 的 5 以及 7-9HCP 时，responder 可以用 3M 来 respond。如果是 invitational，并且在寻找 5-3/4-4/5-4 fit（或 NT game），则可以 respond 2NT。

-   3♣: minimum，任何 shape
    -   3♦: asks for 3-card major
        -   3M: shows 3-card major，4315 或 3415 shape
        -   3NT: majors 中为 4-4，4414 或 4405 shape
    -   其他任何叫法：sign off
-   3♦: maximum，majors 中为 4-4，GF
    -   3M: flag，随后的 bids 是 control bids
    -   3NT: to play
-   3M: maximum，shows 3-card major

### After 1♦-2♣/2♦

If balanced, priority bid by opener is 2♥ to hide info in case of 3NT (except for 1♦-2♦-2NT/3NT). Can reveal support later if responder indicates slam interest.

If unbalanced (singleton/void), opener should bid 2♠ or 3♣ or (very rarely, when applicable) 3M.

-   2♦ (over 2♣): 5 or more diamonds, unbalanced, denies 4+♣
    -   2M: semi-natural, confirms GF
    -   3♣/3♦: non-forcing, presumably natural
-   2♥: 11-13 balanced.
    -   2♠: puppet to 2NT
        -   2NT: forced, responder wants this when responder has junk in majors to protect majors from initial lead
            -   3m: GF, slam interest
            -   3M: 6+ of minor, exactly 4 of major (with 5 of minor or major, initial response is 1M, with 6/5, would have responded to 2H with major and not puppeted to 2NT)
            -   3NT: to play, wanted opener to declare
    -   2NT: slam interest, balanced, asks for 4-card major
    -   3 of same minor (or 3♣ after 2♦): invite, non-forcing
    -   3 of major: 6 of minor, 5 of major, GF
    -   3NT: to play
-   2♠: 4-card support, undisclosed splinter for responder's minor, GF (can't be short diamonds)
    -   2NT: asking bid for shortness
        -   3♦: short ♠
        -   3♥: short ♥
        -   3♠: short ♣
-   2NT (over 2♣): 4441, minimum
-   3NT (over 2♣): 4441, maximum
-   2NT (over 2♦): 6 diamonds balanced, minimum
-   3NT (over 2♦): 6 diamonds balanced, maximum
-   3♣: 3 in responder's minor, 5 in other minor, 4-card major. GF
    -   3♦: asks for major
-   3♦: 6+♦, very good suit, GF
-   3M: 6♦, 5 of major

If responder is a passed hand (limited to 10HCP):

-   Pass: to play, may even do this in a misfit
-   2♦ (over 2♣): to play, may even do this in a misfit
-   2NT: can't tolerate ♦/♣, to play
-   anything else: natural, game interest

### After 1♦-2♥/2♠ (reverse Flannery)

2♥: 5♠, 4 or 5♥, 6-9HCP. 2♠: 5♠, 4 or 5♥, invitational. As responder, do not use Flannery with GF hand. Instead, respond 1♠, and continue as normal.

-   Pass: to play
-   2♠ (over 2♥): to play
-   2NT: game interest, asking for shape and points
    -   3♣: 5♠-4♥, min
    -   3♦: 5♠-4♥, max
    -   3♥: 5♠-5♥, min
    -   3♠: 5♠-5♥, max
-   3♣: 5-5 minors, asking responder to set contract. No game interest
-   3♦: showing diamonds, presumably 6+?
-   game contract: to play

### After 2♣-2♦

-   2♥: have a 4-card major
    -   2♠: asking for 4-card major (structure is ♥low/♠low/♥max/♠max)
        -   2NT/3♣: hearts/spades, 10-13HCP
            -   Pass: sign off
            -   3♣ (over 2NT): sign off
            -   3 of opener's major: game invite
            -   3 of other major: slam try in opener's major
            -   game: to play
        -   3♦/♥: hearts/spades, 14-15HCP, GF
            -   3 of opener's suits: slam try
            -   game: to play
    -   2NT: natural invite
    -   3♣: natural invite
    -   3♦: slam try in clubs
-   2♠: medium or maximum, no 4-card major
    -   2NT: asking bid
        -   3♣: medium
        -   3♦: maximum, singleton/void diamonds
        -   3♥: maximum, singleton/void hearts
        -   3♠: maximum, singleton/void spades
        -   3NT: maximum, balanced (couldn't have been short clubs with 2♣ opening)
    -   3♣: sign off, to play. Opener required to pass
    -   3♦: slam try in clubs
    -   3M: 5 cards exactly
-   2NT: maximum, no 4-card major, stoppers in both majors
    -   3♦: slam try in clubs
    -   3M: 5 cards exactly
-   3♣: minimum hand, no 4-card major
    -   3♦: slam try in clubs
    -   3M: 5 cards exactly
-   3♦/3M: 5-card suit (in addition to 6+ clubs), GF

3M responses are exactly 5 because responder would've responded to opening bid with 3♣/3♦/3♥ invitational transfers with 6+.

### After 2♣-2NT

-   3♣: required (2NT is puppet, either pre-emptive raise or 5-5 GF hand)
    -   3♦: 5-5 hearts-spades
    -   3♥: 5-5 diamonds-spades
    -   3♠: 5-5 diamonds-hearts

There isn't a need to show 5 clubs because, with a 5-club GF hand, responder would've bid 2♦ followed by 3♦ for a slam try in clubs

### After 2♦-2NT

2NT is an asking bid. With 5 of a major and 7-9HCP, responder can respond with 3M. If invitational and looking for 5-3/4-4/5-4 fit (or NT game), can respond 2NT.

-   3♣: minimum, any shape
    -   3♦: asks for 3-card major
        -   3M: shows 3-card major 4315 or 3415 shape
        -   3NT: 4-4 in the majors, 4414 or 4405 shape
    -   anything else: sign off
-   3♦: maximum, 4-4 in the majors, GF
    -   3M: flag, subsequent bids are control bids
    -   3NT: to play
-   3M: maximum, shows 3-card major
