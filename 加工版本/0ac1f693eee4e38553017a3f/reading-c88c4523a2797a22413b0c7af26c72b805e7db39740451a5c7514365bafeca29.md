## 原文

Today I’m excited to make available the code and downloads for two books of music I’ve collected for my periodic singing parties: one book of carols ([Github](https://github.com/benkuhn/carols), [PDF](https://github.com/benkuhn/carols/releases/download/v1.0/book.pdf)) and one book of rounds ([Github](https://github.com/benkuhn/rounds), [PDF](https://github.com/benkuhn/rounds/releases/download/v1.0/book.pdf)). I’ve used them for enough singing parties that I think I’ve worked out the worst of the kinks, so it’s time to release them.

I had a few motivations in putting these books together, rather than using other existing books:

-   They don’t have all the tunes I like.
-   They take up space with tunes I don’t like.
-   They pick bad arrangements.
-   They pick bad lyrics.
-   They make typos that I can’t fix.
-   It’s expensive to buy enough copies to have a good singing party.

If you’ve had any of these problems, I hope you find these books useful! I’m open to suggestions for new carols, edits to existing ones, or (of course) pull requests.

Happy singing!
