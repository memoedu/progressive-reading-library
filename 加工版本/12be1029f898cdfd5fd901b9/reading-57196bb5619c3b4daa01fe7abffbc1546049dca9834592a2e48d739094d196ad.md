# You should try contra dancing

---

## 正文

如今在旧金山，一个流行的破冰话题是：“如果 AGI 意味着谁都不必工作，你会怎样度过一生？”对我来说，我想答案中大得出人意料的一部分，是一种听起来有点土气的 folk dance，叫作 contra dancing。

我开始尝试回答这个问题时，想的是：有哪些事情是我以 _atelically_ 的方式去做的——因为我喜欢事情本身，而不是为了追求某个更长远的目标？对我来说，其中很多都与这样的事情有关：(1) 让身体感到愉悦，(2) 帮助我感到与他人相联结。

我推荐 contra dancing 的理由是，在让身体愉悦、让人与人联结的活动中，它是那些_最容易达到好玩程度_的活动之一，因此也最适合拿来入门。许多其他形式的 dance 都需要上好几周的 lessons，之后才会有人鼓励你去社交场合跳。相比之下，contra dancing 的 format 意味着，你可以在第一次 dance 之前参加半小时的 beginner lesson，而且到那天晚上结束时，你很可能已经玩得十分开心。

更重要的是，这么容易就能开始享受乐趣，也塑造了它的 community 和 priorities，使它成为我参加的所有活动中氛围最好的活动之一。

* * *

我第一次接触 contra 是在初中。我妈妈经营着一个 intentional community，当时一位叫 Bree 的室友搬了进来。Bree 对这个叫作“contra dancing”的东西_痴迷不已_，每周都会试着说服我和其他室友跟她一起去。

而我坚决拒绝。我是个极其不酷的初中生，因此，我害怕做任何哪怕带着一丝不酷气息的事，免得又给同学多一个评判我的理由。我也非常局促；我甚至从没去过学校舞会，也几乎不和女生说话。但 Bree 锲而不舍，另一位室友也迷上了它，最后我也好奇起来。

不过，那时我已经摆出了一副坚定讨厌 contra 的架势，害怕如果承认自己改变了主意，室友们就会取笑我。所以我继续拒绝。

最后，比我还怀疑这件事的室友 Thomas 告诉 Bree，只有我去他才去，大概是想让她别再烦他，转而来烦我。啊哈——我的绝佳机会！我可以把自己的回心转意伪装成对 Thomas 的恶作剧。“好啊，没问题！一言为定！”

一个星期四晚上，Bree 和我来到 [Concord Scout House](https://www.neffa.org/thursday-contras/)，直接加入了下一支 dance。那是一支异常 complex 的 [square dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_dance)，我搞糊涂了——事实上，我糊涂得让整个 square 最后跳到一半就放弃了。✻✻ 这种事的发生概率至多只有 1-in-10,000；在此后 20 多年经常参加 dancing 的日子里，我再没见过这种事。

我羞愧得无地自容。我不仅在参加一项极其不酷的活动，还是_那里最不酷的人_，用自己的无能和笨拙毁掉了那些比我稍微没那么不酷的人的乐趣。

但其他 dancers 似乎并不这么看。他们没有怨恨或取笑我，而是说：“天哪，刚才那支真的很难！”“抱歉，平时真的不是这样的！”“请别放弃，再试几支 dances 吧！想和我一起 dance 吗？”

我又试了几支 dances，没有再引发任何崩盘，到那天晚上结束时，我满心都是兴奋与喜悦。第二周我又去了，再下一周也是。我参加了长达十二小时的 [Snow Ball](https://www.monadnockfolk.org/%E2%98%BC-%E2%98%BC-%E2%98%BC-peterborough-snow-ball-2026/)，然后又参加了持续整个周末的 [Flurry Festival](https://www.flurryfestival.org/)。当人们开始期待和我一起 dance 时，我有生以来第一次觉得自己很酷；当其中一些人约我出去时，我就觉得自己更酷了。有一段时间，它占据了我生活中很大的一部分。虽然如今我 dancing 的强度可能只有最投入时的四分之一，它仍然是我所做的最让我快乐、也最珍惜的事情之一。

我带去参加 contra dancing 的人里，大约每十个人就有一个会这样：某一刻忽然开了窍，它便成为他们生活中很大的一部分。

* * *

那么，contra dancing 到底_是_什么？

别人这样问我时，我常常觉得难以回答，因为描述一支 contra dance 的 mechanics，并不能真正传达它的 essence，也不能说明它为什么好玩；但如果不理解 mechanics，又很难真正理解 essence。所以我会两方面都试着说说。但你得答应读到讲 essence 的部分，不能只是听着 mechanics 有点傻，就不看了。

从 Mechanically 的角度来说：你来到 dance hall。你邀请某个人 dance，或者对方邀请你。你加入其他 couples 排成的一条 long line（“set”），与 adjacent 的一对（“neighbor”）couple 组成 four-person group，牵起手。一位 _caller_ 站在 dance hall 的顶端，为你们即将跳的 dance 做一次“walk-through”，依次 calling out 一系列 figures（“circle left three places”“对你的 neighbor 做 do-si-do”等）。到结束时，你已经沿着 line 向上或向下 moved，准备好与迎面而来的一些新 neighbors 做同样的事情。

caller 旁边是一支 live band。（他们的声音大概有点像[这样](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/1uAQAUheWc3NjVE6RFAkwt)。）pianist plays [four potatoes](https://www.fiddlehangout.com/archive/740)，fiddler 随即开始一支 reel。在 dance 的前几次循环中，caller 会 calls out 那些 figures，等 dancers 进入 flow 后便不再出声。

看起来有点像这个视频，不过我不知道它能传达出多少那种氛围，因为 contra 自己跳起来远比看别人跳好玩：

额外彩蛋：你能在这支 dance 里看到高中时的我！开头画面最右边那个穿深红色 T 恤的人就是我。

* * *

对我而言，contra dancing 的 _essence_ 在于 flow、喜悦和 community。

光看其实看不出来，但好的 contra dancing，很大程度上在于学会如何与其他 dancers 来回 trade momentum（“share weight”），让每个 figure 都 flows seamlessly into 下一个 figure。当你做对时，感觉妙不可言。partner 握着你手的方式所发生的 Subtle changes，会 cue 你进入下一个 figure。dance 变得 effortless。你可以关掉脑子，只管跟着这个巨大的 dancing super-organism 的邀请去做。

它也让身体感到愉悦。contra dancing 的 core figure 叫作 _swing_，其中你和 partner 采用 waltz-like hold，围绕一个 shared center 进行 pivot。做对时，感觉像是飞翔与顶级拥抱的结合。即使不在 swing 中，shared weight、smooth momentum 和 twirling 的“flourishes”也会让你感到 weightless、graceful，并与 music 和其他 dancers 深深相联。

许多种 dance 在跳得好时都会让人感到 flowy、兴奋不已。contra 的不同之处在于，达到这种状态非常容易。大多数形式的 dance 都需要上好几周的 lessons，才会开始好玩。在 contra 中，你只需要在 dance 开始前半小时到场，参加一次 beginner lesson。†† 事实上，你可能连这一步都省掉也没问题！当然，在那之后还有很多可学——improving 你的 swing technique、学习如何 share weight，以及在哪里 put flourishes 才能获得最好的喜悦和 flow——但所有这些，都是为了让你在乐趣量表上从 8/10 达到 11/10；达到 8 其实相当容易。

因此，contra 让你很容易在和任何人 dancing 时，都能享受到 baseline level 的乐趣。而且，因为它是一种 group dance，最终你会感到自己与整个 set 相联，而不只是与你的 partner 相联。我想，正是这两点，造就了 contra dancing 中我特别喜欢的另一个方面：它的 community 是如此友好、热情。

许多种 dance 都有点势利——experienced dancers 看不起 newbies，并试图避免和他们 dancing。相比之下，我第一次 dance 时把自己的 square 搞得一团糟，得到的却是道歉和“希望你能再试一次”，这样的经历在 contra 中很常见。许多 contras 都会明确请 experienced dancers 与 newcomers 一起 dance。玩得这么开心的时候，实在很难势利起来！你会想把这份快乐分享给所有人。‡‡ 这还有一个好处，就是 contra 比大多数形式的 dance 都更加 intergenerational。我的 dance 朋友里既有二十多岁的，也有七十多岁的！

正如读过初稿的 Chloe 所说：“我想，在很多方面，你第一次 dance 的故事就是它的核心——你怎么做都不会错。或者，即便你做错了，它不知怎的也会带来更多的爱。”

* * *

在我看来，这让 contra dancing 成为_最好的 dance 入门方式之一_：

-   每支 dance 都会先被 walked through，所以你不必担心要决定做什么。
-   你可以非常快地达到 8/10 的乐趣水平，让 practice 变得值得享受，而不是一项苦差事。
-   这个 community 热情得不可思议，让身为 beginner 的压力小了很多。

它有足够的 structured 特点，能够做到 beginner-friendly，同时也为 improvisation 和 technique 留出了足够空间，使它很有 depth。这让它成为通向其他 dances 和 movement practices 的绝佳入口。我刚开始 contra dancing 时，对 improvised dancing 有很多恐惧和心理障碍（例如，在普通的美国高中／婚礼风格舞会上，我跳起舞来会觉得不自在），但最近我尝试了 [fusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusion_dance) 和 [contact improv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_improvisation)，两种都玩得很开心。人们以 contra 为入口，进入更难的 dance 形式，是很常见的事。

不过，最重要的是，它本身就能带来无比的快乐！在我知道的活动中，只有这一项让我听到好几个朋友提起：每次去，他们都会笑得脸颊疼。

* * *

不只是好玩，读过初稿的几个人还各自提到，contra dance 恰好与……生活中其他方面让他们烦恼的许多事情形成鲜明对照：

-   我生活中的许多时光，都是一动不动、安安静静地在屏幕前度过的；而 contra 则将 three-dimensional、充满 movement 和 sound 的体验发挥到了极致。

-   许多场合的默认氛围，是某种 ironic detachment。§§ 至少对我而言，过度使用 ironic detachment，会对我看待世界、与世界互动的方式产生细微却深远的影响。这是逗人开心的简单办法，但最后却会掩盖或麻痹许多更“真实”的反应。我发现，玩一种我称为“the ironic detachment game”的游戏十分有趣，还有点 psychoactive 的效果；在这个游戏里，我不许自己用 ironically detached 的方式回应任何事情。而 contra dancing 的默认氛围，则是真诚的喜悦。

-   在日常生活中，人很容易变得 atomized、isolated。在一场 contra 中，每个人都做着相同的 steps，共同完成 dance；它有着深厚的 communal 特质。

-   在许多社交场合（尤其是在旧金山），我常觉得别人正在“掂量”我，看看我有多有趣、多重要。而在大多数 contras 中，人们只是因为我在场就很开心。

-   我做的许多事情（工作、choral music、blogging……）几乎都把卓越放在一切之上来奖励。Contra 却 welcomes imperfection：我们宁愿要一支 amateur live band，也不要 recorded professionals，而且不认为 organized teaching 应该超过半小时。

这让它成为应对许多日常 stressors 的一剂让人踏实、滋养身心的 antitode。初中时，我那么被它吸引，我想不只是因为好玩：在某个深层意义上，它对我非常有益。

* * *

如果我已经让你跃跃欲试，想去试试 contra，接下来你应该这样做：

-   前往 [try contra dot com](https://www.trycontra.com/)，寻找你附近的 dances。如果有多个 dances，我建议试着弄清哪个规模最大（规模更大的 dances 往往更有活力、氛围也更好），不过，单凭网站可能不太容易判断。
-   记下 dance 的地点和时间，包括 beginner lesson。记得提前到场参加！
-   关于 Dress code：contra dancing 通常属于 light cardio；穿你觉得方便活动的衣服。‖‖ 我个人很容易出汗，通常会带几件上衣替换，不过我认识的大多数其他 dancers 都不需要这样做。
    -   大多数人穿得很随意；许多女性（还有一些男性！）会穿裙子，尤其是那些在你 twirl 时会展现有趣效果的裙子。
    -   查看 dance 的网站，看看有没有对 footwear 作出说明；有些 dances 要求你穿鞋底干净的 soft-soled shoes，以免损坏地板。
    -   带上水瓶！大多数 dances 都有饮水设施，但由于 dances 之间的休息时间很短，能更快喝到水会方便一些。
-   如果你参加了一次 dance 而且很喜欢，那么从 8/10 的乐趣提升到 9.5/10，最简单的方法就是找一个 swing 特别好的人，问问对方你可以怎样 improve 你的 technique。（如果你拿不准，就找一个 fast 而且 smooth 的人。）你开口请教，会让对方很开心！¶¶ Teaching 一个 swing 与仅仅拥有一个好的 swing 是两种不同的 skill，所以你不妨多问几个人。
-   如果你有朋友会 contra dances，试着和他们一起去！他们会非常高兴带你去，你们双方也都会玩得更开心。✻✻✻✻ 我最近开始了一项让朋友们喜欢上 contra dancing 的行动（也因此写了这篇文章）！进展\_非常顺利\_，而且这也让我玩得开心多了！

玩得开心！希望你笑得脸颊都疼。

## 原文

A popular icebreaker in San Francisco these days is “How would you spend your life if AGI meant nobody needed to work?” For me, I think a surprisingly big part of the answer is a dorky-sounding kind of folk dance called contra dancing.

I started trying to answer that question by thinking: what are the things I do _atelically_—because I enjoy them for their own sake, not in pursuit of some longer-term goal? For me, a lot of that has to do with things that are (1) physically joyful, and (2) help me feel connected to other people.

My pitch for contra dancing is that among physically joyful and connecting activities, it’s one of the ones where it’s _easiest to get to the point where it’s fun_, and therefore one of the best to start out with. Many other forms of dance require weeks of lessons before you’re even encouraged to do it socially at all. By contrast, the format of contra dancing means that you can go to a half-hour beginner lesson before your first dance, and there’s a good chance you will be having a lot of fun by the end of the evening.

What’s more, the fact that it’s so easy to start having fun shapes its community and priorities, such that it has among the best vibes of any activity I do.

* * *

I first encountered contra in middle school. My mom ran an intentional community, and we had a housemate move in named Bree. Bree was _obsessed_ with this thing called “contra dancing,” and every week she would try to convince me and our other housemates to come with her.

Which I absolutely refused. I was an extremely uncool middle schooler, and that meant I was terrified of doing anything that even whiffed of uncoolness, lest I give my classmates another reason to judge me. I was also deeply awkward; I’d never even been to a school dance and barely talked to girls. But Bree was persistent, and one of our other housemates got hooked, and eventually I was intrigued.

By then, though, I was dug in as a contra hater, and I was scared that if I admitted I had changed my mind, my housemates would make fun of me. So I kept refusing.

Finally, my housemate Thomas, who was even more of a skeptic than I was, told Bree that he would only go if I went, presumably so that she would stop bugging him and bug me instead. Aha—my golden opportunity! I could pass off my change of heart as a prank on Thomas. “Okay, sure! It’s a deal!”

Bree and I showed up at the [Concord Scout House](https://www.neffa.org/thursday-contras/) one Thursday evening and jumped into the next dance. It was an unusually complex [square dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_dance), and I got confused—in fact, so confused that my entire square ended up giving up halfway through.✻✻ This is at least a 1-in-10,000 event; I've never seen it happen again in over 20 years of regular dancing.

I was mortified. Not only was I doing a deeply uncool activity, I was _the least cool person there_, ruining it for all the slightly less uncool people with my incompetence and lameness.

Except that the other dancers didn’t seem to see it that way. Instead of resenting me or making fun of me, they said things like “Gosh, that one was really tricky!” and “Sorry, that’s really not what it’s usually like!” and “Please don’t give up, try a few more dances! Want to dance with me?”

I tried some more dances, I didn’t cause any more collapses, and by the end of the night I was overflowing with exhilaration and joy. I went back the next week, and the week after that. I went to the twelve-hour [Snow Ball](https://www.monadnockfolk.org/%E2%98%BC-%E2%98%BC-%E2%98%BC-peterborough-snow-ball-2026/) and then the weekend-long [Flurry Festival](https://www.flurryfestival.org/). I got to feel cool for the first time in my life when people started being excited to dance with me, and even cooler when some of them asked me on dates. It was a huge part of my life for a while, and although I’m now dancing at maybe a quarter of my peak intensity, it’s still one of my most joyful and treasured things I do.

This happens to about one in ten people I take contra dancing: something clicks and it becomes a big part of their life.

* * *

So what exactly _is_ contra dancing?

When people ask me this I often feel stuck, because describing the mechanics of a contra dance doesn’t really capture its essence or why it’s fun, but without understanding the mechanics, the essence doesn’t really make sense. So I’ll try both. But you have to promise to read through to the essence part, and not just bounce off if the mechanics sound silly.

Mechanically: you show up to the dance hall. You ask someone to dance, or they ask you. You join a long line (“set”) of other couples and take hands in a four-person group with one adjacent (“neighbor”) couple. A _caller_ stands at the top of the dance hall and does a “walk-through” of the dance you’re about to do, calling out a series of figures (“circle left three places,” “do-si-do your neighbor,” etc.) At the end, you’ve moved up or down the line, and you’re ready to do the same thing with some new neighbors coming at you.

Next to the caller is a live band. (They probably sound a bit like [this](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/1uAQAUheWc3NjVE6RFAkwt).) The pianist plays [four potatoes](https://www.fiddlehangout.com/archive/740) and the fiddler launches into a reel. The caller calls out the figures for the first few times through the dance, then drops out as the dancers get into flow.

* * *

To me, the _essence_ of contra dancing is about flow, joy, and community.

You can’t really tell from watching, but a lot of what makes for good contra dancing is learning how to trade momentum back and forth (“share weight”) with other dancers so that each figure flows seamlessly into the next one. When you get this right, it feels amazing. Subtle changes in how your partner holds your hand cue you into the next figure. The dance becomes effortless. You can turn your brain off and just do what the giant dancing super-organism invites you to do.

It’s also physically joyful. The core figure of contra dancing is called the _swing_, in which you and your partner take a waltz-like hold and pivot around a shared center. Done right, it feels like a cross between flying and a world-class hug. Even outside of the swing, the shared weight, smooth momentum, and twirling “flourishes” will make you feel weightless, graceful and deeply connected to the music and the other dancers.

Lots of kinds of dance feel flowy and exhilarating when done well. What makes contra different is how easy it is to get there. Most forms of dance require weeks of lessons before they get fun. In contra, all you need to do is show up for one beginner lesson half an hour before the dance starts.†† In fact, you can probably get away without even that! Of course, there’s plenty to learn after that—improving your swing technique, learning how to share weight, and where to put flourishes for the best joy and flow—but all of that is for getting you from a 8/10 to an 11/10 on the fun scale; getting to an 8 is pretty straightforward.

So contra makes it easy to have a baseline level of fun dancing with anyone. And because it’s a group dance, you end up feeling connected to your entire set, not just your partner. I think those two things are behind my other favorite part of contra dancing, which is how friendly and welcoming the community is.

Many kinds of dance are a bit snooty—the experienced dancers look down on newbies and try to avoid dancing with them. By contrast, my experience at my first dance, where I caused my square to crash and burn and was met with apologies and “I hope you’ll give it another shot,” is typical for contra. A lot of contras explicitly ask experienced dancers to dance with newcomers. It’s just hard to be snooty when you’re having so much fun! You want to share it with everyone.‡‡ Another benefit of this is that contra is much more intergenerational than most forms of dance. I have dance friends in their twenties and seventies!

As draft reader Chloe put it: “I think in many ways, your story of your first dance is the heart of it—you can’t do it wrong. Or even if you do, it somehow leads to more love.”

* * *

In my opinion, this makes contra dancing _one of the best entry points to dance_:

-   Each dance is walked through, so you don’t have to worry about deciding what to do.
-   You can get to an 8/10 fun level, where practice becomes rewarding instead of a chore, really quickly.
-   The community is incredibly welcoming, which makes it a lot less stressful to be a beginner.

While it’s structured enough to be beginner-friendly, there’s also enough room for improvisation and technique to give it a lot of depth. This makes it a great on-ramp to other dances and movement practices. When I started contra dancing I had a lot of fear and blocks about improvised dancing (for example, I didn’t feel comfortable dancing at standard American high school/wedding style dances), but recently I tried out [fusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fusion_dance) and [contact improv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_improvisation) and had fun at both. It’s pretty common for people to use contra as a gateway into more difficult forms of dance.

Most importantly, though, it’s just incredibly joyful on its own! It’s the only activity I know of where I’ve had multiple friends mention that every time they go, their cheeks hurt from smiling so much.

* * *

Beyond just fun, multiple draft readers independently commented that contra dance is a precise opposite of many things that bother them about… the rest of life:

-   Much of my life happens on a screen in motionless silence; contra is as three-dimensional, movement- and sound-filled as you can get.

-   The default mood of a lot of spaces is a sort of ironic detachment.§§ At least for me, when I overuse ironic detachment it has subtle but far-reaching effects on how I see and engage with the world. It's an easy way to amuse people, but ends up masking or numbing out a lot of more "real" responses. I've found it really interesting and a bit psychoactive to play what I call "the ironic detachment game," in which I am banned from responding to anything in an ironically detached way. The default mood of contra dancing is earnest joy.

-   In everyday life it’s easy to end up atomized and isolated. At a contra, everyone does the same steps and makes the dance together; it’s deeply communal.

-   In a lot of social contexts (especially in San Francisco), I often feel like I’m being “sized up” for how interesting or important I am. At most contras, people are just happy I’m there.

-   Many things I do (work, choral music, blogging…) reward excellence above almost everything. Contra welcomes imperfection: we’d rather have an amateur live band than recorded professionals, and don’t believe in more than half an hour of organized teaching.

This makes it a grounding and nourishing antitode to a lot of everyday stressors. When I was so drawn to it in middle school, I don’t think it was just about the fun: on some deep level it was very good for me.

* * *

If I’ve gotten you excited enough to try contra, here’s what you should do:

-   Go to [try contra dot com](https://www.trycontra.com/) to find dances near you. If there are multiple dances, I recommend trying to figure out which one is the largest (larger dances tend to have more energy and better vibes), although that might be tricky to figure out from the websites.
-   Make a note of where and when the dance is, including the beginner lesson. Remember to show up beforehand for that!
-   Dress code wise: contra dancing is generally light cardio; wear something you are happy to be active in.‖‖ I personally sweat a lot and usually bring multiple shirts to change into, although most other dancers I know don't need to do this.
    -   Most people dress pretty casually; many women (and some men!) wear skirts, especially skirts that do fun things when you twirl.
    -   Look at the dance site to see if they say anything about footwear; some dances request that you wear soft-soled shoes with clean soles to avoid damaging floors.
    -   Bring a water bottle! There are fountains at most dances but it helps to be able to hydrate more quickly since the breaks between dances are short.
-   If you go to a dance and like it, the easiest way to go from 8/10 fun to 9.5/10 is to find someone with a really good swing and ask them how you can improve your technique. (If you’re not sure, look for someone who’s fast and smooth.) They will be tickled that you asked!¶¶ Teaching a swing is a separate skill from just having a good swing, so you may want to ask a few different people.
-   If you have a friend who contra dances, try going with them! They will be thrilled to bring you and it’ll be even more fun for both of you.✻✻✻✻ I recently started a mission to get my friends into contra dancing (hence also this post)! It's going \_great\_ and it's made it way more fun for me!

Have fun! I hope you smile so much your cheeks hurt.
