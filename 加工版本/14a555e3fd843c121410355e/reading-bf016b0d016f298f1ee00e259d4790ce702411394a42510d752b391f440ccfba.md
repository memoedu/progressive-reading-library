# Redesign

## 正文

我最近决定，自己应该克服在 web design 方面的 [learned helplessness](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_helplessness)。我觉得自己的 personal website 是个不错的试验品，因为我几乎不可能[把它弄得更难看](https://www.benkuhn.net/redesign/old_bknet.png)，而且它确实也有那么一点实际的 use case。[1](https://www.benkuhn.net/redesign/#fn:1) 按我室友的说法，秘诀就是“你就是得着迷才行”，所以这周我花了几段时间埋头折腾（这对我来说不算太难），这就是结果。

我决定自己 write 这个 site，部分是因为这会是一次学习的经历，但主要只是因为我想完全 control 它，而且这样比摸清如何为现有某个完整的“solutions”进行 theming 更快。[Django](https://www.djangoproject.com/) 这个 web framework 让这件事变得相当容易。我想整个东西还不到 200 lines，包括一个 RSS feed 之类的东西。这次 rewrite 大概花了我 10 小时，其中可能有 5-7 小时都在摆弄 CSS。[2](https://www.benkuhn.net/redesign/#fn:2)

其实我对它挺满意的。我学到了很多，也有相当把握觉得自己让它变好了，虽然这很难判断。不过，欢迎任何反馈！

## 原文

I recently decided I should get over my [learned helplessness](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_helplessness) at web design. I thought my personal website was a good guinea pig, since I had about zero chance of [making it look worse](https://www.benkuhn.net/redesign/old_bknet.png) and it does have a little bit of an actual use case.[1](https://www.benkuhn.net/redesign/#fn:1) According to my roommate, the secret is “you just have to be obsessive”, so I spent a few chunks of this week obsessing (not too difficult for me) and this is the result.

I decided to write the site myself, partially because it would be a learning experience but mostly just because I wanted complete control over it and this was faster than learning all the ins and outs of theming one of the complete “solutions” out there. The [Django](https://www.djangoproject.com/) web framework made it pretty easy. I think the whole thing is under 200 lines, including an RSS feed and stuff. The rewrite took me maybe 10 hours, of which maybe 5-7 were fiddling with CSS.[2](https://www.benkuhn.net/redesign/#fn:2)

I’m actually pretty happy with it. I learned a lot and I’m reasonably sure I made an improvement, although it’s hard to tell. I welcome any feedback though!
