# It's not utilitarian to live in a cardboard box

---

## 正文

想要 maximize 你给 charity 的 donation，却担心这需要你放弃许多自己喜欢的好东西？有 justified 理由的 purchases 比你想象的更多。这是因为 attention 是一种比 money 更 scarcer 的 resource。每当我不得不为 money 操心时，这都会占用我那很小的 working memory 中的 space，而这些 space 本可以用于有用且有趣的想法。我认为，对许多人来说，只需达到一个相当低的 point，把 working memory 中 marginal 的部分用于设法 increase 你的 income（或者，如果你不是在 earning to give，就用于你所 optimize 的 equivalent 事物），就会比 reducing expenditures 更 productive。

这也呼应了例如 [Ramit Sethi 的观点](http://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/blog/save-on-coffee/)：比起为了每天不买一杯拿铁而耗尽自己的 attention，increase earnings 更容易。显然，这并不意味着你永远不该尝试 cut costs；例如，从一开始就不喝拿铁，所耗费的精力要少得多，而某些 spending cuts，比如选择更 cheaper 的房子，能带来很大的好处。但一般来说，仅仅因为 cost cuts 更 [available](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Availability_heuristic)，并不意味着它们就是你能做的最好的事。

## 原文

Trying to maximize your donation to charity, but worried that this requires you to give up lots of nice things for yourself? There are more purchases than you think that are justified. This is because attention is a scarcer resource than money. Any time that I have to worry about money, it takes up space in my very small working memory that could be used for useful and interesting thoughts. I think that for many people there’s a fairly low point at which it’s more productive to spend your marginal bits of working memory on trying to increase your income (or equivalent thing that you optimize if you’re not earning to give) rather than reducing expenditures.

This mirrors e.g. [Ramit Sethi’s point](http://www.iwillteachyoutoberich.com/blog/save-on-coffee/) that it’s easier to increase earnings than to suck up all of your attention trying not to buy a latte every day. Obviously, this doesn’t mean that you should never try to cut costs; for instance, it takes a lot less energy never to start drinking lattes, and some types of spending cuts like a cheaper house are big wins. But in general, just because cost cuts are more [available](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Availability_heuristic) doesn’t mean they’re the best thing you can do.
