# 这……是什么

---

## 正文

在最近一次 retreat 中，我坐在 cushion 上，面朝一面墙；我的 meditation partner 坐在我身旁、我的 visual field 之外，开始给我读 Ken Wilber 的 pointing-out instructions——《[From You to Infinity](https://www.scribd.com/document/478871846/Advaita-Ken-Wilber-From-you-to-Infinity-in-Three-pages-pdf)》。这篇文字读起来相当 trippy。接下来发生的事十分耐人寻味，而且很可能是我迄今为止人生中最重要的一次 experience，所以我想在这里尽可能详细地记下自己还能记得的细节，免得忘记当时发生的事。

首先，我记得自己的 breathing rate 开始 increase，出现了一些 anxiety 的感觉：“有什么坏事就要发生了。”我的 mind 是 empty 的（我的 baseline 通常没有 verbal 或 image-based thoughts，除非我需要它们，所以这很正常）。我没有试着 process 那些不断 rising 的 anxiety，只是任它待在那里；我既兴奋又担心，但感觉会有什么有意思的事发生，于是我就这样与它 sat with it。

某个时刻，有什么突然翻转了。我不记得前后那几秒钟的确切情形，但我记得自己在哭，而且非常 distressed。“我的”嘴在动，说出下面这些话（加上引号，是因为我清楚记得自己说的就是这些原话）

-   “我不 understand 我不 understand 我不 understand”
-   “如此 beautiful”
-   “在所有 directions”
-   “Awareness 在 up and down、left and right、front and back”
-   “这一切都是我”
-   “一直都是这样”
-   “一切都是由 love 构成的”

接着，我指着一盏灯（是啊，用普通语言确实有点难以描述这件事），含着眼泪说：“那也是我。”随后又对我的 meditation partner 说：“我 love 这盏灯，就像我 love 你一样。”第一次望进她的眼睛时（别忘了，她之前坐在我身旁），我感觉自己在和“自己”说话，不是指作为“José”的自己，而是有某个东西在和它自己说话，而那个东西同时 manifests 为 José 和我的 meditation partner。

随后有人握住我的手，让我做几次 deep breaths，平静下来。然后“我说”：“请继续。”她便继续读那篇文字。后来我站起身，虽然仍然有些 distressed，但还是和另一个人说了话，而那也感觉像是在和自己说话。

重要的是，我的 perceptions 并没有被 altered：没有像 acid trip 中那样 flashy 或 fractal 的 colors，没有奇怪的声音，也没有 visual 或 sound hallucinations。只有这样一种延续至今的 felt sense：一切就是这样，而这正是我从十几岁起就在寻找的东西（关于 consciousness 如何 works 的答案；这个 topic 曾让我如此着迷，以至于我决定把它搁置一旁，因为试着 grasp 它似乎太令人沮丧了）。如果非要用 visual terms 来表达那种 felt sense，我会想到一种 vivid luminosity 的感觉，但那并不是我眼睛看到的东西，我的眼睛当时在正常地看东西。虽然我 know“就是这样”，但我现在 experiencing 它的 intensity，并没有那一刻那么强。那个 altered state 只持续了几分钟。

奇怪的是，当时还有一种 knowing：“一直都是这样。”那感觉像一个巨大的 cosmic joke，也就是说，我原本期待的是某种烟花般绚烂的重大时刻，就像人们参加 ayahuasca ceremonies 后所描述的那样，结果却不是；它有一种异想天开、诙谐的意味，仿佛那个东西一直就在我眼前，只是我从来不让自己去看。

换一种说法，就是 universe 在取笑我，同时也乐在其中，而我从始至终就是那个同一个 universe。

我感觉那些话是自己有意说出来的吗？某种意义上是，某种意义上又不是。我有一种 knowing，知道自己随时都可以从那个 state 中出来，让它停止，而且隐约觉得这会在适当的时候发生。这就像我现在也能开始蹦蹦跳跳，但同时又觉得“气氛不对，所以我不想这么做，也就不会这么做”。换一种说法，就是我想要的只有此时此地的 present moment，changing 任何东西都显得很傻，我为什么要那样做呢。

当时存在一些 terror，但我这个人平时就总说：“我想一直盯着事物的 truth，直到我的 retinas 都烧坏。”所以，这种想要 know 到底发生了什么的深切渴望，让我继续了下去。

我的 conceptual mind 凝视着事物的 true nature 这轮明亮的太阳，感受到了一些 emotional pain，就像一只拳头紧攥着玻璃碎片，伤害着自己。最终，我觉得自己多少放下了一个始终驱动着我的人生的核心承诺：To know and understand everything。我清楚记得，这是我对“人生有什么意义”的回答，它让我觉得比“happiness”或“meaning”更贴切。这个博客的标语“To estimate, compare, distinguish, discuss, and trace to its principal sources everything”（原本出自 Tolkien 的文字）就与此直接相关。让我开始 meditation journey 的那个感受——不必时时刻刻都在 learning，我可以只是 sit——终于得到了一些 completion：一方面是我不会 know everything 所带来的深切 heartbreak，另一方面则是如今 knowing 自己 know 自己并不 know 的那种奇趣。

我和其他一些有过类似 experiences 的人聊过这件事，而情况并不总是如此。伴随这次 experience 而来的强烈 distress，源于我把 knowing and understanding everything 变成了自己 identity 的一部分。我曾在一个又一个 field 中向自己证明，只要是我想 learn 的东西，我就能 learn：我当时想，没有任何东西能逃出我的 intellect。如果你也是这样的人，这会感觉像是被 universe 狠狠扇了一巴掌……而且还想再来，因为在内心深处，你真正想要的就是 _see_ 这个东西。反过来，如果一个人更能接受 not knowing，那么这可能仍然很有意义，却不会令人 distressing。

## 这……是什么

我不是第一个有过这种 experience 的人。我喜欢把 experiences 联系起来，所以接下来就试着弄清这究竟是什么。它显然不是 Buddhist Theravada tradition 中描述的 cessation/nirodha，因为当时并没有 nothing 或 emptiness 的感觉（我从未有过 cessation）。

在寻找一个合适的 term 之后，我看到的那个东西，就是 Tibetan Buddhists 所说的 _[rigpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigpa)_，或者 ground of being。而整个 experience 则符合 Zen 中 _[kensho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kensh%C5%8D)_ 这个名称。它也是 Roger Thisdell 所说的“[Big Mind](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HWkZI0W6q5o)”nonduality（与 Empty 或 Centerless 相对）。

我曾 experienced 过 jhanas，那感觉相当 pleasant，而且是发生在一个 Self 身上的；相比之下，这次 experience 完全属于另一个 category。与它相比，jhanas 感觉只像一道开胃菜。

与此同时，我感觉自己只是看到了某种更深之物的一角。我并没有 enlightened，但我已经看到了远处那道光的一点点。

在[之前的一次](https://nintil.com/jhanas) retreat 中，我望着大海时有过一次 experience，带着类似的 qualities：luminosity、“一直都是这样”、“beautiful”。在那个 nondual moment 发生前几天，我也有过类似的 experience，当时我望着一盏灯，为它如此 beautiful 而惊叹落泪。但那并不 distressful，那里仍然有一种 sense of self 在 experiencing 它。

在那次望海的 experience 中，我没有“就是这个”的感觉，更多是一种轻松的“哇，是啊，到处都有这么多 beauty，而我默认一直在 filtering it out，知道这一点真好”。相比之下，这次 experience 感觉更 existential，带着“对，就是这个”的 felt sense，而且还不止于此。

不过，现在回过头看，或许可以恰当地说，存在着“某个东西”，而人在不同的 meditative experiences 中，可以看到它的若干 facets：beauty、love、与它的 unity，等等。后面这次 experience 感觉更 complete，同时有 beauty 和 love 这两个 components。

说“一切都如此 beautiful”还算说得通，但“一切都是由 love 构成的”听起来就有点疯癫，因为 love 是一种 emotion，对吧？你也完全可以说“一切都如此 loving”，或者“universe loves 你，一切都没问题，而且永远都会如此”，这些也传达了同样的感觉。

Sasha Chapin 曾经写过“[Deep Okayness](https://sashachapin.substack.com/p/how-i-attained-persistent-self-love)”，我想他说的就是这个。其中一些感受仍然伴随着我，但并不完整。

我在这次 experience 将近一周之后写下这些，而我仍然能感觉到一点；类似于 jhana retreat 之后的 afterglow，我感觉自己更加置身于这个充满惊奇的世界之中，好事自然发生，我不再无谓地抗拒事物本来的样子。我内心的一部分希望这一切一直如此，另一部分则接受它可能不会一直这样。不知道这会持续多久！

## 走到这里花了多长时间

在这之前，我一直想知道要花多长时间。我对待 meditation，就像有人在玩某种宇宙彩票。有时你坐下来，perfect bliss 就会出现。有时你坐下来，会因为墙壁竟然如此 beautiful 而哭泣。有时你连续一个月每天坐一个小时，却什么也没有发生。

许多关于 meditation 的记述往往是 atemporal 的：它们描述 stages 或 states，却很少说人们花了多长时间才到达那里。偶尔能看到有人很早就突然 getting enlightened，也有人经历了数十年的 practice。不过，我不认为这就是 enlightenment（感觉像是偷偷瞥见了那个东西）。我觉得，把自己的时间线和“我做了什么”公开出来会有些用处，尽管我不认为这对每个人都会奏效。

我最初接触 meditation，是在 June 22, 2024 前后的某一天。那天我回到家，心想，嗯，我不想看书，也不想做任何事，于是就坐在那里盯着天花板。然后我想，好吧，meditation 应该是个不错的消磨时间的方式，于是买了一本 The Mind Illuminated，因为我曾在之前住过的一处 grouphouse 见到过这本书。随后我又读了 MCTB 和其他书，而且阅读显然远远多于 practice。2024 年，我 practiced 得很不规律，如果没记错，每次 sits 不超过 30 min，而且可能每 4 天才有 1 天，甚至更少。到了 2025 年，我最终参加了三次 retreats：

-   与 [Tucker Peck](https://meditatewithtucker.com/group-retreats/) 一起进行的三天 retreat（对于整天 sitting 所 arises 的 pain，我 Developed 了一些 equanimity，也说服了自己，几天什么都不做也可以感觉很好）
-   一次 7 天的 jhana retreat（Jhourney）（见[这里](https://nintil.com/jhanas)）。那篇文章的第一段，与我在这篇文章中描述的有相似的 quality，但当时仍然有一种 separate observer 的感觉。
-   在 Diamond Mountain 进行的 9 天 retreat（同样与 Tucker 和 [Upali](https://www.upalimeditation.com/) 一起）（不像 jhourney 中发生的那些那么 flashy，而是更加 still、surrendered；我去时以为自己会做 jhana practice，但后来更倾向于做 shikantaza，并随着 retreat 的进行，逐渐加入更多 structure）。回头看，Upali 当时可能埋下了一颗种子，因为我记得我们谈过我所能 understand 的 limits；我记得，当时他温和的追问让我有些恼火，而这后来促使我选择了他作为 teacher。

我还在 Alembic 与 Michael Taft 一起做过大约 3 次线下 sits，并在家跟着[这个](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exNayPPi8VY&list=PL590ElXNAb01rapZdDZVaXxlDiw8FVrEo)播放列表做了 30 天的 Michael Taft sits，不过当时没起什么作用。

最后，在 February 2026，我又参加了一次 7 天的 retreat，也就是这次 experience 发生的地方：[Sleepawake](https://sleepawake.camp/)。虽然它严格来说并不是 meditation retreat（也就是说，你可以与别人交谈），但我还是做了一些 meditation。

所以，是从 June 22, 2024 到 February 10, 2026；也就是大约 1 年零 7 个月。

## 原文

At a recent retreat, I found myself sitting on a cushion facing a wall while my meditation partner sat to my side, outside of my visual field and started reading to me "[From You to Infinity](https://www.scribd.com/document/478871846/Advaita-Ken-Wilber-From-you-to-Infinity-in-Three-pages-pdf)", pointing-out instructions from Ken Wilber. The text is quite trippy to read. What happened next was quite intriguing and it was probably the most significant experience of my life thus far so I wanted to write down as much detail as I can remember here so I never forget what happened.

First, I remember my breathing rate started to increase, there was some sense of anxiety "Something bad is about to happen". My mind was empty (I generally have no verbal or image-based thoughts as baseline unless I need them so this is normal). I didn't try to process the rising anxiety, I just left it there; I was excited and worried, but there was a sense that something interesting would happen so I sat with it.

At some point something flipped. I don't remember exactly the seconds before or after but I remember I was crying and very distressed. "My" mouth was moving, emitting words like the below (Quoted because I remember sharply that I said these exact things)

-   "I don't understand I don't understand I don't understand"
-   "So beautiful"
-   "In all directions"
-   "Awareness up and down, left and right, front and back"
-   "It's all me"
-   "It's always been like this"
-   "Everything is made of love"

Then I pointed at a lamp (yeah normal language kind of struggles at describing this) and said, among tears "That's also me" followed by "I love this lamp as I love you" to my meditation partner. Looking into her eyes for the first time (remember, she was sitting by my side) I had the sense that I was talking to "myself", not myself as in "José" but rather that there's a thing that's talking to itself, and that thing manifests as both José and my meditation partner.

Someone then held my hand and asked me to take some deep breaths to calm down. Then "I said" "Please continue", and she kept reading the text. Eventually I stood up and while still somewhat distressed I talked to one other person and it also felt like it was talking to myself.

Importantly, my perceptions were not altered: there were no flashy or fractal colors like in an acid trip, no weird sounds, no visual or sound hallucinations. There was just this felt sense that continues to this day that it's all like this and that this is what I had been looking for since I was a teenager (an answer to how consciousness works, a topic that has obsessed me so much I decided to put it away as it seemed too frustrating to try to grasp). If I had to put that felt sense in visual terms, a sense of vivid luminosity comes to mind but that's not what my eyes saw, my eyes were seeing normally. Though I know that "It is like this", I am not experiencing it with the same intensity as I did in that moment. The altered state lasted a few minutes only.

Weirdly, there was also the knowing that "it's always been like this", and that felt like a gigantic cosmic joke, ie I was expecting some flashy fireworks big moment like what people describe when they go on ayahuasca ceremonies but instead no, there was a whimsical funny aspect to it, like the thing has been in front of my face all along and I just didn't let myself look.

Another way to put it is that the universe was making fun of me while simultaneously enjoying it, all the while I was that same universe.

Did I feel like I was deliberately saying those things? In a way yes and in a way no. I had the knowing that I could pop out of that state at any time and stop it and some sense that it would happen at the right time. It's the same sense that right now I could start jumping up and down but at the same time "it's not the vibe so I don't feel like it so I won't". Another way to put it is that all I wanted is the present moment right there, right then, changing anything seemed silly, why would I want that.

There was some terror present, but I'm someone that goes around life saying "I want to stare at the truth of things until my retinas burn out" so this deep desire to know what's going on kept me going.

There was some emotional pain in my conceptual mind staring at the bright sun that is the true nature of things, like a fist clenching around shards of glass, hurting itself. Eventually I felt like I somewhat gave up on a core commitment that had been driving me through life: To know and understand everything, something I consciously remember as my answer to "what's the point of life" that felt more right than "happiness" or "meaning". The tagline of this blog "To estimate, compare, distinguish, discuss, and trace to its principal sources everything" (originally something Tolkien wrote) is directly connected to that. What started my meditation journey (the sense that it's ok to not be learning all the time, I can just sit) found some completion: the deep heartbreak that I won't know everything and the whimsy of now knowing that I know that I don't know.

I talked to some other people about this that have had similar experiences and it's not always like this. The incredible distress that came with it was a product of me having made knowing and understanding everything part of my identity. Field after field I had showed to myself I can learn whatever it is that I want to learn: nothing could ever escape my intellect, I thought. If you are that kind of person, this will feel like getting smacked by the universe... and wanting more of it, because deep down what you really wanted was to _see_ this. If on the other hand someone is more okay with not knowing, then it may still be meaningful but not distressing.

## What... is this

I'm not the first person to have had an experience like this. I like linking and connecting experiences, so I then was trying to find what this was. It was clearly not the cessation/nirodha described in the Buddhist Theravada tradition as there was no sense of nothing or emptiness present (I've never had a cessation).

After looking for a term that fits, the thing that I saw is what Tibetan Buddhists call _[rigpa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigpa)_ or the ground of being. And the whole experience fits the name _[kensho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kensh%C5%8D)_ from Zen. It is also what Roger Thisdell calls "[Big Mind](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HWkZI0W6q5o)" nonduality (in contrast to Empty or Centerless).

Having experienced the jhanas, which felt fairly pleasant and happening to a Self, this experience was totally in a different category. The jhanas feel like an appetizer in comparison to this.

At the same time I got the sense that I just saw the tip of something deeper. I am not enlightened, but I've seen a tiny bit of that light that's far away.

In a [previous](https://nintil.com/jhanas) retreat I had an experience while looking at the ocean that had similar qualities: the luminosity, the "always been like this", the "beautiful". I had had a similar experience to that a few days prior to the nondual moment, looking at a lamp and crying in awe at how beautiful it is. But that wasn't distressful, there was still a sense of self there experiencing it.

During the ocean experience, I didn't have this sense that "this is it", was more of a cool "wow yeah there's so much beauty everywhere and I'm filtering it out by default, good to know". In contrast, this experience felt more existential, with a felt sense that "yep this is it" and there's more of it.

Though now looking back, it's probably apt to say that there's "a thing" and one can see a number of facets of it in different meditative experiences: the beauty, the love, the unity with it, etc. This latter experience felt more complete, having both the beauty and the love components.

To say "everything is so beautiful" makes some sense, but "everything is made of love" sounds kind of deranged because love is an emotion right? One could equally say "everything is so loving" or "the universe loves you, it's all ok and will always be", that also captures the same vibe.

Sasha Chapin once wrote about "[Deep Okayness](https://sashachapin.substack.com/p/how-i-attained-persistent-self-love)" and I think this is what he meant. Some of that is still with me but not fully.

I'm writing this almost a week after the experience and I still feel it a bit; similar to the afterglow of a jhana retreat, I feel more in the world, which is full of wonder, good things just happen, I'm not fighting the way things are unnecessarily. Part of me wants this to stay like this, part of me accepts that it may not be like that. I wonder how long it will!

## How long did it take to get here

Prior to this point I had wondered how long it would take. I relate to meditation like someone playing some kind of cosmic lottery. Sometimes you sit and perfect bliss appears. Sometimes you sit and you cry at how beautiful walls can be. Sometimes you sit for one hour every day for a month and absolutely nothing happens.

Many accounts of meditation tend to be atemporal: they describe stages, or states, but rarely how long did it take people to get there. There are mentions here and there of people getting enlightened suddenly very early on and people taking decades of practice. Though I don't think this is enlightenment (it feels like a sneak peek at the thing). I thought it'd be useful to make public my own timeline and "what I did", though I don't think this will work for everyone.

I first got into meditation one day around June 22, 2024. I got home one day and I thought huh I don't feel like reading a book or doing anything, so I just sat there staring at the ceiling. Then I thought well I guess a good way to spend time is meditation, so I bought a copy of The Mind Illuminated, as I had come across it at a prior grouphouse I was at. That was followed by reading MCTB and other books, and definitely doing way more reading than practice. I practiced very inconsistently in 2024, no more than 30 min sits at a time if I remember correctly, and maybe 1 out of 4 days or even less. In 2025 I ended up going to three retreats:

-   A three day retreat with [Tucker Peck](https://meditatewithtucker.com/group-retreats/) (Developed some equanimity towards the pain that arises with sitting all day, and convinced myself it can feel good to do nothing for a few days)
-   A 7 day jhana retreat (Jhourney) (See [here](https://nintil.com/jhanas)). The first paragraph of that essay has a similar quality to what I describe in the present post, but there was still a sense of a separate observer.
-   A 9 day retreat at Diamond Mountain (also with Tucker and [Upali](https://www.upalimeditation.com/)) (Not as flashy as what happened in jhourney, more still, surrendered, I went in assuming I'd do jhana practice but I was more inclined to do shikantaza instead, and to eventually add more structure as the retreat went on). In retrospect Upali probably planted some seed there as I remember we talked about the limits of how much I could understand; I remember back then being annoyed by his gentle questioning, which then made me choose him as a teacher.

I have also done like 3 in person sits with Michael Taft at the Alembic, and 30 days of Michael Taft sits from [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=exNayPPi8VY&list=PL590ElXNAb01rapZdDZVaXxlDiw8FVrEo) playlist at home, which didn't do anything at the time.

Lastly in February 2026 I did one more 7 day retreat where the experience itself happened, [Sleepawake](https://sleepawake.camp/). Though it wasn't a meditation retreat as such (ie you could talk to people), I did some amount of meditation.

So from June 22, 2024 to February 10, 2026; or 1 year and 7 months or so.
