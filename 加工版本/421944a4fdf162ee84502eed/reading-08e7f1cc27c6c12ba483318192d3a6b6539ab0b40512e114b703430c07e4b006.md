# Frugality for EA-influenced spending patterns

---

## 正文

你可以通过多得令人惊讶的方式，实际获得长期、低投入且幅度相当大的日常 spending discounts——尤其是当你具有典型 EAer 的 spending habits 时（即主要购买必需品，较少外出就餐或娱乐，而且 credit 良好）。以下是其中一些：

-   [US Bank Cash+ card](https://cashplus.usbank.com/?redirect=cashplus) 允许你选择 categories，获得 5% 的折扣（每个 quarter 最高适用于 $2,000）。值得注意的是，这包括 _charitable donations_。虽然与通过 check/debit/direct transfer 进行 donating 相比，credit card processing fees 会消耗其中一部分优惠，但你仍然会获得相当可观的净收益。（Paypal 向 nonprofits 收取 2.2% 的费用。）

-   要在 Amazon 获得实际 5% 的折扣，可以使用 Amazon for Effective Charities browser addons（[使用说明](http://effective-altruism.wikia.com/wiki/Shop_for_Charity)）。你每次在 Amazon 购物的金额中，都会有 5% 转给 SCI。

-   [Sallie Mae Mastercard](https://www.salliemae.com/credit-cards/sallie-mae-card/) 为你在 groceries、gas 和 bookstores 的消费提供 5% cash back，其中 bookstores 包括 Amazon。（cash-back amount 的 monthly caps 分别为 $250、250 和 750。）它可与前一项优惠 Stacks，从而在 Amazon 获得实际 10% 的 discount。

-   [PenFed](https://www.penfed.org/) 提供一张 card，你在旅行（包括航班）上每消费一美元，就能获得 5 个“points”，不过这些“points”的价值似乎更接近 0.8 cents。（Disclaimer：我还没用过这张卡；他们把我的申请弄丢了，或者出了类似的问题。）

-   对于其他 expenses，你能获得的最佳 discount 似乎来自 [Citi Double Cash](https://www.citi.com/credit-cards/credit-card-details/detail.do?ID=citi-double-cash-credit-card) card，它对所有消费都提供 2% 的返还。

-   有些 cards 会针对不同 categories 提供 rotating 5% cash back，这些 categories 有时包括外出就餐，也可能包括其他 expenses。我通常不用这些卡，因为它们需要我过多关注 categories，而且我担心它们会诱使我花掉原本不会花的钱。

当然，这些优惠都没有 covered 的主要 expense 是我的 rent，但 credit cards 并不那么愿意在这方面给人们提供 discounts……另一方面，我确信还有其他可能的 discounts 被我漏掉了。

## 原文

There are a surprising number of ways that you can effectively get permanent, low-effort, fairly large discounts on everyday spending—especially if you have the spending habits of a typical EAer (i.e., mostly necessities, relatively little eating out/entertainment, and strong credit). Here are some:

-   The [US Bank Cash+ card](https://cashplus.usbank.com/?redirect=cashplus) lets you pick categories to get 5% off of (up to $2,000 per quarter). This notably includes _charitable donations_. Although credit card processing fees will eat some of this compared to donating via check/debit/direct transfer, you’ll still come out pretty far ahead. (Paypal charges 2.2% to nonprofits.)

-   For an effective 5% off of Amazon, use the Amazon for Effective Charities browser addons ([instructions](http://effective-altruism.wikia.com/wiki/Shop_for_Charity)). 5% of each of your Amazon purchases will go to SCI.

-   The [Sallie Mae Mastercard](https://www.salliemae.com/credit-cards/sallie-mae-card/) gives you 5% cash back on groceries, gas and bookstores, where bookstores include Amazon. (There are monthly caps on the cash-back amount of $250, 250 and 750 respectively.) Stacks with the previous for an effective 10% discount on Amazon.

-   [PenFed](https://www.penfed.org/) offers a card that gives you 5 “points” per dollar spent on travel (including flights), although the “points” seem to be worth more like 0.8 cents. (Disclaimer: haven’t used this one yet; they lost my application or something.)

-   For other expenses the best discount you can get appears to be the [Citi Double Cash](https://www.citi.com/credit-cards/credit-card-details/detail.do?ID=citi-double-cash-credit-card) card, which gives 2% back across the board.

-   There are some cards that offer rotating 5% cash back on different categories, which sometimes include eating out and potentially other expenses. I don’t usually use these because they require me to pay too much attention to the categories and I’m worried about them inducing me to spend money I otherwise wouldn’t.

My main expense that isn’t covered by any of these is, of course, rent, but credit cards are not so eager to give people discounts on that… on the other hand, I’m sure there are other potential discounts I’m missing.
