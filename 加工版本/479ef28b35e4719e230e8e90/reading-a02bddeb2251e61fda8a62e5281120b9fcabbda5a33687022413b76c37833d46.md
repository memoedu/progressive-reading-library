# How to have a productive argument

---

## 正文

Slate Star Codex 的 Scott Alexander 写了一篇[很棒的文章，谈一个糟糕的 argument](http://slatestarcodex.com/2013/05/30/fetal-attraction-abortion-and-the-principle-of-charity/)：

> 最近，Alas, A Blog 写了一篇文章，说 Democrats 并不真正关心 helping the poor，他们只关心 increasing government’s ability to take your money。我们可以 prove 这一点，因为 Republicans 给 charity 的钱 consistently 比 Democrats 多——也因为，如果 Democrats 真的关心 the poor，他们就会停止 supporting 那种 discourages lifting yourself out of poverty 的 welfare system。唯一的 explanation 就是，这个国家的一亿多 Democrats 全都是 moral mutants，把 increased labyrinthine bureaucracy 当作 terminal moral value。
> 
> 不，等等，抱歉！完全不是这回事。他们说的是，civil rights activists 并不真正想要 prevent 针对 Muslims 的 hate crimes，他们只关心 supporting terrorism。我们可以 prove 这一点，因为对于数以万计的 Muslims 在国外的 wars 中被 killed 和 maimed，他们似乎觉得完全可以接受，也不推动对那些 wars 进行任何 intervention——还因为他们拒绝 ban Muslim immigration to America，而这项 policy 既会 decrease 针对 Muslims 的 hate crimes，也会 decrease terrorism 的 chance。唯一的 explanation 就是，这个国家的一亿多 civil rights activists 全都是 moral mutants，把 increased terrorism 当作 terminal moral value。
> 
> 不，等等，再次抱歉！也不是这回事！他们说的是，pro-lifers 并不真正关心 fetuses，他们只是 support government coercion of women。我们可以 prove 这一点，因为他们拒绝 support contraception，而 contraception 会 decrease 对 fetus-murdering abortions 的 need——还因为，他们似乎觉得 rape 或 incest 情况下的 abortion 完全可以接受。唯一的 explanation 就是，这个国家的一亿多 pro-lifers 全都是 moral mutants，把 increased oppression of women 当作 terminal moral value。
> 
> 不，等等，还是不对！我真是彻底乱套了！他们说的是，atheists 并不真正 doubt God 的 existence，只是他们太 proud，不愿 worship 除自己以外的任何东西。我们可以 prove 这一点，因为 atheists 有时会在极其危急的情况下 pray for help，——而且……
> 
> 不，等等！原来还真是第三个！就是关于 pro-lifers 和 abortion 的那个。哎呀。不过，容我辩解一句：这些 essentially identical arguments，我实在很难分清。[1](https://www.benkuhn.net/argument/#fn:1)

你大概应该去[读读全文](http://slatestarcodex.com/2013/05/30/fetal-attraction-abortion-and-the-principle-of-charity/)，因为它写得很棒。不过，要是你没有时间，下面就是 Scott 对 _Alas, A Blog_ 的 argument 进行 takedown 时最精彩的部分：

> Alas 在说 pro-lifers 应该 support contraception 时，犯的恰恰就是 The Last Superstition 曾警告过的 error。Ze 注意到 Christians 做的事情并不 agree with modern moral philosophy，于是就 assuming Christians 不是 stupid 就是 evil，而没有想到，他们有一套 ze 从未听说过的、古怪的 moral philosophy。
> 
> 所以，与其替 pro-lifers 开脱，不如先把他们说得更糟一些。他们并不 hate women。他们并不 love oppression。情况比这糟糕得多。Pro-lifers 不是 consequentialists。

对，对，_对_。这才是对 [mind-killers](http://lesswrong.com/lw/gw/politics_is_the_mindkiller/) 进行 productive 思考的方式。

看到当你真的在乎另一方的人时，会发生什么了吗？看到当你把他们 model 为人，而不是——怎么说呢——被设计来把食物和水 transform 成 misogynistic arguments 的 machines 时，会发生什么了吗？结果就是，你开始_真正 understand 他们_，然后也许就能展开一场 _productive debate_，而不至于像往常那样，最后所有人都只顾着发疯似的胡乱争吵。（也许吧。最后这部分仍然很难。）

我总是看到这种事情发生，这让我非常难过。尤其是，这种事情竟然如此频繁地发生在那些持有完全 reasonable 的 opinions 的人身上。他们一直觉得自己在某件事上是 right 的（通常也确实有道理），可没过多久，他们就忘了：与自己 arguing 的那个人是一个真实的人，有真实的背景、经历和 emotions，而不是某种 abstract 的 The Other Side 的 idea，等着他们去 score points on。

_请不要让这种事发生在你身上。_ 跟我一起说：

> 我的 opponent 是一个真实的人。
> 
> 他们不是什么 abstract 的 arguments 的 set。
> 
> 他们和其他真实的人一样，是 thoughts、feelings、desires 和 beliefs 交织成的复杂整体。
> 
> 他们的存在不是供我去“score points on”，而是让我与他们一起 find the truth。
> 
> 我的 opponent 的 values 并不是 insane 的。
> 
> 如果它们看起来是这样，我会更努力地思考。
> 
> 然后，我会弄清楚究竟发生了什么，并 solve 那个 problem，而不是向朋友们大肆抱怨他们，以炫耀自己的 brilliance 和 correctness。

* * *

1.  澄清一下：我赞扬一篇 attacking 某篇 pro-choice 文章的文字，并不一定反映我自己的 views。糟糕的 argument 就是糟糕的 argument。 [↩︎](https://www.benkuhn.net/argument/#fnref:1)

## 原文

Scott Alexander of Slate Star Codex has an [awesome post about a terrible argument](http://slatestarcodex.com/2013/05/30/fetal-attraction-abortion-and-the-principle-of-charity/):

> Recently, Alas, A Blog wrote an article saying that Democrats don’t really care about helping the poor, they only care about increasing government’s ability to take your money. We can prove this, because Republicans consistently give more to charity than Democrats – and because if Democrats really cared about the poor they would stop supporting a welfare system that discourages lifting yourself out of poverty. The only explanation is that the hundred-million odd Democrats in this country are all moral mutants who hold increased labyrinthine bureaucracy as a terminal moral value.
> 
> No, wait, sorry! That wasn’t it at all. They were saying that civil rights activists don’t really want to prevent hate crimes against Muslims, they only care about supporting terrorism. We can prove this because they seem pretty okay with the tens of thousands of Muslims who are being killed and maimed in wars abroad that they don’t promote any intervention in – and because they refuse to ban Muslim immigration to America, a policy which would decrease hate crimes against Muslims but also decrease the chance of terrorism. The only explanation is that the hundred-million odd civil rights activists in this country are all moral mutants who hold increased terrorism as a terminal moral value.
> 
> No, wait, sorry again! That wasn’t it either! They were saying that pro-lifers don’t really care about fetuses, they just support government coercion of women. We can prove this because they refuse to support contraception, which would decrease the need for fetus-murdering abortions – and because they seem pretty okay with abortion in cases of rape or incest. The only explanation is that the hundred-million odd pro-lifers in this country are all moral mutants who hold increased oppression of women as a terminal moral value.
> 
> No, wait, still wrong! I’m totally breaking apart here! They were saying that atheists don’t really doubt the existence of God, but they are too proud to worship anything except themselves. We can prove this because atheists sometimes pray for help during extreme emergencies, — and…
> 
> No, wait! It turns out it was actually third one after all! The one with the pro-lifers and abortion. Oops. In my defense, I have trouble keeping essentially identical arguments separate from one another.[1](https://www.benkuhn.net/argument/#fn:1)

You should probably go [read the whole thing](http://slatestarcodex.com/2013/05/30/fetal-attraction-abortion-and-the-principle-of-charity/) because it’s great. But in case you don’t have time, here’s the highlight of Scott’s takedown of _Alas, A Blog_’s argument:

> In saying pro-lifers should support contraception, Alas is making exactly the error that The Last Superstition warned against. Ze’s noticing that Christians do things that don’t agree with modern moral philosophy, and so assuming Christians are either stupid or evil, instead of that they have a weird moral philosophy ze’s never heard of.
> 
> So instead of excusing pro-lifers, start by tarring them further. They don’t hate women. They don’t love oppression. It’s much worse than that. Pro-lifers are not consequentialists.

Yes, yes, _yes_. This is how you think productively about [mind-killers](http://lesswrong.com/lw/gw/politics_is_the_mindkiller/).

See what happens when you actually give a damn about the people on the other side? See what happens when you model them as people, instead of, I don’t know, machines designed to transform food and water into misogynistic arguments? What happens is that you start to _actually understand them_ and then maybe you can have a _productive debate_ that doesn’t end up with everyone flailing around madly like usual. (Maybe. The last part’s still hard.)

I see this happening all the time and it makes me really sad. Especially because of how frequently it happens to people who hold completely reasonable and opinions. They go around thinking (justifiably, usually) that they’re right about something, and then before long they’ve lost sight of the fact that the person they’re arguing with is a real person with a real background and experiences and emotions, not some abstract idea of The Other Side that they have to score points on.

_Please don’t let this happen to you._ Repeat after me:

> My opponent is a real person.
> 
> They are not some abstract set of arguments.
> 
> They are a complicated lump of thoughts and feelings and desires and beliefs, just like other real people.
> 
> They are not there to “score points on”, but to find the truth with.
> 
> My opponent’s values are not insane.
> 
> If it seems like they are, I will think harder.
> 
> Then I will figure out what is actually going on and solve that problem, instead of ranting about them to my friends to show off my brilliance and correctness.
