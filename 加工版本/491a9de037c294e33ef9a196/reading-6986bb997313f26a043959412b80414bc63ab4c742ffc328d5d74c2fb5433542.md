# A side note to the gold is money debate

---

## 正文

[gold 今天是 money 吗？,](http://daviddebedoya.wordpress.com/2013/08/31/is-gold-money-today/) 杰出的 economist、我的朋友 David de Bedoya 在他的博客上这样问道

我们知道 gold 是什么。它是 periodic table 中的第 79st 个 element。就是这样。大家对此都没有异议，因此我们的工作已经完成了一半。我们还知道，gold 具有适合成为 money 的良好 properties。然而，有潜力成为某种东西，并不意味着它实际上就是那种东西。

接下来：money 是什么？这里就有几种 definitions 了。

-   Common view：普通公民所使用的 money 的 concept。基本上，就是“你可以用来 exchange 其他 goods 的 tokens”。Gold 并不是这种东西，因为在世界上大多数地方，这些 tokens 都是 iredeemable 的纸片。
-   Chartalist view：“Money 始终是一种 chartal mean of payment。我们把那些 chartal means of payment 称为 money。因此，money 的 definition 就是‘chartal mean of payment’的 definition”（Knapp 的 definition，摘自 Juan Ramón Rallo 对 [money 的 conceptions 所作的精彩 analysis）。](http://juanramonrallo.com/2013/06/leccion-5-la-redefinicion-moderna-del-origen-y-de-las-funciones-del-dinero/) 当然，今天的 state money 是用纸张以及 gold 以外的 metals 制成的，因此这种 view 也会排除 gold 作为 money 的可能性。
-   Mengerian view：“Money 将倾向于是其 marginal utility 以最慢的 pace decreases 的 asset”。上述 good 将具有最 thinnest 的 bid-ask spread。以下是一些数据 (01-09-13)。
    -   Gold-USD:  1$  （price 的 0.071%）
    -   Gold-EUR: 0.76€ （price 的 0.072%）
    -   Gold-GBP 0.65 £   （price 的 0.072%）
    -   EUR-USD:  0.0005$ （price 的 0.038%）
    -   EUR-GBP: 0.001€  （price 的 0.12%）
    -   GBP-USD: 0.002$ （price 的 0.12%）
    -   USD-JPY: 0.15$  （price 的 0.15%）
    -   USD-Oil: 0.06 （price 的 0.08%）

从中无法得出明确的结论。Gold spreads 看起来与 EUR-USD 不相上下。我们可以相当准确地说，今天在一个 country 内部，money 往往是其 governmet fiat paper。但在世界范围内，最 liquid 的 asset，也就是 money，其候选者是 US dollar 或 gold。从这里无法作出绝对明确的判断。因此，这种 view 没有给我们一个答案。

-   Asset of last resort view：“Money 是在发生剧烈的 economic meltdown 时，wealth 将 flow to 的 asset”。gold 具有良好的 properties，其 History 可以追溯到数千年前，无疑是这种 view 下的最佳候选者。每一次 financial crisis 都让我们窥见这一点：wealth flows to gold。因此，从这个角度来看，gold 会是 money。

那么，gold 是 money 吗？按照 money 这个词的含义的 common view，我会回答不是。然而，gold 是那块 stable 的基岩（从 long term 来看），位于波涛起伏的海洋之下，而 national currencies 的船只就在这片海洋上沉浮。与其说 100 年后 US dollar 仍会像今天这样被使用，不如说 gold 仍会如此，后者更有把握。

## 原文

[Is gold money today?,](http://daviddebedoya.wordpress.com/2013/08/31/is-gold-money-today/) asks great economist and friend David de Bedoya at his blog

We know what gold is. The 79st element of the periodic table. That's it. Universally agreed, so we have half the work done. We additionally know that gold has nice properties to be money. However, having potential to be something doesn't actually make that into said something.

Next: What is money?. That's where you have a few definitions.

-   Common view: The concept of money that the average citizen employs. Basically, "tokens that you can exchange for other goods". Gold is not this, since those tokens are iredeemable pieces of paper in most of the world.
-   Chartalist view: "Money is always a chartal mean of payment. To those chartal means of payment we call them money. The definition of money is, so, that of "chartal mean of payment" (Knapp's definition, extracted from Juan Ramón Rallo's [fine analysis of the conceptions of money).](http://juanramonrallo.com/2013/06/leccion-5-la-redefinicion-moderna-del-origen-y-de-las-funciones-del-dinero/) Of course, state money today is made of paper and metals other than gold, so this view would rule out gold as money too.
-   Mengerian view: "Money will tend to be the asset whose marginal utility decreases at the slowest pace". Said  good will have the thinnest bid-ask spread. Here are some (01-09-13).
    -   Gold-USD:  1$  (0.071% of price)
    -   Gold-EUR: 0.76€ (0.072% of price)
    -   Gold-GBP 0.65 £   (0.072% of price)
    -   EUR-USD:  0.0005$ (0.038% of price)
    -   EUR-GBP: 0.001€  (0.12% of price)
    -   GBP-USD: 0.002$ (0.12% of price)
    -   USD-JPY: 0.15$  (0.15% of price)
    -   USD-Oil: 0.06 (0.08% of price)

Nothing clear can be extracted. Gold spreads seem to be on par with the EUR-USD. We could say with reasonable accuracy that today within a country, money tends to be its governmet fiat paper. But at a world scale, candidates for being the most liquid asset, that is, money are the US dollar or gold . Nothing can be said with absolute clarity from here.  So we don't get an answer from this view.

-   Asset of last resort view: "Money is the asset wealth will flow to in the event of a brutal economic meltdown". With good properties and a History going back millenia, gold is indoubtedly the best candidate for this view. We get a glimpse of this in every financial crisis: wealth flows to gold. So from this perspective, gold would be money.

So is gold money? I'd answer with a no to that, as per the common view of the meaning of the word money. However, gold is the stable bedrock (in the long term) that lies below the moving ocean in which the ships of national currencies rise and sink. It'd be safer to say that in 100 years gold will still be used as it is today than saying that the US dollar will be.
