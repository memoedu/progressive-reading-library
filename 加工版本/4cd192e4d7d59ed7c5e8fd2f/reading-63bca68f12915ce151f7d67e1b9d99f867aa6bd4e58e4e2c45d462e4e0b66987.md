# Your room can be as bright as the outdoors

---

## 正文

这是我从塞内加尔回来后在波士顿度过的第一个冬天。现在太阳大约在 4:15 落山，天黑后我有多难 focus，突然变得_极其_明显。

过去我隐约注意到过这一点，并通过把 sleep schedule 提前来应对（或者干脆处于 unproductive 的状态）。但现在我大约 6:30 就醒了，而且已经很难撑到晚上参加活动。所以最后，受到[一些](https://meaningness.com/metablog/sad-light-led-lux)[博客](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/zsG9yKcriht2doRhM/inadequacy-and-modesty)[文章](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/hC2NFsuf5anuGadFm/how-to-build-a-lumenator)的启发，我决定不再追逐 natural sunlight，转而自己造一份。

我买了一个极其 bright 的“corn cob”式 bulb。它 emits 的 light 相当于大约 40 个 incandescents，还会 produces 足够多的 waste heat，以至于需要一个 internal cooling fan 来 dissipate 它。我把它放在书桌旁、我的 peripheral vision 之内（直接看它有些不舒服）。根据我那准确性存疑的 iPhone light meter，它在我面前窗帘上的 reflection 比真正从室外照进来的 sunlight 还 brighter（达到 600 lux），而我室内的 overall light levels 从 50 升到了 400 lux（“晴天时的 sunrise 或 sunset”）。（[来源](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lux)）。

> _更新 2020-11-16：在家里，我最后把 corncob bulb 换成了从 Home Depot 买来的三个 [7-way splitters](https://amzn.to/2WvLZOx) 和 21 个 [100W equivalent 5000K Cree bulbs](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Cree-100-Watt-Equivalent-A19-Dimmable-Exceptional-Light-Quality-LED-Light-Bulb-Daylight-5000K-TA19-16050MDFH25-12DE26-1-11/311407015)。我发现 Cree bulbs 正在打折，所以价格差不多，light 更 diffused，而且它们的 color-rendering index 更高。评论中还有关于其他替代选择的讨论。_

效果非常大：从 3:30pm 到我关灯的那一刻，我变得明显更 productive。我不再一到外面天黑就强烈地想停止工作，而是能够按照夏天的正常时间继续工作，在晚饭前才停下来。我估计，这盏 lamp 每天为我争取了半小时到两小时，具体取决于当天有多阴。

为了试着 capture 这种 difference，我用一个 manual camera app，以相同的 exposure 和 white balance，拍下了书桌的前后照片。由于 dynamic range 太小，手机 camera 并不能真正 capture 全部效果，但希望这些照片多少能传达一点那种惊艳感：

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/after_hu7b50a8293bf7fd24a260dfad0562ee83_3033707_600x800_resize_q75_box.jpg)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/after.jpg "full size") [![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/before_hubd9bf75ddebb091873b32d87b4d8e400_3970010_600x800_resize_q75_box.jpg)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/before.jpg "full size")

自从我装上这个 bulb，所有来过我家的人都说，我们的客厅现在真让人心情愉快；有些人甚至还没注意到那盏灯，就已经这么说了。我的伴侣和几个朋友也在买自己的。

供参考，以下是我买的东西（注意，这个 bulb 今天正在打折）：

-   我在 Amazon 上能找到的 cheapest-per-lumen 的“corn bulb”，[1](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/#fn:1)30,000 lumens 售价 $100。注意，这款有一个 built-in cooling fan，运行起来相当吵（根据我那准确性存疑的 iPhone sound level meter，在 1m 处为 45db）。

-   [这些 E26 to E39 adapters](https://amzn.to/34JagCN)，能让这个 bulb 装进 normal sockets。

-   我不想折腾已有的 fixtures，所以买了[一个 rated for 250 watts 的 basic fixture](https://amzn.to/2OooCTG)。

注意事项：

-   这个 bulb 太重，所以 fixture 上的 clamp 完全不起作用——直接把它拆掉就好。

-   如果你不想靠 unplugging 来 turn the bulb off，可以考虑[像这样的 outlet switch](https://amzn.to/2Dzgaux)（我自己没试过）。

-   最初，我把 fixture 塞进 lamp 的一块 packing foam 里，使它 stabilized（见图），但今天我意识到，也可以把 metal guard 架在一个空的 2lb 酸奶盒里；这样更好，因为能给 power cord 留出穿行的空间。

-   如果你想把 bulb 装进已有的 fixture/socket，一定要检查 (a) 它是否 fit（它非常大），以及 (b) socket 是否 rated for 250w（很多并不是）。

* * *

为什么不是人人都这么做？！

那些博客文章全都是由被描述为患有“crippling” seasonal depression 的人撰写的，或是关于他们的。我从来没有 depressed 过，所以才过了这么久才去尝试（甚至才注意到 light 和 productivity 之间的 correlation）。

现在回头看，这很傻。“Seasonal affect disorder”这个说法，让它听起来像一种 discrete 的东西，要么有，要么没有，但大概每个人都注意到过，自己在阴天会更 lethargic。一个更好的 model 或许是：seasonal depression 只是人们 respond to light 的那条 curve 的 tail end：

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/sad_hu48e2e224e60752d8c8beef77f69c7b92_268324_1000x702_resize_q75_box.jpg)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/sad.jpg "full size")

Typical indoor light levels 在 [100-500 lux](https://www.engineeringtoolbox.com/light-level-rooms-d_708.html) 之间，_即使在 high end_，也只和户外 sunrise 一样 bright，比 [daylight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daylight) 大约 dimmer 100x。除了“我们一直是这么做的”以外，似乎没有理由认为，整天待在一个 dimmer 100x 的环境里_不会_很糟糕。Indoor darkness 似乎属于这样一类事情：我们不质疑它，只是因为它一直如此。

不过，直到最近，质疑这件事还多少有些纸上谈兵，因为购买足够多的 bulbs（以及为它们 power）来改善这种情况，实在太贵。我的 bulb 是一个 LED bulb，而在过去 50 年里，这种 bulb 的 cost per lumen 每年大约 falling 20%（[来源](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitz%27s_law)）。LEDs 的 efficiency 直到最近才超过 fluorescents，但它们的价格仍在迅速下降。10 年前，一个和我的一样 bright 的 bulb 可能要花 \$1,000 instead of $100。

但最近，让整个家充满与 full daylight 一样 bright 的 light，已经完全可行。阴影处的 Ambient daylight 大约是 10k lux，也就是每 square meter 10k lumens。对于一套 70 m2 的公寓，这就是 700k lumens。我买的 bulb 是 \$100 for 35k lumens, so $2k 就能为整套公寓买来 sunlight。（再过 10 年，如果 LEDs 没有 hit physical limits，就只要 $200！）

* * *

最后，回答几个关于 ultra-bright-lamp 的 FAQs：

-   **这与“light therapy lamps”有什么不同？** Light therapy lamps 是 _weak_ 的。只有把它们放在大约一英尺外、直接对准你的脸时，它们才能给你合理数量的 light。一个 high-power corn bulb 能让整个房间都变得明显 brighter，从而提供好得多的 daylight simulation。参见[“你需要更多 lumens。”](https://meaningness.com/metablog/sad-light-lumens)

-   **这样运行起来不会很贵吗？** 它每天 draws 大约 2 kWh（假设 usage 为 8h），按通常的费率计算，花费大约是 $0.30。这相当于一台现代冰箱一天的用量，或洗一批衣服的用量。各位，Power 很便宜！

-   **使用这么多 power，不会对地球不好吗？** Coal 的 carbon intensity 大约是 1 kg CO2e / kWh（[来源](http://shrinkthatfootprint.com/electricity-emissions-around-the-world)），所以一个 coal-powered lamp-day 会 produces 2 kg，大约相当于一个芝士汉堡的三分之一到二分之一（[来源](http://www.openthefuture.com/cheeseburger_CF.html)）。

-   **5000K 不是很“cold”的 light 吗？** “Cold” lights（高于 2700k，也就是 typical incandescent color temperature）的名声不好，但 5000K 实际上比太阳还不那么 cold（太阳大约是 5500-6500K），而太阳显然没有坏名声。我那个完全 non-validated 的 theory 是，人们不喜欢 cold light，是因为大多数 cold lights（过去主要是 fluorescents）的 [color rendering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_rendering_index) 比 incandescents 差得多：也就是说，它们只在 visible spectrum 的 discrete parts 中 emit light，这会 changes colors 的 relative appearance，让一切看起来都很奇怪。例如，比较一下 fluorescent 与 incandescent 的这两个 example spectra：

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/fluorspec_huf361eca36a7d1a29d7eafc3d048a6288_84026_600x366_resize_box_3.png)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/fluorspec.png "full size")

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/ledspec_huba6fcc090052d63bbbb178326696ff96_183417_600x430_resize_box_3.png)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/ledspec.png "full size")

左：fluorescent（[来源](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fluorescent_lighting_spectrum_peaks_labeled_with_colored_peaks_added.png)）；右：LED（[来源](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:White_LED.png)）

-   **这不会让人难以入睡吗？** 会的，这么 bright 的 bulbs 会 dramatically suppress 我的 sleepiness（这也正是部分目的！）。我每天工作结束就把它关掉，通常在 6pm，距离睡觉大约还有 3 小时。

-   **有什么缺点？** 当其他 current-hungry appliances 启动时，这盏 lamp 会 flickers，[2](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/#fn:2)它的 fan 很吵，外观难看，很难为它 set up 一个好看的 fixture，没有 on/off switch（我直接 unplug 它），而且不容易在整个空间里提供 even illumination。我正在着手 building 一个 DIY ultra-bright lighting setup，预计它在所有这些方面都会有效得多——敬请期待。

## 原文

This is my first winter in Boston after coming back from Senegal. The sun sets at around 4:15 now, and it’s suddenly become _extremely_ salient how hard it is for me to focus after dark.

I’d vaguely noticed this in the past, and dealt with it by shifting my sleep schedule earlier (or just being unproductive). But I’m waking up around 6:30 now and I already struggle to stay up for evening events. So finally, inspired by [some](https://meaningness.com/metablog/sad-light-led-lux) [blog](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/zsG9yKcriht2doRhM/inadequacy-and-modesty) [posts](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/hC2NFsuf5anuGadFm/how-to-build-a-lumenator), I decided to give up on chasing natural sunlight and make my own instead.

I bought an extremely bright “corn cob” style bulb. It emits as much light as about 40 incandescents, and produces enough waste heat that it needs an internal cooling fan to dissipate it. I put it next to my desk, in my peripheral vision (it’s somewhat uncomfortable to look at directly). According to my questionably-accurate iPhone light meter, its reflection off the curtains in front of me is brighter than the actual sunlight coming in from the outdoors (at 600 lux), and overall light levels in my interior went from 50 to 400 lux (“sunrise or sunset on a clear day”). ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lux)).

> _Update 2020-11-16: For my home, I ended up switching from the corncob bulb to three [7-way splitters](https://amzn.to/2WvLZOx) and 21 [100W equivalent 5000K Cree bulbs](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Cree-100-Watt-Equivalent-A19-Dimmable-Exceptional-Light-Quality-LED-Light-Bulb-Daylight-5000K-TA19-16050MDFH25-12DE26-1-11/311407015) from Home Depot. I found the Cree bulbs on sale so they were about the same price, the light was more diffused and they have a higher color-rendering index. There’s discussion of other alternatives in the comments._

The effect was huge: I became dramatically more productive between 3:30pm and whenever I turned off the light. Instead of having a strong urge to stop working whenever it got dark out, I was able to keep working my normal summer schedule, stopping just before dinner. I estimate the lamp bought me between half an hour and two hours a day, depending on how overcast it was.

To try to capture the difference, I used a manual camera app to take before and after photos of my desk with the same exposure and white balance. The phone camera doesn’t really capture the full effect since the dynamic range is so small, but hopefully it conveys a little bit of the amazingness:

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/after_hu7b50a8293bf7fd24a260dfad0562ee83_3033707_600x800_resize_q75_box.jpg)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/after.jpg "full size") [![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/before_hubd9bf75ddebb091873b32d87b4d8e400_3970010_600x800_resize_q75_box.jpg)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/before.jpg "full size")

Everyone who’s visited my house after I installed the bulb has remarked on how cheery our living room now is, some of them before noticing the light. My partner and several friends are buying their own.

For reference, here’s the stuff I bought (note that the bulb is on sale today):

-   The cheapest-per-lumen “corn bulb” I could find on Amazon,[1](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/#fn:1) $100 for 30,000 lumens. Note that this one has a built-in cooling fan that runs decently loud (45db at 1m according to my questionably-accurate iPhone sound level meter).

-   [These E26 to E39 adapters](https://amzn.to/34JagCN) that will allow the bulb to be put in normal sockets.

-   I didn’t want to mess around with my existing fixtures, so I bought [a basic fixture rated for 250 watts](https://amzn.to/2OooCTG).

Notes:

-   The clamp on the fixture does nothing with this bulb because it’s too heavy—just take it off.

-   If you don’t want to turn the bulb off by unplugging it, consider [an outlet switch like this one](https://amzn.to/2Dzgaux) (I haven’t tried it personally).

-   Originally I stabilized the fixture by wedging it inside a piece of the lamp’s packing foam (see picture), but today I realized that I could rest the metal guard inside an empty 2lb yogurt container instead, which is better because it leaves room for the power cord to run.

-   if you want to put your bulb in an existing fixture/socket instead, make sure to check (a) that it will fit (it’s very large) and (b) that the socket is rated for 250w (many aren’t).

* * *

Why doesn’t everybody do this?!

All the blog posts were written by/about people who were described as having “crippling” seasonal depression. I’ve never been depressed, which is why it took me so long to give it a try (or even notice the correlation between light and productivity).

In retrospect, that was silly. “Seasonal affect disorder” makes it sound like it’s a discrete thing that you have or don’t, but probably everyone has noticed they’re more lethargic on cloudy days. A better model might be that seasonal depression is just the tail end of a curve in how people respond to light:

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/sad_hu48e2e224e60752d8c8beef77f69c7b92_268324_1000x702_resize_q75_box.jpg)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/sad.jpg "full size")

Typical indoor light levels run from [100-500 lux](https://www.engineeringtoolbox.com/light-level-rooms-d_708.html), which _at the high end_ is about as bright as sunrise outdoors, and about 100x dimmer than [daylight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daylight). Other than “we’ve been doing it for a while,” there seems to be no reason to expect that being in a 100x dimmer environment all day _wouldn’t_ be awful. Indoor darkness seems to be one of those things that we don’t question only because it’s been that way forever.

Until recently, though, questioning it would have been somewhat academic, because it was too expensive to buy (and power) enough bulbs to do anything about it. My bulb is an LED bulb, for which cost per lumen has been falling about 20% per year for the last 50 years ([source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitz%27s_law)). LEDs only passed fluorescents in efficiency recently, but they’re still dropping fast. 10 years ago a bulb as bright as mine might have cost $1,000 instead of $100.

But as of recently, it’s totally practical to fill your entire house with light that’s as bright as full daylight. Ambient daylight in the shade is about 10k lux, or 10k lumens per square meter. For a 70 m2 apartment, that would be 700k lumens. The bulb I bought is $100 for 35k lumens, so $2k would buy sunlight for the entire apartment. (In another 10 years, if LEDs don’t hit physical limits, it’ll be $200!)

* * *

To close, some ultra-bright-lamp FAQs:

-   **How is this different from “light therapy lamps”?** Light therapy lamps are _weak_. They only give you a reasonable amount of light if you point them directly at your face from around a foot away. A high-power corn bulb makes your entire room much brighter, thus providing a much better simulation of daylight. See [“You need more lumens.”](https://meaningness.com/metablog/sad-light-lumens)

-   **Isn’t this expensive to run?** It draws about 2 kWh per day (assuming 8h of usage), which costs about $0.30 at typical rates. This is comparable to one day of a modern fridge or one load of laundry. Power is cheap, folks!

-   **Isn’t using this much power bad for the planet?** Coal has a carbon intensity of about 1 kg CO2e / kWh ([source](http://shrinkthatfootprint.com/electricity-emissions-around-the-world)), so one coal-powered lamp-day produces 2 kg, approximately one-third to one-half of a cheeseburger ([source](http://www.openthefuture.com/cheeseburger_CF.html)).

-   **Isn’t 5000K very “cold” light?** “Cold” lights (above 2700k, the typical incandescent color temperature) have a bad reputation, but 5000K is actually less cold than the sun (which is about 5500-6500K), which clearly does not have a bad reputation. My totally non-validated theory is that people don’t like cold light because most cold lights (historically, mostly fluorescents) have much worse [color rendering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_rendering_index) than incandescents: that is, they emit light only in discrete parts of the visible spectrum, which changes the relative appearance of colors and makes everything look weird. For instance, compare the two example spectra of a fluorescent vs. an incandescent:

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/fluorspec_huf361eca36a7d1a29d7eafc3d048a6288_84026_600x366_resize_box_3.png)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/fluorspec.png "full size")

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/ledspec_huba6fcc090052d63bbbb178326696ff96_183417_600x430_resize_box_3.png)](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/ledspec.png "full size")

Left: fluorescent ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fluorescent_lighting_spectrum_peaks_labeled_with_colored_peaks_added.png)); right: LED ([source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:White_LED.png))

-   **Doesn’t it make it hard to sleep?** Yup, bulbs this bright suppress my sleepiness dramatically (that’s partly the point!). I shut it off every day when I’m done working, usually at 6pm, about 3 hours before bedtime.

-   **What are the downsides?** The lamp flickers when other current-hungry appliances turn on,[2](https://www.benkuhn.net/lux/#fn:2) has a loud fan, looks ugly, is hard to set up a nice fixture for, doesn’t have an on/off switch (I just unplug it), and doesn’t make it easy to provide even illumination throughout a space. I’m working on building a DIY ultra-bright lighting setup that I expect to be way more effective on all these dimensions—stay tuned.
