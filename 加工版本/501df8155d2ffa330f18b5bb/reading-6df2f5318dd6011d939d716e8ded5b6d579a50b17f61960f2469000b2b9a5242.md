# Mini Blog Post 12: The Map is not the Territory — Neel Nanda

---

## 正文

我曾经 internalised 的最重要的 concepts 之一，是“[map 不是 territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map%E2%80%93territory_relation)”这句话。map 指的是我的 mind 和 internal experiences：我的 beliefs、intuitions、feelings、memories、sensory experiences。而 territory 指的是 objective reality，也就是我之外的世界。“当你不再相信它时，它也不会消失的东西。”

和所有最重要的 insights 一样，这并不_感觉_像一个 insight——谈论它时，很难不让它显得显而易见。_显然_，reality 存在于我的头脑之外，而我的 perceptions 并不是 perfectly accurate。对我来说，感觉关键的部分是：我_只有_ map。Objective reality 存在于我的头脑之外，但我_只能_看到自己的 internal experiences。然而，我在乎的一切都 lives in the territory。因此，我的 goal 是从自己的 map 中，尽可能 infer 关于 territory 的情况。而 map 并不会_自动_ correspond to territory，所以这需要仔细思考并付出努力。

而关键的困难在于，从内部看，map _感觉_就像是 territory。我的 beliefs 并没有附上标签，说明哪些是 biased，哪些不是；它们全都只是感觉像 _truth_。而如果我在乎 finding objective truth，我就需要训练一种 skill：越过这种感觉，尽我所能地 understand 那个 true territory。

用 vision 的 example 来说明，这一点也许最清楚。当我环顾四周时，世界里到处都是 objects。但我拥有的只有自己的 map——light 从 objects 上 bounces off，hits 我的 retina，并在我的 brain 中被 converted 为 signals。这里存在相当强的 correspondence，所以我常常可以假装 map _就是_ territory。但这并不会自动成立，我的 mind 在幕后做了许多事情。[optical illusions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_illusion) 最清楚地展现了这一点。如果我们忘记了 [unreliable eye-witness testimony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyewitness_testimony#Reliability) 究竟可能有多不可靠，那么这种情况与我们的 memories 结合时，就会带来真实的 real-world consequences。

不过，vision 是一个容易理解的 case。通过 light 从 object 上 bouncing off，它与 reality 之间有着_显然的_ entanglement。当涉及我们的 intuitions，以及关于世界的 beliefs 时，这件事就有趣得多。要在生活中做成任何事情，我都需要对 territory 有 accurate view，understanding 什么是 true，以及我的 actions 会带来什么 consequences。而我常常_感觉_自己对 territory 有 clear view，实际上却是在透过自己的 biases 和 assumptions 所构成的 flawed lens 看它——那是一张有着许多涂画的 map。Intuitions 和 assumptions 可能很重要，也可能 contain 许多 information，但这并不是_自动的_。这需要用心留意。

我发现，说“X lives in the map”和“X lives in the territory”，能让我获得许多 conceptual clarity。这是 track subtle errors 的好办法，也是整个 heuristics & biases literature 建立其上的 fundamental insight。用这个 insight 来表述，我以前的许多文章就都能串起来了：

-   [我们不擅长善待别人](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-10-seek-positive-externalities)，因为 goal 是让别人更 happier，而这 lives in the territory。但我们关于该如何做到这一点的 intuitions 则 live in the map，而且会 misses 许多 creative options

-   [我的 intuitions 对 optimising things 而言是 poor guide](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-9-what-it-means-to-optimise)，因为我的 _goals_ live in the territory，而我的 intuitions 则 live in the map。

-   [Morality 很难](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-8-what-altruism-means-to-me)，因为我_真正_在乎的东西 lives in the territory，而我的 caring 感受和 altruism 则 lives in the map。对我来说，这是 [Effective Altruism（有效利他主义）](https://effectivealtruism.org/) 的 core insights 之一

-   [面对生活中的 problems，我感到 helpless，但只要我足够 creative，它们就是 fixable](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-7-problems-are-for-fixing)。因为“这_是_ solvable”是关于 territory 的 fact，而“这_似乎_ solvable”则是关于 map 的 fact

-   [我的 perform 往往远低于自己本来能达到的水平](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-6-stop-pressing-the-try-harder-button)，因为“我_能够_做什么”是关于 territory 的 fact，却会被 confused 为 map 中的 question：“我怎样才能在这件事上更加努力”

-   [我常常能通过 paraphrasing 来 detect misunderstandings](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-5-how-to-learn-from-conversations)，因为“我是否 understand 了他们的意思”是 territory 中的 question，而“我是否_感觉_自己 understood 了”则是 map 中的 question

-   [我常常未能 take action](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/become-a-person-who-actually-does-things)，因为“现在 acting 是否会 help 我的 long-term goals”是 territory 中的 question，而“现在是否_看起来_是 act 的 perfect time”则是 map 中的 question

-   [我常常未能采取 deliberate, purposeful actions](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/be-deliberate)，因为“这个 action 是否 help 我的 long-term goals”是 territory 中的 question，而“这是否_感觉_像我应该做的事情”则 lives in the map

-   [我做的 experiments 从来都不够多](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/the-value-of-experiments)，因为“这个 experiment 是否会教会我什么”是关于 territory 的 question，而“我是否_感觉_自己有什么可学的”则 lives in the map

对于你还没读过的文章，这些说法可能不太清楚，但希望它们能让你多少体会到，这个 insight 究竟有_多么_重要。几个常见的 trends 是，我的 map 在 tracking 某些重要事物时 systematically 表现很差：abstract ideas，例如 opportunity costs 和 value of information；我还没有想到的 creative ideas；overweighting risks 和 underweighting rewards；关于我的 beliefs 的 uncertainty。

提醒一下：这是一个 leaky abstraction——本文的教训_不是_把 map 中的一切都扔掉。我的 map 的 key features 之一，是我的 emotions，以及我的 feelings。Noticing“这让我 happy”是 valuable information，因为“我是 happy”是关于 map _和_ territory 的 fact。我的 mind lives in the real world，而我在乎其中发生的事情。但即使在这里，也必须 distinguish 这一点。选择职业时，我在乎什么会让我在 longterm 中 happy，这是 territory 的 question。而 map 却会用“什么_感觉_会让我 happy？”这个 question 来 substitutes 它。我认为，无数人为了钱做着没有灵魂、却并未真正让他们 happy 的工作，背后的 key error 就在这里。

我认为，世界上几乎所有人都会弄错 map 与 territory 之间的 distinction。这并不是因为人们愚蠢！这是一个难以掌握的 insight，而且很_难_始终留意。在抽象层面 understand 一个 bias 很容易，但从内部看，一个 bias 感觉只是另一个 belief。因此，你需要越过这些 intuitions，制作出_能够_ distinguish 这些东西的 tools。我的一些做法是：

-   对 subtle cues 进行 [Noticing](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/GLPaZamxqkx7XJbXv/the-skill-of-noticing-emotions)，例如 confusion 或 overconfidence

-   与别人交谈，请他们提出建议和批评

-   主动寻找 opposing views，并真诚地倾听

-   对自己的 beliefs 进行 Challenging，并试着弄清楚自己为什么错了

-   查看 evidence，以及那些更 grounded 于 territory 的东西

在更实际的层面上，我有时发现，这在 processing emotions 时非常有用，尤其是 insecurities。从内部看，一个 insecurity 就是我心底一个纠缠不休的 doubt 声音。总是往最坏处想——别人是不是不喜欢我？这够好吗？我是不是该停止[做事情](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/become-a-person-who-actually-does-things)？我是不是表现得很奇怪？而从最初这颗 doubt 的种子开始，人很容易陷入 paralysing insecurity 和 self-doubt 的 spiral，难以逃脱。

通常会尝试的做法，是运用 willpower 并让自己分心，以此忽略它。但这对我效果很差，因为它_可能_是 true。我无法单凭 force of will，就让自己相信一个谎言。但 insecurities live in the map，而 doubt 涉及的是 reality、也就是 territory 的 question。Insecurity 是我的 map 中的 systematic flaw——总看最坏的一面，缺乏 calibration。因此，为了 overcome 它，我会寻找_更强_的东西，寻找与 reality 有着更好 connected 关系的东西。因为 notice flaws 并 question 自己的那种 impulse，是我尊重、也不会从自己身上去除的东西。如果我试图凭 force of will 忽略它，我的一部分就会反抗。但我的所有部分，都能为了 finding _truth_ 而 align

我实际采用的 process：

-   在 loop 开始之前，对最初一闪而过的 insecurity 进行 [Notice](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/GLPaZamxqkx7XJbXv/the-skill-of-noticing-emotions)

-   提醒自己：“一个 insecurity 只是一个 cognitive bias”

-   寻找与 reality 有着_更好_ connected 关系的东西

    -   一位可信赖的朋友的 opinion

    -   Historical evidence（历史证据）

        -   我私下保留着一份清单，记下自己最自豪的事情、最 happiest 的 memories，以及收到过的最有意义的赞美；这能成为 ground 我的 self-esteem 的绝佳方式。

如果你从这篇文章里只记住一件事，那就记住这一点。map _不是_ territory。我在乎的一切都 lives in the territory。但我拥有的只有 map，而从内部看，它_感觉_就像 territory。我需要始终小心留意两者的 difference。

## 原文

One of the most important concepts I’ve ever internalised is the phrase “[The map is not the territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map%E2%80%93territory_relation)”. The map refers to my mind and my internal experiences: my beliefs, my intuitions, my feelings, my memories, my sensory experiences. And the territory refers to objective reality, the world outside me. “That which, when you stop believing in it, doesn't go away.”

As with all of the most important insights, this doesn’t _feel_ like an insight - it’s incredibly hard to talk about without seeming obviously true. _Obviously_ reality exists outside of my head, and my perceptions aren’t perfectly accurate. The part that feels key to me is that I _only_ have the map. Objective reality exists outside of my head, but I can _only_ see my internal experiences. But everything I care about lives in the territory. So, my goal is to infer what I can about the territory from my map. And the map does _not_ automatically correspond to the territory, so this takes careful thought and effort.

And the key difficulty is that, from the inside, it _feels_ like the map is the territory. My beliefs don’t have labels attached saying which ones are biased and which ones are not, they all just feel like _truth_. And if I care about finding objective truth, I need to train the skill of looking past this, and trying as hard as I can to understand the true territory.

This is perhaps clearest with the example of vision. When I look around me, the world is full of objects. But all I have is my map - light bounces off objects, hits my retina, and is converted to signals in my brain. And there’s a pretty strong correspondence here, so often I can pretend the map _is_ the territory. But this is not automatically true, and my mind is doing a lot of things behind the scenes. This is made clearest with [optical illusions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_illusion). And this has genuine, real-world consequences when combined with our memories, if we forget how [unreliable eye-witness testimony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyewitness_testimony#Reliability) can be.

But vision is an easy case. There’s an _obvious_ entanglement with reality, via light bouncing off an object. This is far more interesting with our intuitions, and beliefs about the world. To get anything done in life, I need to have an accurate view of the territory, understanding what is true and what the consequences of my actions will be. And it often _feels_ like I have a clear view of the territory, when really I’m looking at it through the flawed lens of my biases and assumptions - a map with a lot of scribbles on it. Intuitions and assumptions can be important, and can contain a lot of information, but this is not _automatic_. It’s something that needs care and attention.

I find I get a lot of conceptual clarity from saying “X lives in the map” vs “X lives in the territory”. This is a good way to track subtle errors, and is the fundamental insight upon which the entire heuristics & biases literature is built. A lot of my previous posts click into place when phrased in terms of this insight:

-   [We’re bad at being nice to others](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-10-seek-positive-externalities), because the goal is to make other people happier, which lives in the territory. But our intuitions for how to do this live in the map, and misses a lot of creative options

-   [My intuitions are a poor guide for optimising things](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-9-what-it-means-to-optimise), because my _goals_ live in the territory, while my intuitions live in the map.

-   [Morality is hard](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-8-what-altruism-means-to-me), because what I _truly_ care about lives in the territory, while my sense of caring and altruism lives in the map. To me, this is one of the core insights of [Effective Altruism](https://effectivealtruism.org/)

-   [I feel helpless about problems in my life, but they’re fixable if I’m creative enough](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-7-problems-are-for-fixing). Because “this _is_ solvable” is a fact about the territory, while “this _seems_ solvable” is a fact about the map

-   [I often perform far below what I could](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-6-stop-pressing-the-try-harder-button), because “what _could_ I do” is a fact about the territory, which gets confused for the question “how can I try harder at this” in the map

-   [I can often detect misunderstandings by paraphrasing](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/mini-blog-post-5-how-to-learn-from-conversations), because “did I understand what they meant” is a question in the territory, while “does it _feel_ like I understood” is a question in the map

-   [I often fail to take action](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/become-a-person-who-actually-does-things), because “will acting now help my long-term goals” is a question in the territory, while “does now _seem_ like the perfect time to act” is a question in the map

-   [I often fail to take deliberate, purposeful actions](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/be-deliberate), because “does this action help my long-term goals” is a question in the territory, while “does this _feel_ like something I should be doing” lives in the map

-   [I never do enough experiments](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/the-value-of-experiments), because “will this experiment teach me something” is a question about the territory, while “does it _feel_ like I have something to learn” lives in the map

This may not be very clear for the posts you haven’t read, but hopefully it gives some flavour of just _how_ important this insight is. A few common trends are that my map is systematically bad at tracking some important things: abstract ideas, like opportunity costs and the value of information; creative ideas I haven’t thought of yet; overweighting risks and underweighting rewards; uncertainty about my beliefs.

A warning: This is a leaky abstraction - the lesson of this post is _not_ to throw away whatever is in the map. One of the key features of my map is my emotions, and what I feel. Noticing “this makes me happy” is valuable information, because “I am happy” is a fact about the map _and_ the territory. My mind lives in the real world, and I care about what happens inside of it. But even here, it’s important to distinguish this. When choosing a career, I care about what will make me happy in the longterm, a question of the territory. While the map substitutes this for the question “what _feels_ like it’ll make me happy?” I think this is the key error behind the countless people working soulless jobs for money, that isn’t really making them happy.

I think the distinction between the map and the territory is something that almost everybody in the world gets wrong. And this is not because people are stupid! This is a difficult insight, and it’s _hard_ to keep track of. It’s easy to understand a bias in the abstract, but from the inside a bias feels like just another belief. So you need to look past these intuitions, and craft tools that _can_ distinguish these things. Some of mine:

-   [Noticing](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/GLPaZamxqkx7XJbXv/the-skill-of-noticing-emotions) subtle cues, like confusion or overconfidence

-   Talking to others and asking for advice and criticism

-   Seeking out opposing views and genuinely listening

-   Challenging your beliefs, and trying to figure out why you’re wrong

-   Looking at evidence, and things that are more grounded in the territory

On a more practical level, I sometimes find this very useful when processing emotions, especially insecurities. From the inside, an insecurity is a niggling voice of doubt at the back of my mind. Always thinking the worst - do people dislike me? Is this good enough? Should I stop [doing things](https://www.neelnanda.io/blog/become-a-person-who-actually-does-things)? Am I being weird? And from this initial seed of doubt, it’s easy to start on a spiral of paralysing insecurity and self-doubt, from which it’s hard to escape.

The standard thing to try is to ignore this, by applying willpower and distracting myself. But this works badly on me, because it _could_ be true. I can’t make myself believe a lie by sheer force of will. But insecurities live in the map, while the doubt is about a question of reality, of the territory. Insecurity is a systematic flaw in my map - seeing the worst, a lack of calibration. And so to overcome it I find something _stronger_, and seek something better connected to reality. Because the impulse to notice flaws and question myself is one I respect, and will not remove from myself. And part of me will rebel if I try to ignore it with force of will. But all of me can align behind finding _truth_

My process in practice:

-   [Notice](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/GLPaZamxqkx7XJbXv/the-skill-of-noticing-emotions) the fleeting of insecurity at the start, before the loop begins

-   Remind myself that “an insecurity is just a cognitive bias”

-   Seek something _better_ connected to reality

    -   An opinion of a trusted friend

    -   Historical evidence

        -   I keep a private list of the things I’m proudest of, my happiest memories, and the most meaningful compliments I’ve received, and this can serve as an excellent way to ground my self-esteem.

If there’s one thing you retain from this article, let it be this. The map is _not_ the territory. And everything I care about lives in the territory. But all I have is the map, and from the inside it _feels_ like the territory. And I need to always be careful to keep track of the difference.
