## 原文

I finally decided to drop my aging and probably vulnerability-riddled Django 1.4 app and use a static site generator instead.

Practically, almost nothing should change, except that if you’re reading this via RSS you probably just got 10 articles showing up as unread again. (Sorry!)

While I was at it, I also merged the posts previously on scraps.benkuhn.net back into here, since it’s a pain to maintain two websites. You can find them under the [scraps](https://www.benkuhn.net/tag/scraps/) tag.

I haven’t ported over the comments from either of the old sites yet, and haven’t decided what I’m going to do for comments on the new site. If you have any suggestions or preferences, you should [email me](https://www.benkuhn.net/contact/) :)
