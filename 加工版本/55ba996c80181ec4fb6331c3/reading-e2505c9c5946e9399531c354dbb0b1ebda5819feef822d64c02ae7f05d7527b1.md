## 正文

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/drudgery.png)

大多数 work 都很糟糕。

你的 boss 要求苛刻，tasks 卑微琐碎，你得到的 paid 与自身 worth 不相称，而且你似乎永远睡不够。

那么，很多人讨厌自己的 jobs，也就不足为奇了。

[FIRE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIRE_movement)（那些想在三十多岁时 retire 的人）、cryptocurrency 狂热者和 passive-income 爱好者都有一个共同点：想要逃离。逃离 work 的单调苦役。

## 为什么逃离不是答案

第一个问题是，work 往往是必要的。第二个问题是，即使你能够逃走，最终也会遇到同样的问题。

你需要 money 来生活，而假设你并非生来就有 trust fund，那就意味着你必须通过 work 来 earn 它。要在不偷窃的情况下得到这些 money，就得由其他人给你。这意味着，他们需要用它来 exchange 某种他们更加 value 的东西。

这个 value equation，正是许多 investing scams 行不通的原因。它们诱使你相信，不必 adding value 来 exchange，也能得到 money。真正的 investing 确实能做到这一点——让别人使用你的 money，作为 exchange，之后收回它，并获得 interest（或 profits）。然而，Schemes 试图绕过这一 logic，让你相信有一种办法，能让你付出比其他所有人更少的 work、承担更少的 risk，却 earn 多得多。

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/real-opportunities-vs-shams.png)

然而，仅有 Value 还不够。因为我们生活在 market economy 中，所有人都在 competing，以提供它。这意味着，如果你提供的 value 其他所有人也都能提供，它往往就会被 bid down 到 lowest price。

这个 competitive equation 表明了为什么大多数 passive-income systems 都会 fail。你也许能够 automate 一个 system，让它在你睡觉时 generate money，但又有什么能阻止别人做同样的事，并 lowering 你的 profits 呢？

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/rare-vs-common-skills.png)

Early-retirement 爱好者通过节俭生活，并保持高得惊人的 savings rate，设法避开了这个困难。如果做得好，你甚至可能将自己的 working life 缩短十年（或二十年）。

然而，彻底放弃 work 会留下一片空白。你打算如何度过自己的时间？什么会赋予你 meaning 和 purpose？Retiring early 听起来很诱人，直到你意识到，那只是一项避开自己不喜欢的事物的 plan——而不是一项（仅凭自身就能）创造你所热爱的生活的 strategy。

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/work-eliminated.png)

## 除了逃走，你应该做什么？

大多数 work 都很糟糕。但你的 work 不必如此。

你完全有可能拥有一份真正 meaningful、fulfilling 的 career。它会给你挑战，让你兴奋，让你觉得自己肩负着 mission——而不只是拥有一个 job position。

关于如何获得这种 work，经典建议是 follow your passion。勇敢一点。Quit 你的 job。进入你梦想中的 profession，即使大多数人都无法成功。

这个建议很愚蠢，而且它之所以糟糕，有两个原因。

首先，follow-your-passion 建议带来的问题，与我上面提到的其他 work-escape strategies 相同。人们自认为拥有的 pre-existing passions，其范围远比实际存在的全部 jobs 狭窄。人们梦想成为 dancer，而不是 office manager。

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/passion-vs-jobs.png)

由于那些与“passion”有关的 jobs 远少于实际存在的全部 jobs，它们往往是 extremely competitive 的。因此，它们的 pay 更低，让人难以维持生计。Competition 还可能 amplify 其他 negative tendencies。人们为了 passion 而进入的 industries 中，有多少卑劣的 bosses 和 colleagues，我已经听说过无数次了。这些人之所以能存在，是因为换成更平淡乏味的 pursuits，人们就不会忍受这种事。然而，当你在 art、film 或 non-profits 中 working 时，恶劣的人却能一直留下来，因为那些 following their passion 的人会忍受他们。

这条建议糟糕的第二个原因是，人们获得自己 passionate 的 jobs，甚至根本不是通过这种方式。_So Good They Can’t Ignore You_ 的作者、与我共同教授 [Top Performer](https://www.top-performer-course.com/) 的 Cal Newport 认为，恰恰相反，大多数人是通过 building rare and valuable skills，找到自己热爱的 work 的。

## 不要逃离你的 Career——要 Master 它

在你的 work 中 Cultivating rare and valuable skills 并不容易。但要建立一种充满你真正享受的 meaningful activity 的生活，这是唯一的道路。

然而，如果你能做到这一点，那么你想过什么样的生活，就完全由你自己决定。你可以把自己的 career capital 转化为一份 ambitious、high-paying 的 job，承担 responsibilities，并获得 lucrative rewards。或者，如果你想轻松一些，也可以通过 negotiate，减少 work，获得更 flexible 的 hours，并摆脱那些狗屁 work assignments。

没有 career capital，你在每次 negotiation 中都握着一手烂牌。结果就是，你获得的 pay 更少，尊重更少，opportunities 更少，选择自己想要的 lifestyle 的 flexibility 也更少。然而，有了这种 capital，你就可以设计自己真正想过的生活，而不只是逃离它。

## 原文

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/drudgery.png)

Most work sucks.

Your boss is demanding, the tasks are menial, you’re not paid what you’re worth and you can never seem to get enough sleep.

It’s no wonder, then, that a lot of people hate their jobs.

The thing that [FIRE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIRE_movement) (the people who want to retire in their thirties), cryptocurrency junkies and passive-income enthusiasts all have in common is the desire to escape. To get away from the drudgery of work.

## Why Escape Isn’t the Answer

The first problem is that work is often necessary. The second problem is that, even if you could run away, you’d end up with the same kinds of problems.

You need money to live, and, assuming you weren’t born with a trust fund, that means you’re going to have to work to earn it. To get that money without stealing, other people have to give it to you. Which means they need to give it in exchange for something they value more.

This value equation is the reason a lot of investing scams don’t work. They trick you into thinking you can get money without adding value in exchange. Real investing does do this—by allowing others to use your money, in exchange for it back later with interest (or profits). Schemes try to bypass this logic, however, to show you that there’s a way you can earn far more for less work and less risk than everyone else can.

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/real-opportunities-vs-shams.png)

Value, however, isn’t enough. Because we live in a market economy, we’re all competing to deliver it. Which means that the value you offer that everyone else can tends to get bid down to the lowest price.

This competitive equation, shows why most passive-income systems fail. You may be able to automate a system that can generate money while you sleep, but what’s to stop somebody else from doing the same thing and lowering your profits?

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/rare-vs-common-skills.png)

Early-retirement enthusiasts manage to avoid this difficulty by living frugally and having an insane savings rate. Done well, you might even be able to shave a decade (or two) from your working life.

However, abandoning work altogether creates a vacuum. What are you going to do with your time? What is going to give you meaning and purpose? Retiring early sounds sexy, until you realize it’s merely a plan to avoid something you don’t like—not a strategy (on its own) to create a life you’ll love.

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/work-eliminated.png)

## What Should You Do Instead of Running Away?

Most work sucks. But yours doesn’t have to.

It is possible to have a truly meaningful and fulfilling career. One which will challenge you, excite you and make you feel like you have a mission—rather than just a job position.

The classic advice for how to get this kind of work was to follow your passion. Be brave. Quit your job. Go into your dream profession, even though most people don’t make it.

This is dumb advice, and it’s bad for two reasons.

First, follow-your-passion advice creates the same problems as other work-escape strategies I mentioned above. The list of pre-existing passions people think they have is much narrower than the total jobs which exist. People dream of being a dancer, not an office manager.

![](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/passion-vs-jobs.png)

Since the “passion” jobs are far fewer than the total jobs out there, they tend to be extremely competitive. Thus they pay lower and make it hard to survive. Competition can also amplify other negative tendencies. I can’t tell you how many times I’ve heard of despicable bosses and colleagues in industries people go into for passion. They exist, because in more humdrum pursuits, people wouldn’t put up with it. However, when you’re working in art, film or non-profits, toxic people persist because those following their passion will put up with it.

The second reason this is bad advice is that it’s not even how people get jobs they’re passionate about. Cal Newport, author of _So Good They Can’t Ignore You_, and my co-instructor for [Top Performer](https://www.top-performer-course.com/), argues that, instead, most people find work they love by building rare and valuable skills.

## Don’t Escape Your Career—Master It

Cultivating rare and valuable skills in your work isn’t easy. But it’s the only path to building a life full of meaningful activity that you actually enjoy.

If you can do this, however, then the choice about what kind of life you want to live is completely up to you. You can translate your career capital into an ambitious, high-paying job with responsibilities and lucrative rewards. Or, if you prefer to take it easier, you can negotiate to work less, with more flexible hours and without bullshit work assignments.

Without career capital, you enter every negotiation with a lousy hand. As a result you get less pay, less respect, fewer opportunities and less flexibility to choose the lifestyle you want to live. With that capital, however, you can design the life you really want to live, not just escape from it.
