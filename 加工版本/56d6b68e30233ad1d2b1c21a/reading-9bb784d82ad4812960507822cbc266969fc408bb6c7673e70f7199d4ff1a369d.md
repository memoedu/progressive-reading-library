# Why values change: some theories

---

## 正文

过去，slavery 是一种常见做法，将 women 和 homosexuals 置于较低的 moral standing 也是如此。Violence 被视为 honorable，而 war 和 torture 更为常见。Values 往往更加 communitarian/collectivist，而 tradition 则备受尊重。

此外，在 Industrial Revolution 期间，Europe 的（一些）人经历了 [values](http://stanfordpress.typepad.com/blog/2016/01/the-six-virtues-of-intelligence.html) 的 change（'burgeois virtues'）；众所周知，这对于 explain 为什么它首先发生在 Europe 而不是 China 至关重要。

这里，我介绍一些试图 explain value change 的 nature 的 theories。

需要注意的是，这些 theories 并不探讨 morality 最初为什么会存在。那不是它们的关注点。还有其他 theories 试图 explain morality，或找出它的 structure（在 moral psychology 中，至少有 Haidt's Social Intuitionist Theory、Greene's Dual Process Theory、Kohlberg's Moral Development Theory 和 Mikhail's Universal Moral Grammar）。

这些 theories 并不试图 explain 为什么 values 会 change，而是试图 explain 它们为什么是现在这个样子。我在下面汇集的 theories 关注的是为什么会发生那种 change。

**Imposition theory（\[需要 citation\]）**

拥有 fitter values 的 Societies 可以 conquer 拥有 unfitter values 的 societies，并 impose 自己的 values。最终，那些 promoted societal capacity for domination（包括 intra-societal cooperation）的 values 将会 predominate。

另一种 channel 是，hold power 的 elites 利用 power 来 influence societal values，从而 promote 那些 benefit elites 的 values。

还有一种情况是，succesful cultures 通过它们的 cultural production（History books、movies、music，甚至 memes）来 spread 自己的 values。这会 shapes 其他 cultures 中的 values。

**Cultural evolution theory（[Hayek](http://www.wiwi.uni-jena.de/Papers/wp-b0001.pdf)）**

Values 的 change 可以比 genetic evolution 所允许的更快。虽然存在一个为 tribal setting 而 evolved 的 moral values 的 core，但那些开始持有能够 promote economic growth 的 values（对 property rights、contracts、markets 的 respect）的 tribes 将会 thrive，并 displace 其他 societies。那些希望改善自身处境、却不那么 succesful 的 societies，最终会 imitated 那些 Succesful societies。

**Sentimentalist theory（[Smith](http://fee.org/freeman/adam-smith-moral-philosopher/)）**

Human being 对于 "mutual sympathy of sentiments" 有一种 innate desire，而且总体而言，具有一种 [moral sense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moral_sense_theory)：在别人身上看到与自己相同的 sentiments，会让我们感到愉快。这起到一种 centripetal force 的作用，drives 人们趋向那些确保自己能与他人的 values 'fit in' 的 rules。

**Cognitive theory（[Kanazawa](http://personal.lse.ac.uk/kanazawa/pdfs/JBS2009.pdf)、[Huemer](http://link.springer.com/article/10.1007%2Fs11098-015-0588-9)）**

Increased cognitive abilities（通过 Flynn effect）会 lead to values 的 change。要么是因为那些 values 逐渐被视为 true（Huemer），要么是因为 intelligence 会 leads 人们从 evolutionary point of view 去 seek novel values（Kanazawa）。

**Pathogen load theory（[Thornhill](http://www.psmag.com/health-and-behavior/bugs-like-made-germ-theory-democracy-beliefs-73958)）**

Pathogens 会 trigger 一种以不同 values 为形式的 response（总体而言是 conservative 的 values），这些 values 有助于 reduce infection 和 spread of disease。

**Modernization theory（[Inglehart, Welzel](http://my.fit.edu/%7Egabrenya/cultural/readings/Inglehart-Baker-2000.pdf)、[Newson, Richerson](http://www.des.ucdavis.edu/faculty/richerson/newsonrichersonwhymodern.pdf)）**

Industrialization、economic development，以及 society 的 economic structure 中的其他 changes，会 lead to societal values 的 changes。

**Technological theory（\[需要 citation\]，也许与 Modernization 相同）**

Technology 通过 changing individual 可用的不同 options 的 cost/benefit structure，来 changes values。当无法获得 contraception 或 abortion 时，Women 可能被视为 lower status；但当这些可以获得时，她们便[逐渐被视为 moral equals](http://politikon.es/2015/06/02/bajo-los-adoquines-los-anticonceptivos-i/)，并相应地被 granted rights。

补充：也许[这](http://www.jeremygreenwood.net/papers/fgg14.pdf)算得上 evidence

你应该已经注意到，将所有这些 theories 放在一起时，问题出在哪里：technological advancement、wealth、intelligence、low pathogenic load，以及相对 liberal 的 values，具有不可忽视的 correlations。因此，你需要找到一种 test，使你能够 discriminate 它们，尽管很有可能，所有这些 theories 都对最终的 value change 有一定 influence。

## 原文

In the past, slavery was a common practise, as was holding women and homosexuals in a lower moral standing. Violence was seen as honorable, and war and torture were more common. Values tended to be more communitarian/collectivist, and tradition was something that was highly respected.

Also, during the Industrial Revolution, (some) people in Europe went through a change in [values](http://stanfordpress.typepad.com/blog/2016/01/the-six-virtues-of-intelligence.html) ('burgeois virtues') that is known be a key to explain why it happened first in Europe, but not in China.

Here I present some theories that try to explain the nature of value change.

It is important to note that these theories do not address why morality is out there in the first place. That's not their point. There are other theories that try to explain morality, or find its structure (In moral psychology there's at least Haidt's Social Intuitionist Theory, Greene's Dual Process Theory, Kohlberg's Moral Development Theory, and Mikhail's Universal Moral Grammar).

These theories don't try to explain why values change, they try to explain why they are how they are right now. The theories I gather below focus on why that change happens.

**The Imposition theory (\[citation needed\])**

Societies with fitter values could  conquer societies with unfitter ones, and impose their own. In the end, values that promoted societal capacity for domination (including intra-societal cooperation) would predominate.

Another channel would be that elites who hold power use it to influence societal values as to promote those values that benefit the elites.

Yet another one would be that succesful cultures spread their values via their cultural production (History books, movies, music, or even memes). This shapes values in other cultures.

**The Cultural evolution theory ([Hayek](http://www.wiwi.uni-jena.de/Papers/wp-b0001.pdf))**

Values can change faster than genetic evolution would allow. While there is a core of moral values that evolved for a tribal setting, tribes that come to hold values that promote economic growth (respect for property rights, contracts, markets) will thrive and displace other societies. Succesful societies will end up being imitated by less succesful ones seeking to improve their conditions.

**The Sentimentalist theory ([Smith](http://fee.org/freeman/adam-smith-moral-philosopher/))**

Human being have an innate desire for "mutual sympathy of sentiments", and in general, a [moral sense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moral_sense_theory): it pleases us to see in others the same sentiments we have. This acts as a centripetal force that drives people towards rules that ensure that they 'fit in' with others' values.

**The Cognitive theory ([Kanazawa](http://personal.lse.ac.uk/kanazawa/pdfs/JBS2009.pdf), [Huemer](http://link.springer.com/article/10.1007%2Fs11098-015-0588-9))**

Increased cognitive abilities (through the Flynn effect) lead to a change in values. Either because those values simply come to be seen as true (Huemer), or because intelligence leads one to seek novel values from an evolutionary point of view (Kanazawa).

**The Pathogen load theory ([Thornhill](http://www.psmag.com/health-and-behavior/bugs-like-made-germ-theory-democracy-beliefs-73958))**

Pathogens trigger a response in the form of different values (conservative ones, in general) that are useful to reduce infection and spread of disease.

**The Modernization theory ([Inglehart, Welzel](http://my.fit.edu/%7Egabrenya/cultural/readings/Inglehart-Baker-2000.pdf), [Newson, Richerson](http://www.des.ucdavis.edu/faculty/richerson/newsonrichersonwhymodern.pdf))**

Industrialization, economic development, and other changes in the economic structure of society lead to changes in societal values.

**The Technological theory (\[citation needed\], maybe same as Modernization)**

Technology changes values through changing the cost/benefit structure of the different options available to an individual. Women may be regarded as lower status when there is no access to contraception or abortion, but when those are available, they [come to be regarded as moral equals](http://politikon.es/2015/06/02/bajo-los-adoquines-los-anticonceptivos-i/) and granted rights accordingly.

EDIT: Perhaps [this](http://www.jeremygreenwood.net/papers/fgg14.pdf) counts as evidence

You will have noticed what is problematic about all these theories taken together: That technological advancement, wealth, intelligence, low pathogenic load, and relatively liberal values have non-negligible correlations. So you would need to find a test that allows you to discriminate among then, though it is highly plausible that all of these theories have some influence in the resulting value change.
