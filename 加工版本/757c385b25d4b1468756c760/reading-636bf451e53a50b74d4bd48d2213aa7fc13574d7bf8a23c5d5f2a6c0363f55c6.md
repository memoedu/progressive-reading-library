## 原文

My radio silence for the past month or so was due to the end-of-semester crunch time. But I’m safely on vacation now and hope to get back into the rhythm of regular blogging soon. A good starting point is probably some major status updates that have transpired in the last month:

-   After becoming increasingly dissatisfied with my ability to get things out of Harvard, I decided that this past semester would be my last. I’ll still be graduating on schedule with the class of ‘15, thanks to advanced placement tests, but I get to spend the next year doing whatever I want instead of taking more [bogus general education courses](https://www.benkuhn.net/gened).

-   Since I’m not at Harvard anymore, I’m also free to live wherever I want. For now that means Berkeley, CA, the Cambridge of the West. If you’re in the area, say hi!

-   I [loved my internship at GiveWell](https://www.benkuhn.net/gw) and got a lot out of it, but in the end I thought that it probably wasn’t my comparative advantage for doing good in the world. So I’m back to computer science, which I’m excited about for a number of reasons (about which more anon).

-   My current foray back into the world of computer science starts in two weeks. I’ll be working at a very small startup (so small it doesn’t have a good name yet!) on using machine learning to revolutionize the stodgy industry of credit lending and securitization, or something like that. I’ll probably write about it here after I join (and after they leave stealth mode).

-   To commemorate these various updates, and also my return to having time to write, I’ve updated the site design! [Let me know](https://www.benkuhn.net/contact/) if you have constructive criticism, or if you run into any bugs.
