哪些 problems 是人类_只有_经过非常漫长的时间才能 solve 的？我们又该如何 build 能够 solve 那些 problems 的 institutions？

下面列出了一些人类历经格外漫长的时间所 undertaken 的非凡 projects。许多 examples 由 [Twitter](https://twitter.com/michael_nielsen/status/1224902391331319809) 上的网友提供。

这里关注的是 goal-directed projects（例如 scientific experiment 或 building），而较少关注更 decentralized 或 unplanned 的 changes（例如 languages、domestication of livestock、cities、religions）。当然，那些也很迷人，但它们较少体现出 long-term goal 的意味。Jericho 令人惊叹，但它的 founders 当时大概并没有想过：“我希望 9,000 年后这里仍然存在。”

本页是对 [Patrick Collison 的 /fast projects 清单的一番发挥](https://patrickcollison.com/fast)。这些 projects 与那些 projects 之间的 commonalities 多得令人惊讶。

-   Fermat's Last Theorem 的 proof 涉及数量惊人的 mathematics，这些 mathematics 是在数十年乃至数百年间 developed 的：[wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiles%27s_proof_of_Fermat%27s_Last_Theorem)。

-   许多 Cathedrals 的 built 历时超过一个世纪。[Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notre-Dame_de_Paris) 就是一个 example，历时 1163-1345。这样的例子还有很多。

-   一个至今仍在进行的 example 是 [Sagrada Familia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagrada_Fam%C3%ADlia)，于 1882 年开始，一直持续到今天。

-   [Cape Grim Air Archive](https://research.csiro.au/acc/capabilities/cape-grim-air-archive/)，自 1978 年以来一直在 archiving air，以便后来的 researchers 能够 conduct longitudinal studies。

-   [Framingham Heart Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framingham_Heart_Study)，一项始于 1948 年、关于 heart 的 longitudinal study。

-   [Central England Temperature series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_England_temperature)，从 1659 年延续至今。

-   [LIGO gravitational wave detector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LIGO) 的早期 designs 于 1967 年 developed；instrument 本身的 developed 历时数十年，并于 2016 年首次 saw gravitational waves[1](https://michaelnotebook.com/slow/index.html#fn1)。

-   [E. coli long-term evolution experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._coli_long-term_evolution_experiment)，从 1988 年延续至今。

-   [pitch drop experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_drop_experiment)，从 1927 年延续至今[2](https://michaelnotebook.com/slow/index.html#fn2)。

-   [Clock of the Long Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clock_of_the_Long_Now)，期望能持续 10,000 年。

-   我猜，许多关键的 open source systems（Linux、Wikipedia）在 100 年后仍会存在。如果某些 standards 在一千年后仍然出现，也许是以 modified 的 form 出现，我也不会惊讶。ASCII gives rise to Unicode gives rise to $$???$$。Unix Time 或 TCP/IP 将来会被 replaced 吗？Modified：当然。但至少有一些可能，它们会陪伴我们数千年。

-   [Manhattan 的 2nd Ave Subway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Avenue_Subway)，其 preparatory construction 始于 1942 年。First phase 于 2017 年 opened。

-   这份[世界上 oldest companies 的清单](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_oldest_companies)发人深省。遗憾的是，始于 578 年的 [Kongo Gumi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kong%C5%8D_Gumi) 于 2006 年 demise。它从事 construction business，最初是 Shinto Shrines 的 construction（如果我没记错的话）。这样的 Shrines 会经历 continuous 的 renewal 过程；[Izumo-taisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izumo-taisha) 就是一个 example，其历史可能追溯至 BCE。

-   [Study of Mathematically Precocious Youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Study_of_Mathematically_Precocious_Youth)，始于 1971 年，截至 2025 年仍在继续（由 Tom McCarthy 提供）

-   Wikipedia 列出了[许多其他 long-term exeriments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-term_experiment)。

一个有趣的问题：这些 projects 中，哪些是_必须_花很长时间的，哪些本可以 greatly accelerated？

## 原文

[![](https://michaelnotebook.com/assets/home.png)](https://michaelnotebook.com/index.html)

What problems can human beings _only_ solve over a very long period of time? And how can we build institutions that solve those problems?

Below is a list of marvellous projects which human beings have undertaken over an exceptionally long time. Many examples contributed by people on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/michael_nielsen/status/1224902391331319809).

The focus is on goal-directed projects (e.g., a scientific experiment or a building), less on more decentralized or unplanned changes (e.g., languages, domestication of livestock, cities, religions). Of course, those are also fascinating, but they have less of a sense of a long-term goal. Jericho is incredible, but the founders probably weren't thinking "I hope this is still here in 9,000 years".

This page is a [riff on Patrick Collison's list of /fast projects](https://patrickcollison.com/fast). There are surprisingly many commonalities with those projects.

-   The proof of Fermat's Last Theorem involved an incredible amount of mathematics developed over decades and centuries: [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiles%27s_proof_of_Fermat%27s_Last_Theorem).

-   Many Cathedrals were built over more than a century. An example is [Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notre-Dame_de_Paris), over 1163-1345. There are many more.

-   An ongoing example is the [Sagrada Familia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagrada_Fam%C3%ADlia), begun in 1882, and continuing today.

-   The [Cape Grim Air Archive](https://research.csiro.au/acc/capabilities/cape-grim-air-archive/), which has been archiving air since 1978, so later researchers can conduct longitudinal studies.

-   The [Framingham Heart Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framingham_Heart_Study), a longitudinal study of the heart which began in 1948.

-   The [Central England Temperature series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_England_temperature), from 1659 to the present.

-   The early designs for the [LIGO gravitational wave detector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LIGO) were developed in 1967; the instrument itself was developed over many decades, and first saw gravitational waves in 2016[1](https://michaelnotebook.com/slow/index.html#fn1).

-   The [E. coli long-term evolution experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._coli_long-term_evolution_experiment), from 1988 to the present.

-   The [pitch drop experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_drop_experiment) from 1927 to the present[2](https://michaelnotebook.com/slow/index.html#fn2).

-   The [Clock of the Long Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clock_of_the_Long_Now), which aspires to last 10,000 years.

-   I suspect many key open source systems (Linux, Wikipedia) will still be around in 100 years. I won't be surprised if certain standards still show up in a thousand years, perhaps in a modified form. ASCII gives rise to Unicode gives rise to \[???\] Will Unix Time or TCP/IP ever be replaced? Modified: sure. But there's at least some chance they'll be with us for millenia.

-   The [2nd Ave Subway in Manhattan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Avenue_Subway), with preparatory construction beginning in 1942. First phase opened in 2017.

-   The [list of oldest companies in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_oldest_companies) is food for thought. Sad to report the 2006 demise of [Kongo Gumi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kong%C5%8D_Gumi), which began in 578. It was in the construction business, originally of Shinto Shrines (I believe). Such Shrines undergo a continuous process of renewal; an example is the [Izumo-taisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izumo-taisha), which may date to BCE.

-   The [Study of Mathematically Precocious Youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Study_of_Mathematically_Precocious_Youth), begun in 1971, and continuing as of 2025 (contributed by Tom McCarthy)

-   Wikipedia lists [many other long-term exeriments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-term_experiment).

A fun question: of these projects, which _required_ a long time, and which could have been greatly accelerated?
