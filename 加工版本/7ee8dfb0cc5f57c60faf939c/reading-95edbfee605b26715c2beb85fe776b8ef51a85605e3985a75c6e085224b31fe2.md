## jhanas 是什么？

jhanas 是 Theravada Buddhist tradition 中描述的 altered states of consciousness，但它们早于 Buddha；Buddha 说，自己是向之前就在 practicing 它们的其他人学到的。

有 8 或 9 个 jhanas（如果 cessation counts as 一个 jhana），这让我相当困惑。为什么不是 120 个 jhanas，或者比如 3 个？它们为什么会是那样 discrete 的。~即使像 Daniel Ingram 这样痴迷于 deep meditation、比任何人都 meditated 得更接近 reality，甚至能看见 jhanas 的 subcomponents 的人，也仍然把那些看作 subcomponents，而 jhanas 仍然是它们本来的样子。这太疯狂了！它们是 quantized 的！这当然让我想知道，为什么会这样。我首先想到的、可能与 jhanas 类似的东西，是 [modes of vibration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLGW7cWQGEY)。~

补记：Ingram 似乎确实在 [suggest](https://pro.x.com/i/decks/1621398072822759425)，确实还存在其他 jhanas！

每个 state 都有自己的 vibe，会在这篇博文末尾描述。

Jhanas 可以是 lighter 或 deeper 的（甚至达到它们成为 awareness 中唯一事物的程度）。在 retreat 期间，我 experienced 的 jhana 属于 lighter 的一端，我猜如果继续 practicing，就会 experience deeper jhanas。

## 为什么要 jhana？

Jhourney 的答案在[这里](https://www.jhourney.io/faqs#intro-jhanas)。我自己的答案是：出于 curiosity，也希望能得到一次 brain update，让自己更加快乐，或许还能用新鲜有趣的方式看待事物。

Buddhist 的答案是：它是通往 Enlightenment 的 path 的一部分，是 [Eighfold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noble_Eightfold_Path#Right_concentration) Path 中的第 8 步。Buddhists 告诫人们不要成为 jhana junkies（笑），不要把 jhanas 误当成 enlightenment，而要把它们作为工具，而非道路的终点。在 jhana'd 之后，我能理解为什么有人会把 jhanic states 的 attainment 误当成 enlightenment，但我个人认为，要达到我所理解的 enlightened 或 awakened 的合理 definition，还需要继续往前走

## 谁最有可能 jhana

在 Jhourney retreats 上，作为一名 [experienced](https://nadia.xyz/meditation-experience?) meditator，_似乎_对 reaching 最初的 jhanas 没有太大帮助，尽管确实有助于进入 formless jhanas。现在我能理解原因了。我自己之前参加过 [Tucker Peck](https://meditatewithtucker.com/) 的一次 3 天 retreat，也曾连续几周每天做 1hr 的 Do Nothing meditation，却没有进入 jhana。我缺少一个关键要素，后面会详细讲。但一旦具备了它，一切就豁然贯通了。原来，self-acceptance 和良好的 emotional internal landscape，是进入较早那些更 embodied 的 jhanas 的关键，而我在这方面相当擅长：

-   我喜欢做我自己，我觉得自己挺棒的！
-   我不赞成憎恨任何人。哪怕是 Hitler。Hitler 就是穿着 mechahitler 套装的无辜婴儿 hitler。我们应该为那些 atoms 最终变成了那副样子而难过，并原谅它们的不明白。我认为，在某种 fundamental level 上，每个人内心深处始终都在尽自己最大的努力，即使有时最终走向了糟糕透顶的境地。
-   我在 Big Five personality system 中的 neuroticism component 上，score 接近 zero
-   我是一个 playful、curious 的人，几乎从不觉得无聊
-   我是极其 truth-driven 的，即使令人痛苦或害怕，无论多么不舒服，我也会全心全意地选择它，而不是移开视线

## 如何 Jhana：jhourney 的 approach

Jhourney 关于 jhana 的核心 instruction 是找到：

1.  找到某种能 evokes 一种 open-hearted sensation（即 object of meditation）的东西（jhourney 把这种东西叫作 'scaffolding'）
2.  对它进行 Focus
3.  如果它消失了，就再次使用 (1)。
4.  继续这样做

这些 scaffolds 可以是现实生活中的 memory、visualization、mantra，或一个 smile。最后这种基本上就是 TWIM method，我曾短暂尝试过，但没能坚持下来：脸上的 smiles 感觉很假，而且感觉自己还在引入不必要的 body tension。某种意义上，Jhourney 就是 generalized TWIM，只不过人们可以选择对自己最自然地起作用的方式。对我来说，那是女朋友开心时的一段 memory。不过，最终随着 practice 增多，其他东西也可能起作用。Jhourney 提醒 meditators 不要一直 switch scaffolding，因为重点不是对 scaffolding 进行 focus，而是它所 induces 的 sensation。

## Pali 都去哪儿了？！

我上面写的是 'open hearted sensation'；Jhourney 不是 Buddhist retreat，我遇到的 [Pali](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/glossary.html) 数量为零。不过，基本上，'open hearted sensation' 可以换成任意一种 [brahmaviharas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahmavihara)。许多 methods 建议用 loving-kindness（_metta_）来 achieve jhana，而我是在想起女朋友有多开心时，通过 empathetic joy（_mudita_）entered jhana。不过，这样说也不完全准确：和 [Leigh Brassington](https://www.leighb.com/jhana3.htm) 一样，实际 practice 中的 Jhourney 似乎接受任何 pleasant sensation，无论它 arises 于何处。

在到达那里之前，需要先 develop 出 Jhourney 所称的 _collectedness_，达到一个合理 level。我此前从未在 meditation context 中遇到过这个词；他们解释说，它像 concentration，但不是 single pointed。我想，好吧，那这不是 _ekagatta_，而是更泛指的 _upacara samadhi_，或许是 _sati_ 或 mindfulness；不过 mindfulness 也可以有其他含义。原来，西方并没有完全准确的 terms，能用来指向 guide meditation 时所必须指向的东西，所以只能在变成一个 [Pali-chanting](https://x.com/ArtirKel/status/1914711613598523512) 的 [corncob](https://x.com/dril/status/134787490526658561)，或使用一些比较新颖的词语之间作出选择，而 Jhourney 采取了后一种 approach。

更具体地说，Jhourney 要求学生对自己的 object of meditation 进行 focus，同时保持对其他一切的 awareness，也就是说，有一条明确的 instruction 是 _反对_ ekagatta 的。我想，这是不是为了避免学生过分努力地 _只_ 对 object of meditation 进行 focus，因为那会难得多得多，而且要 reach 他们所教的 jhanic states，并不需要这样。

## 要 jhana，你就必须不想要 jhana

人生中一个深刻的领悟是，有些东西要得到它，就必须不想要它。或者更准确地说，不能 crave 它。你必须 let go of jhana，真正接受自己在 retreat 期间不 jhanaing。如果你已经 unblocked 了相关的 emotional blockages，那么 jhanas 给人的感觉就像宇宙向你竖起大拇指，说你正朝着正确的方向前进。

那么，如何 jhana？答案很简单：在踏入 container 时，set the intention，表明自己确实想要 jhana；而一旦进入其中，就 let it go，只管允许它发生。取而代之的是，说服自己：如果你 sit 足够多的小时（我每天 meditating 超过 8 小时），并且以 curiosity 和 enjoyment 迎接每一个 moment，你就会 jhana。要做到这一点，你可以采用以下任一种方式：

-   注意到，在你之前的人做了完全相同的事，并且取得了 results；或者，如果你没法直接相信别人，或觉得自己拥有外星人的 brain chemistry，那么：
-   把 jhanas 看作那个竖起的大拇指，把目标设为 full-sending 自己 experience 的每一个 moment，无论发生什么，都把目标设为成为一个 enjoyer of things。Jhourney 把这叫作 'conductivity'（你对自己的 experience 接受了多少）
    -   这对我很容易：我的 Twitter 简介里真的就写着“享受万物的人”，我真心相信任何事物都能被享受，而且从不觉得无聊。
    -   如果你是个容易觉得无聊的人，jhanas（的尝试）会把你治好

这也是 Jhourney 所强调的，他们的 instruction handbook 开篇就直白地写道：_jhana 真正的价值不在于 jhanas 本身；而在于让你更容易成为自己渴望成为的那个人_

## 选择你自己的冒险

如前所述，Jhourney 允许每位参与者选择自己通往 jhana 的方式。retreat 有一个 structure：

-   两次 group sits（早上和晚上），每次约 1hr
-   与 facilitators 进行 1:1 interviews，以及用于获取 feedback 的 office hours
-   一些 breathwork sessions
-   安排一些时间阅读手册中的相关章节
-   以及充足的 solo meditation 时间

不过，当然，你想做什么都可以；而且 retreat 的 format 似乎每次都会调整；我们有 [Jonny Miller](https://www.jonnymiller.co/) 带领的 breathwork，他教的是 FBR（[CCB](https://breathemagazine.com.au/wellbeing/a-basic-guide-to-conscious-connected-breathing/) 的一种 subtype）；据另一位参与者说，之前的一次 retreat 教的是 Wim Hof breathing。

facilitators 一点也不强势，他们真心支持我的目标和 approach，提供有用的建议，或者只是陪我聊聊天（这是一次 silent retreat，有个人能让你兴奋地聊聊自己的 experiences，实在很好）。在我 achieved J1-3 之后的某个时刻，我强烈地冒出一个念头：“去他妈的 jhanas！我不搞了！我要随性地 vibe-meditate。”于是我决定停止使用建议的 practice，转而“做” Do Nothing meditation。这感觉棒极了，没那么 effortful，也更 enjoyable，而且在我没有“做任何事”的情况下，它让我一路到了 J7。感觉就像舒舒服服地窝在心里的沙发上看电影，或者躺在草地上看云。

## Radical non-self coercion 与对 emotions 的 holistic view：关键的生活窍门

如果说我在 retreat 中有什么做法与以往不同，那就是更少把自己强加于自己的 mind 和 body（尽管这听起来可能很奇怪）。

如果我想尿尿，就会中断 sit 去尿尿；如果因为 meditating 而头疼，就会停下来。如果我 restless，就出去散步。我想强调，这样做有坏的方式，也有好的方式。这些 actions 感觉是我以 whole being 作出的，而不是出自某个“对 meditating 感到无聊、想去海滩的 part”。我确实会停下来，mindfully consider 自己是否 _真的_ 想做那些事，并且 _允许_ internal consensus 逐渐形成。

最重要的是，我学会了注意到：自己试图避免 distracted 的方式，居然是 _violently push 我的 thoughts away！_ 我原以为自己很 gentle，结果并不是！我注意到，每当发现 distraction 时，自己会说“Present moment！！”，仿佛在对那个 arose 的 thought，或对没有处于 present moment 的 mind 生气。注意到这一点后，我说，好吧，这些 thoughts 对我是出于好意，它们想要什么？于是我与这些 thoughts 互动（想进一步了解这方面，我推荐阅读 Wholeness Work、Core Transformation 或 Gendlin's Focusing），拥抱它们，原谅它们不明白自己正在 distracting 我，并让它们明白，暂时离开反而能更好地实现它们的目的。比如，对于那些与未来计划有关的 thoughts（像这篇博文），我说：“如果你们少打断我，我就能更深入 jhanas，在博文里也就有更有意思的东西可写，而你们也想要一篇好博文，对吧？”说得有道理，于是它们噗地消失了。

重要的是，我 _相信_ 那些 thoughts 会离开，但即使它们回来，我也打心底里接受。我只会 lovingly、反复地做同样的事，直到自己不再那么 distracted。

另一件我认为帮到了自己的事，是 holistically 看待 emotions，也就是说，不把它们看成 cognitive states，也不把它们看成 body 中的 feelings。相反，一个 emotion 是一种具有许多 components 的 state：dispositions to actions、physiological responses、recurrent thoughts、altered attention，等等。

例如，如果我让自己的 mind 处于 J5，不怎么做什么，下面这些事就会发生：

1.  我的嘴会张开，仿佛想说“哇——”
2.  广阔蓝天的 Images 出现在我的 mind's eye 中，同时还有一个以我为中心、正在 expanding 的 sphere
3.  一种 sense，觉得 outbreath “就像这个（state）”
4.  一种一切皆有可能的 sense

这些都会在没有“做任何事”的情况下出现。但要真正理解 jhanas，就应该尝试更直接地 investigate 它们。可惜，jhourney 主要专注于让你 experience jhanas（这已经是了不起的成就！），而不是进行 Buddhist 领域中的 _insight_ meditation。Jhourney 本质上是关于“wet”（pleasant）concentration 的；而比如 Goenka vipassana（“dry” insight）retreat（我没参加过），则主要是关于 insight 的。这里的 Insight 指的是带你通往 awakening 或 enlightenment 的 path，听起来很引人好奇。

不过，因为 jhourney 确实会引导学生 cultivate curiosity，人最终还是会做一些 investigating。“嗯，我想知道，如果我在 J6 中问自己是谁，会发生什么？”或者“我的 body 到哪里结束？”或者“如果我 imagine 一个披萨，我会 crave 它吗？”某个时刻，对于“我坐着的椅子扶手是不是我的一部分”这个问题，我的 felt sense 是“不清楚”，这让我心想：“咦，真有意思。”这是一种相当轻松有趣的进入 insight 的方式！

有趣的是，虽然“dry insight”素有“hardcore”的名声，但通往 enlightenment 的 jhana path 很可能让人更 joyfully、更快地 relax into awakening。

## Belief 的 changes

这样做是否 change 了我的任何 core beliefs？我不这么认为！我的 beliefs 的 content 仍然 unchanged，但我 experience 它们的方式已经 changed。有些事，我在 intellectually 上 believed，却并没有真正从骨子里感受到。例如，过去 10 年里，如果你问我这个问题：“Fundamentally，everything 是否基本上都是 the same thing？”我会说：“是的，这一切都是某种 atom-and-awareness type stuff，[protopanpsychism](https://nintil.com/consciousness-and-its-discontents) 或某种 adjacent 的东西是 real 的。”我甚至在进行任何 meditation 之前、也没有服用任何可能让人产生这类 thoughts 的 substances 时，就已经在 Nintil 这里写过这个话题；我是 natty、unassisted 地走到这一步的。但现在，我看到了它的 truth，就像面对一个 optical illusion，你有一瞬间能看“对”，而不是得到 mistaken impression。

我现在确实认为，meditation 比我原先想的更 powerful。它真的有效。这些东西是 real 的。是的，你可以给自己的 brain install software upgrades。与 advanced meditators 相比，所有人都相对更 depressed。

另一件有趣的事是，我注意到，随着 meditation，mind 会以一些需要警惕的方式变得 pliable。在 retreat 之前，如果有人谈起比如 astrology 的 truth，我会产生非常 knee-jerky 的 reaction：“这他妈是胡扯！”但现在，这种反应消失了。现在我更接近于：“咦，有意思，这个人是怎么开始 believe 这个\[显然 wrong 的东西\]的？”这类似于 Art of Accomplishment 的 VIEW（Vulnerability、Impartiality、Empathy、Wonder）旨在教授的 mindset。幸好，我并不依靠这种 knee-jerky reaction 来理解世界，我有 reason 和 science；不过，我也能看出，如果一个不如我 vigilant 的人在没有 solid epistemics 的情况下 meditate，可能会怎样掉进 turbo-woo hole。psychedelics 也是如此。但 meditation 可能更 riskier，因为它看起来 natural，没有涉及任何 substance，而且，虽然你确实可以立即终止一次 meditation 的“trip”，而 psychedelics 就不那么容易，但 harm 可能在于，你没有注意到它正在怎样改变你与 truth、morality 这类事物的关系。

## 关于 jhana 的 Tips：我希望自己早就知道的事

如果你和我类似，这些内容可能最管用，不过，我也在这里纳入了一些资料，不久前那个远没有那么 embodied 的我应该会觉得它们有用

-   了解 Gendlin's Focusing、Core Transformation 和 Wholeness work，以更好地 understand 你的 emotions
-   Practice Yoga Nidra 和 Somatic Descent 中的 somatic meditations，进行 body scans
-   Investigate 你“pushing away thoughts”这个 move 的 nature，看看其中是否存在任何 coercion。即使你认为没有，也请考虑一下，你可能错了。
-   只要你不会睡着，Lying down 对 meditation 来说也没问题
-   你可以在一个 jhana 内 run mental experiments（你的 intuitions/mental state 是如何 altered 的），来 test 这个 jhana 的 vibe
-   你不一定会从 J1 enter jhana
-   在 pre-sit 时 Setting an intention，然后在 sit 中 letting it go，会有帮助。想想跳舞：你先设定跳舞的 intention，但一旦到了舞池，你就不会 micromanaging 每一个细小动作，而是让舞蹈自然发生。
-   不管用什么办法，都他妈给我放松下来。Visualize 自己是正在地板上摊开的煎饼面糊。

## 每天是怎么过的

我每天都写了日记，所以能准确告诉你，这些事情是什么时候、怎么发生的。抵达那天是第 0 天，离开那天是第 7 天，不过这两天没发生多少有趣的事，我就跳过了。

1.  **第 1 天**:
    1.  醒来后，仍觉得没有完全休息好。有一场 guided meditation，感觉不太好。我注意到，咖啡让我有些 jittery。哦，这就是许多 meditators 不喝咖啡、改喝茶的原因吗？于是我决定，在 retreat 剩下的日子里猛喝绿茶。我注意到 thoughts 不断冒出来（主要是关于我想做的事情，于是决定把它们写下来，即便为此必须 interrupt 一次 sit。我想，这样做能帮助我真正相信，我想到的好点子不会消失。最后，写下几个之后，它们就多少停歇了一些。
    2.  我尝试了各种 scaffoldings；微笑让我觉得 fake 而 tense，mantras（“愿所有 beings 都 happy”）没什么效果\[不过到了 retreat 后来，它们就有效果了\]，但女朋友 happy 的一段 memory 有效，于是我开始用它来 generate 一种 pleasant sensation，作为 focus 的对象。
    3.  在 sits 期间，我感觉非常 relaxed，每次会 sitting 45 min，并记下：“仍有一点 distracted，但出现了更多 focused 的时刻”，不过没有 reach jhana
2.  **第 2 天**
    1.  早上的 yoga 感觉很棒，我会推荐先用 yoga 给 meditation 热身。简直就像……它们本来就是一起 developed 的一样。
    2.  有一场带领下的 [forgiveness meditation](https://www.dhammasukha.org/forgiveness-meditation)。它对我没什么作用，虽然我知道它对其他人非常 powerful。我真的很喜欢自己，也不因做自己而羞耻，所以我猜这就是原因。

    4.  我的日记里写着“椰子：大自然的奶子”，没有任何解释。我还记下，看茶包的标签随风飘动似乎挺有意思
    5.  我去海滩散步，经历了这篇博文开头的那一刻，因一切的美丽而满怀 awe，哭了起来。我记下：“不确定为什么\[我哭了\]？只是因为美吗？”这里我试着抱着一点怀疑态度，问些有用的问题，比如“嗯，我是不是在 repressing 一种 emotion？”我往往把哭泣与 suppressed 的（negative emotion）的 release 联系起来。

        1.  一切都这么美
        2.  我们都是爱
        3.  无论你做什么，无论你说什么，无论你是谁，你永远都是爱（也永远被爱着）
    7.  我还记下了“cry=laugh”；当时我又哭又笑，注意到这两件事之间有些相似之处。我试着理解原因，却没完全弄明白。是不是当事情比你想的更糟，而你 accept 了它，就会哭；当事情比你想的更好，而你 accept 了它，就会笑；而……当事情_就只是如此_，而你对它们喊着“太他妈好了”，就会又哭又笑？
        1.  与此相关，一位朋友指出，anger 和 lust 之间存在联系
    8.  我记下，自己产生了一种 feeling，与 Psychonaut wiki 对 [MDMA](https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/MDMA#Pharmacology) 所描述的相符
        1.  “MDMA-like feeling（body warm，像有什么东西落进了我的肚子）”
    9.  那是第一次 glimpse 到 jhana！我当时想：“这就是 JHANA 吗！！？”当然，就在这时它消失了。不过，根据 Michael [Taft](https://youtu.be/0K5ypXyF3dY?si=csNVu34A6gMEjs95&t=761) 的说法，即便你立即 pop out，也仍然算是 jhana，所以我就说，我第一次 jhana-adjacent experience 发生在 retreat 的第二天。
3.  **第 3 天**
    1.  我们做了一个叫 Jhourneying 的活动，类似 circling，但纯粹是 emotional 的；一个人会问另一个人的 expereince 中有什么（比如 happiness），然后另一个人会问（它的 texture 是什么样的？），或者是否会出现其他东西。在某种程度上，它是 Circling 与 Gendlin's Focusing 这类 practices 的 hybrid。
    2.  “哇，就像嗑了 MDMA 一样”。每个人看起来都很美，都笑眯眯的，我有一种 desire，想拥抱大家，告诉每个人这一切有多美妙。但这是一次 silent retreat。不过，我并不因不能说话而感到 frustration。
    3.  我又去海滩散步，被雨淋湿了。我没有为此觉得难受，但我觉得这也不算多神奇；即便不在 retreat，我想自己的感受也会差不多。
    4.  我记下：“Welcome，而不是 push distractions <3”。就是在这时，我意识到了前面提过的 self-coercion 问题
    5.  **我 achieved 了 sustained 而 stable 的 J2**！起初我很困惑。烟花呢？？原来，我一直以为会从 J1 enter（那里的确有更多那种烟花），但我最先到达的是 J2。我记下
        1.  Head 非常 hot，停留了很久，stable，仍有 distractions 冒出来
        2.  当我进入 J2 时，body 中出现了一种“glonk” sensation（就像一颗球落进了什么东西，随后炸开，到处都是 J2 的 feeling），我的 spine arched，嘴张开了，然后我 relaxed 进垫子里（当时我躺着）。
        3.  一切都是 warm/fuzzy 的。我问：“我的 chest 里有什么？”“人们相互拥抱，一场派对”
    6.  随后我记下，自己 transitioned 到了一种 calm、still 的 state；做了一些 Gendlin 之后，它感觉像“一片向四处延伸的平静池塘”（J3）。我想，这又是另一种 jhana？酷。我进入了（也就是说，我想象自己进入了）池塘，来到一个感觉好像没什么的地方？我记下：“solidity、钢铁、岩石”。那他妈是什么？是 jhana 吗？它是 stable 的，但感觉……和其他的不一样。回头看，那的确是 J4。
        1.  说明：这些都发生在我的 mind's eye 中，并不是 dreams 或 hallucinations
        2.  我记下：“它们全都 stable”
        3.  我还注意到，自己的确能在它们之间 switch
    7.  我探究那种 solidity sensation，发现了一丝 sense of expansion（J5），但并不清楚（回头看，这一天我没有到达 J5）。我画了下面这幅图，说明那天看到的东西（那个海星一样的东西应该是我，哈哈）。注意，第一个_不是 J1_，而是一种 pre-jhana happy state，主要在 chest 和 arms 中 feel 到。我一直在想，J1 在哪儿？是不是我搞混了，J2 其实是 J1？

4.  **第 4 天**
    1.  去海滩散步，试着 meditate。探索溪流、流入海洋的小河、小小的寄居蟹和穴蟹、丛林，再捡些贝壳，都很有意思。我分了一些给 retreat 上的朋友，圣诞节时还会带一些给家人。![QmNPC0OB](https://nintil.com/images/2025-07-27-jhanas/QmNPC0OB.jpg)
    2.  我试着说了些话，结果……我的声音听起来更低沉了？还是我对 sound 的 perception altered 了？（后来我问了我的朋友、同样享受这次 retreat 的 [Meida](https://x.com/meidamarek_)，她的确确认了，我没有发疯，我的声音确实更低沉了）
    3.  记下自己有一阵子没咬指甲周围的皮了（我有时会这样做）。我摸了摸指尖，这通常会 triggers 一种带着抓取冲动的、“必须咬到光滑为止”的 OCD-like compulsion，但这一次它没有出现。
    4.  1 hour 的 group sit，这次没有 jhana。看来还不能 deterministic 地进入 jhana！
    5.  我去海滩哭了。我意识到，自己就像一个给朋友收集漂亮贝壳的孩子（也就是说，这篇博文就像是我送给大家的一枚贝壳）。我或许更聪明、想得更多，但这个行动的出发点是一样的。十多年来，看见小孩子玩得开心，总会让我有点眼眶湿润；而我一直通过 dissociating 或移开视线来避免这种情况，所以从没问过，为什么这会让我觉得不舒服。\[这一点在日记后面会变得更相关\]
    6.  然后去了泳池；我又哭了，因为我一直没有允许自己承认，自己对父母的爱其实有那么深。他们为我做了那么多！我在一个美丽的出生点（加那利群岛）度过了美好的童年，而不是在其他某个地方度过 trauma-ridden 的童年，对此我满怀感激。
        1.  我想，为什么我会 suppress 那份爱？为什么不让自己 feel 它？不清楚。也许我觉得这样会很肉麻之类的？
    7.  我在留言板上写下：“愿每个人都充满爱”。我们或许不能说话，但……写字……这个还是能做的 :)
    8.  又一次哭泣的时刻：原来我比自己以为的更 altruistic。让别人 happy，真的会让我 happy。“我以前为什么会不这么认为呢”，我一边流泪一边想
        1.  过去我说“我没那么 altruistic！”时，那种口气有点像一个暗地里是 gay 的 homophobe，大声宣称自己讨厌 gay；也就是说，全心全意地 rejecting 某种东西是一回事，而因为某些其他原因，rejecting 自己内心深处想要、或者自己本就是的东西，又是另一回事。Existential Kink 这本书讲的就是同一件事。此前我在 partner dancing 上就经历过这种情况（我不喜欢 partner dancing，和 ecstatic dance 相比，它似乎很 constrained->不想对那些擅长跳舞的人感到 jealousy），所以，自己还藏着这样的东西，我并不意外。
        2.  这一点尤其好笑，因为 10 年前，我就[写过一篇博文](https://nintil.com/egoismo-psicologico-y-altruismo)，放弃了自己此前对 [psychological egoism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_egoism) 的 belief。原来，相信一件事，和打心底里相信一件事，确实是两回事，而且它们可能不同。

    10.  我不确定，自己是不是在 forcefully imagining 自己正 feeling 的东西（就像使用 scaffolding 时那样），还是 mind's eye 中的 depictions 其实是对一种 existing feeling 的 interpretation（Gendlins 的风格）。我花了不少时间 investigating 这一点，学会了把这两者区分开，也更有 confidence，相信自己确实 reached J5 了。
    11.  又一场 breathwork session。这次是 extreme sadness，我哭了。我看见一颗小小的黑色弹珠碎裂了（它被 Gendlin-interpreted 为意指“我的 self”）。“我并不 SPECIAL”，我用全大写记下。我只是一个普通人。很长一段时间以来，虽然我知道自己只是个人类（当然，从许多 metrics 来看，我确实在好的意义上相当 unique），却不知怎么一直相信，自己如此 special，以至于 qualitatively 没有任何东西或任何人与我相似。那个 belief 死去了，我为它 grieved，就像亲眼看着一件美丽的东西在面前崩塌。然后，我感到 relief：正是那个 belief，也曾不时给我带来 existential loneliness：如果我那么 special，那确实永远不会有人能真正“懂”我，但现在看来，这不对。接着，我的 mind-eye 中又出现了一幅 vision（像看电影一样，它自行播放着）：孩子们在一片满是碎弹珠的海滩上玩耍，为自己把它们拼起来；也就是说，我们都是这些东西，由相同的 raw material 构成。
         1.  “这是 jhana 吗？”我想。我记得不够清楚，但我的确认为，这次 emotional released 为我到达 J6 做了准备。
    12.  这时，我与 facilitators 进行了一次 1:1，告诉他们自己一直在经历什么，并确认前几天的 experiences 是否确实是 jhanas，他们说看起来是的；后来，他们邀请我把自己的 experience 和给未来 students 的 tips 一起录下来，我照做了
    13.  试着 sit 1hr。感觉到了我所谓的“serotonin headache”（head warm，不舒服），于是晚上出去散步。我没有强迫自己 meditate。夜里独自坐在海边的沙滩上，我过得很愉快。
         1.  我注意到，自己想信守 commitments 的 desire——“我承诺了要 1hr sit”——与更外层的 commitment——“我来 jhourney 是为了让生活变得更好”——之间存在 conflict。然后我问：“我真的相信，信守这个 commitment、顶着 headache 硬撑会帮助我吗？”“这些 commitments 是我欠谁的？如果我停止 meditating，谁会受伤害？你会让一个有这种 headache 的人继续 meditating 吗？”不会，所以我就在那里结束了 sit。毕竟，那天发生了那么多事，让 brain 休息一下是个好主意。
         2.  在海边，我记下（文中这些“记下”的内容都逐字来自我的笔记，而不是回忆）：“我从不孤独，没有人曾真正孤独，我们在内心深处全都一样，是啊，这听起来很奇怪，哈哈；我想，孤独不意味着‘没有朋友’，而意味着“你 uniquely separate 于所有其他人”。是 unique/special->是孤独的 :) 。理解这一点时，我笑了。
         3.  回头看那一天，我注意到，在那场 breathwork session 中，自己大概处于某种 J6 adjacent 的状态，但当时太 overwhelmed，没能注意到。
5.  **第 5 天**
    1.  在海滩散步。Noticing 到岩石上的 detail 比我原以为的更多。
    2.  Group sit：确实 reached J6。岩石和我是一样的。
        1.  仍然能听见音乐（那次 sit 有一些背景音乐），这些肯定不是 deep jhanas
    3.  在其他的一次 sit 中，我试着 visualize 披萨，看看 craving 的 feeling 是否会 arise，但它没有。有一次，我给自己没有 distractions 的时长计了时：3 minutes。
    4.  做了一次我称为“gigasit”的 sit（1hr30min），是迄今最长的一次。直接在 J5 Entered，随后能够 move 到 J6，在大约 1hr 时到了 J7。
    5.  我注意到，自己有点嫌 breath 烦，感觉它很响；随着我更深入 jhanas，它变得更安静，一度几乎 stopping。我能一口气游过一个奥运泳池，所以我知道，自己可以一阵子不 breathe 而没事，而且必要时，“没空气了啊啊！”的 reflex 会启动，所以在这方面我 trusted 自己的 body。
    6.  下一次去海滩时，我觉得自己 spontaneous 而 playful。
    7.  又做了一次 sit，从 J2 entered，然后我试着看看自己能不能到 J1，结果做到了。终于！狂欢派对！euphoria！生命中 happy 的时刻从眼前闪过。有时会 Shaking，大口喘气。不过我记下，它的 intense 程度不及几天前在海滩上的感受。
        1.  我记下：“J1 <-> J2 感觉像一个旋钮，可以把 euphoric heat 调高或调低”
    8.  我试着 guess/preregister 下一次 insight 会是什么。No self？Impermanence？某种 Nonduality 的东西？\[剧透：retreat 中我一个也没获得\]
    9.  又一场 breathwork sessions，但这一次只是以非常深的方式 relaxing；我注意到，自己可以让 breath 非常 gentle，就像一个几乎没有重量的纸袋。或者说：采用一种 traditional posture 直立，而不是躺下，能让我更少地 feel 到 chest。
        1.  一个 hypothesis：也许 lying down 最适合前四种更 embodied 的 jhanas（你想更多地 feel 自己的 body），而 sitting 最适合 formless 的那些（你想更少地 feel 自己的 body？）
    10.  我记得，晚餐时有人说自己无法 feel 自己的 feelings，我便向他们解释 feelings 如何运作。谁能想到，我（一个多年来大多处于 dissociated 状态、以“我没有 emotions”为 self-identity 的人）有一天竟会向别人解释 sadness 如何运作。

            1.  岩石无法 experience 这些，真令人难过，我想（哭了一会儿）
            2.  能活着，看见我所看见的，听见我所听见的，我真幸运
            3.  谢谢大家！

## 现在呢？

更多 retreats！更多 sits！我现在确信，余生我都想以某种形式 meditate，理由和我想让身体保持健康一样。Theravada Buddhism 中的 [Fetters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fetter_\(Buddhism\)) 之一是“doubt of the path”（作宽泛理解），而 jhourney 无疑 obliterate 了那个 fetter，至少对于狭义理解的 meditation 是如此：这玩意儿是真的，而且太棒了！

## Jhanas 是什么感觉

以下是我 experienced 的内容，以及其他人用自己的话 reported 的内容。这个展示 Jhanas 据说是什么感觉的视频，与我的 [experience](https://x.com/RogerThisdell/status/1882779644635513072) 大致相符。

所有 jhanas（对我而言）都 shared 一些 properties：

-   它们是 absorbing。我的 awareness 大部分都被 jhana 所 dominated。
-   它们是 self-sustaining states。也就是说，它们给人的感觉是可以永远持续下去，就像你可以永远躺在床上一样。但你可以随意 end 它们。
-   人可以在它们之间 shift
-   处于 jhana 中时，也可以像平常一样 have thoughts、hear sounds、imagine 东西。其中有一种 altered mental state，可与例如 MDMA 的相关 reported 内容相比。
-   它们似乎是 discrete states。我没有 experience 过什么我会称为 J1.5 的东西。
-   这些 experiences 与人们所谓的“sutta jhanas（例如 [TWIM](https://www.dhammasukha.org/getting-started-with-twim) 所 teaches 的那些）”或“[soft](https://www.mctb.org/mctb2/table-of-contents/part-iii-the-samatha-jhanas/27-the-concentration-states-shamatha-jhanas/) jhanas”大致 aligned；而不是那些更 engrossing 的“hard jhanas”或“Visuddhimagga jhanas”。Jhourney 声称 teaches 这些 soft jhanas，而且他们确实做到了。
-   它们似乎存在某种 natural ordering。这个 progression 按照规定的 order 发生，lower jhanas 发生在后面的 formless jhanas 之前。
-   可以从 non-jhana 直接 pop into 一个 jhana，不必按 order 来。事实上，我最先 got into 的是 J2，过了一阵子才 get to J1。retreat 后来，我会直接 popping into J4 和 J5，而没有 lower jhanas 的 warm fuzzies。
-   它们本身不会让人 aware 到三个 Buddhist [marks of existence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_marks_of_existence)（sufering、impermanence、no-self）（正如 [Ingram](https://www.mctb.org/mctb2/table-of-contents/part-iii-the-samatha-jhanas/28-the-formless-realms/) 所 points out 的）。我仍然 feel 自己有一个 self，并不 feel 一切都是 impermanent。

## What are the jhanas?

The jhanas are altered states of consciousness described in the Theravada Buddhist tradition but preceding the Buddha, who said he learned them from others who were practicing them before.

There are 8 or 9 jhanas (if cessation counts as a jhana), and this is quite puzzling to me. Why not 120 jhanas or like 3? Why are they discrete like that. ~Even deep meditation autists like Daniel Ingram who have meditated closer to reality than anyone else to the point where they can see the subcomponents of jhanas still see those as that, subcomponents, but the jhanas are still what they are. This is crazy! They are quantized! Now of course this makes me wonder why is that so. The main thing that comes to mind that might be like jhanas are [modes of vibration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLGW7cWQGEY).~

Edit: Ingram does seem to [suggest](https://pro.x.com/i/decks/1621398072822759425) there indeed are other jhanas!

Each state has a vibe to it, describe at the end of this blogpost.

Jhanas can be lighter or deeper (to the point where they are the only thing in awareness). During the retreat I experienced jhana on the lighter end and I suspect if I keep practicing I'll experience deeper jhanas.

## Why jhana?

The Jhourney answer is [here](https://www.jhourney.io/faqs#intro-jhanas). My own answer: out of curiosity and hope that I might get a brain update to become even happier, and maybe see things in new interesting ways.

The Buddhist answer: Part of the path to Enlightenment, step 8 in the [Eighfold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noble_Eightfold_Path#Right_concentration) Path. Buddhists warn people not to become jhana junkies (lol), mistaking the jhanas for enlightenment, and to use them as a tool, not as the end of the road. After having jhana'd, I could see why people could mistake the attainment of jhanic states as enlightenment but I personally think there's more to go before one gets to what I understand as a reasonable definition of enlightened or awakened

## Who is most likely to jhana

At Jhourney retreats, being a [experienced](https://nadia.xyz/meditation-experience?) meditator _seems_ to not help much with reaching the first jhanas, though it does help with getting to the formless jhanas. I myself had been on a prior 3 day retreat with [Tucker Peck](https://meditatewithtucker.com/) and done 1hr a day of Do Nothing meditation for a few weeks without getting into a jhana. I was missing one key ingredient, detailed later. But once I had that, it all clicked. Turns out, self-acceptance and a good emotional internal landscape are key to the earlier more embodied jhanas, and I'm quite good at that:

-   I don't endorse hating anyone. Even say Hitler. Hitler is innocent baby hitler in a mechahitler suit. We should feel sad that those atoms ended up that way and forgive them for not understanding. I think everyone is always trying their best all the time deep down at some fundamental level, even if that ends up in fucked up places sometimes.
-   I score near zero in the neuroticism component in the Big Five personality system
-   I'm a playful curious person that is rarely if ever bored
-   I am extremely truth-driven, even if painful or scary I wholeheartedly will choose that over looking away, no matter how uncomfortable

## How to Jhana, the jhourney approach

Jhourney's core instruction to jhana is to find:

1.  Find something (something jhourney calls 'scaffolding') that evokes an open-hearted sensation (the object of meditation)
2.  Focus on that
3.  If it goes away, use (1) again.
4.  Keep going at it

These scaffolds could be things like a real life memory, a visualization, a mantra, or a smile. This latter is basically the TWIM method, which I had briefly tried but it didn't stick, smiles felt fake in my face and felt also like I was introducing unecessary body tension. Jhourney is generalized TWIM in a way, only that people are allowed to choose whatever works for them most naturally. For me, it was a memory of my girlfriend beind happy. But eventually, once one has more practice, other things may work. Jhourney warns meditators to not switch the scaffolding all the time, becaue the point is not to focus on the scaffolding but rather the sensation it induces.

## Where did the Pali go?!

I wrote above 'open hearted sensation'; Jhourney is not a Buddhist retreat and the amount of [Pali](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/glossary.html) I encountered was zero. But basically 'open hearted sensation' could be swapped for any of the [brahmaviharas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahmavihara). Whereas many methods suggest loving-kindness (_metta_) to achieve jhana, I entered jhana through empathetic joy (_mudita_) at remembering how happy my girlfriend was. Though that's not quite it: As does [Leigh Brassington](https://www.leighb.com/jhana3.htm), in practice Jhourney seems ok with any pleasant sensation, wherever it arises.

Before getting there one has to develop a reasonable level of what Jhourney calls _collectedness_. I had never come across that word in a meditation context; they explained it is like concentration but not single pointed. Ok I thought so this is not _ekagatta_ but rather more generically _upacara samadhi_ or perhaps _sati_ or mindfulness; though mindfulness can also mean other things. Turns out that the West doesn't quite have the exact terms one would need to point to what has to point to to guide meditation, so one has to choose between turning into a [Pali-chanting](https://x.com/ArtirKel/status/1914711613598523512) [corncob](https://x.com/dril/status/134787490526658561) or using somewhat novel words, and Jhourney took the latter approach.

More specifically Jhourney asks students to focus on their object of meditation while keeping awareness of everything else, ie there's an explicit instruction _against_ ekagatta. I wonder if this is to avoid students from trying too hard to focus _only_ on the object of meditation, which would be far far harder and not required to reach the jhanic states they teach.

## To jhana you have to not want to jhana

It is a deep learning in life that there are things that to get them, you have to not want them. Or rather, crave them. You have to let go of jhana, to be actually ok with not jhanaing during the retreat. The jhanas feel like you get a thumbs up from the universe if you have unblocked the relevant emotional blockages that you are going in the right direction.

How to jhana then? The answer is simple enough: set the intention that you do want to jhana when stepping into the container and once within it, let it go, just allow it to happen. Instead, convince yourself that if you sit for enough hours (I was meditating for more than 8 hours a day) and you approach each moment with curiosity and enjoyment, you will jhana. In turn you can do this either by:

-   Noting that people before you did that exact same thing and got results or, if you can't just trust other people or think you have alien brain chemistry then:
-   Think of the jhanas as that thumbs up and make your goal to be full-sending every moment of your experience, make the goal to become an enjoyer of things whatever might come. Jhourney calls this 'conductivity' (how much of your experience you are accepting)
    -   This was easy for me, someone that has literally 'enjoyer of things' for a Twitter tagline, who honestly believes anything can be enjoyed, and who is never bored.
    -   If you are a person prone to boredom, the (attempting of the) jhanas will fix you

This again is something Jhourney emphasizes, in their instruction handbook literally opens with _the real value of jhana is not the jhanas themselves; it's making it easier to be the person you aspire to be_

## Choose your own adventure

As mentioned earlier Jhourney allows each participant to choose their own way to get to jhana. There is a structure to the retreat:

-   Two group sits (morning and night) of around 1hr
-   1:1 interviews with facilitators and office hours to get feedback
-   Some breathwork sessions
-   Some time allocated for reading relevant sections of the manual
-   And plenty of solo meditation time

But you can do whatever you want of course; and the retreat format seems to get tweaked from retreat to retreat; we had breathwork facilitated by [Jonny Miller](https://www.jonnymiller.co/) who teaches FBR (a subtype of [CCB](https://breathemagazine.com.au/wellbeing/a-basic-guide-to-conscious-connected-breathing/)); in an earlier retreat they taught Wim Hof breathing per another participant.

The facilitators were not pushy at all, they were genuinely supportive of my goals and approach, offering helpful tips, or just someone to talk to (it's a silent retreat, and having someone to excitedly talk to about one's experiences is great). At some point after I had achieved J1-3 I had a deep "fuck the jhanas! I'm done! I'm going to just vibe-meditate" moment where I decided to stop using the suggested practice and "do" Do Nothing meditation. That was great, it felt less effortful and more enjoyable and without me "doing anything" it got me up to J7. it felt like chillin' on a mental sofa watching a movie, or lying on grass watching the clouds.

## Radical non-self coercion and a holistic view of emotions: key life hacks

If there's something that I did differently in the retreat is to impose myself less upon my mind and my body (weird as that may seem).

If I felt like peeing, I would break the sit and pee, if my head hurted from meditating, I would stop. If I was restless I would just go for a walk. I want to remark that there's a bad and a good way of doing this. These actions felt like I was taking with my whole being as opposed to a "part bored with meditating that wanted to go to the beach". I did pause to mindfully consider if I _really_ wanted to do those things and _allowed_ internal consensus to build up.

Most importantly, I learned to notice that the way I was trying to avoid being distracted was to _violently push my thoughts away!_. I thought I was being gentle but no! I noticed saying "Present moment!!" upon noticing a distraction, like being angry at the thought that arose or my mind for not being in the present moment. Upon noticing this, I said ok, well these thoughts have good intentions for me, what do they want? So I interacted with those thoughts (I recommend reading Wholeness Work, Core Transformation, or Gendlin's Focusing for more on this), hugged them, forgived them for not understanding that they were distracting me, and getting them to understand that their purpose would be better served by going away for the moment. For example thoughts related about planning for the future (like this blogpost) I said "If you guys interrupt less then I get to go deeper into the jhanas and have more interesting things to say in the blogpost, and you want a good blog post, right?". Good point, and they poof'd away.

Importantly, I _trusted_ that the thoughts would go away but was deeply okay if they came back. I would just lovingly and repeatedly do the same thing until I grew less distracted.

Another thing that I think helped me was to view emotions holistically, ie neither cognitive states nor feelings in the body. Rather an emotion is a state that has many components: dispositions to actions, physiological responses, recurrent thoughts, altered attention, etc.

For example if I let my mind be in J5, without doing much the following happens:

1.  My mouth opens as if wanting to say "woaaah"
2.  Images of vast blue skies show up in my mind's eye along with a sphere centered in myself expanding
3.  A sense that the outbreath is "like this (state)"
4.  A sense that everything is possible

These come up without "doing anything". But to truly understand the jhanas one should try to investigate them more directly. Alas, jhourney is mostly focused on getting you to experience the jhanas (Which is already a feat!), not to do _insight_ meditation in the Buddhist world. Jhourney is essentially about "wet" (pleasant) concentration; whereas say the Goenka vipassana ("dry" insight) retreat (which I have not done) are mainly about insight. Insight here being the path that takes you to awakening or enlightenment, which sounds intriguing.

But because jhourney does point students to cultivate curiosity one ends up doing some investigating anyway. "Hmm I wonder what happens if I ask who am I while in J6?" or "Where does my body end?" or "If I imagine a pizza, do i crave it". At some point the felt sense of "is the arm rest of the chair I'm sitting in part of me" was "unclear" which made me go "huh, intriguing". This is quite a lighthearted fun way to get into insight!

The fun thing is that whereas "dry insight" has a reputation of being "hardcore", it may well be that the jhana path to enlightenment may allow one to relax into awakening more joyfully and faster.

## Belief changes

Did I change any of my core beliefs by doing this? I don't think so! The content of my beliefs remains unchanged, but the way I experience them has changed. There were things that I believed intellectually but didn't really feel in my bones. For example, if you had asked me over the last 10 years the question "Fundamentally, is everything basically the same thing" I would have said "Yes, it's all some kind of atom-and-awareness type stuff, [protopanpsychism](https://nintil.com/consciousness-and-its-discontents) or something adjacent is real". I've even written about this here at Nintil, before doing any meditation and without having taken any of the substances that might make one think such thoughts, I got there natty, unassisted. But now I see the truth of it, just like an optical illusion where for an instance you can see it "right" as opposed to the mistaken impression.

I do now think meditation is more powerful than I thought. It delivers. The stuff is real. Yes you can install software upgrades to your brain. Everyone is comparatively depressed compared to the advanced meditators.

Another interesting thing is that I noticed that with meditation the mind becomes pliable in ways one has to watch out for. Before the retreat, if someone had talked about say the truth of astrology I would have felt a very knee-jerky "that's bullshit!" reaction. But now that's gone. Now I'm closer to "huh intriguing, how did this person come to believe this \[clearly wrong thing\]?". This is similar to the mindset that the Art of Accomplishment's VIEW (Vulnerability, Impartiality, Empathy, Wonder) aims to teach. Fortunately, I don't rely on the knee-jerky reaction to make sense of the world, I have reason and science; however I can see how someone less vigilant than me could fall into a turbo-woo hole if they meditate without solid epistemics. The same is true of psychedelics. But meditation may be riskier because it seems natural, there's no substance involved, and while it's true that you can kill a meditation "trip" immediately and that's less true for psychedelics, the harm may be in not noticing what it's doing to the way you relate to things like truth and morality.

## Tips to jhana: what I wished I had known

This will probably work best if you're like me, though I am also incorporating here materials that my recent-past self that was much less embodied would have found useful

-   Look into Gendlin's Focusing, Core Transformation, and Wholeness work to understand your emotions better
-   Practice Yoga Nidra and the somatic meditations in Somatic Descent, do body scans
-   Investigate the nature of your "pushing away thoughts" move to see if there's any coercion. Even if you think there isn't, just consider you might be wrong.
-   Lying down is fine for meditation, if you don't fall asleep
-   You can run mental experiments within a jhana (how are your intuitions/mental state altered) to test the vibe of the jhana
-   You will not necessarily enter jhana at J1
-   Setting an intention pre-sit and then letting it go within the sit helps. Think of dancing: you set the intention to dance but once on the dancefloor you are not micromanaging every little move, you allow the dance to happen.
-   Chill the fuck out by any means necessary. Visualize being pancake batter spreading onto the floor.

## What the Jhanas feel like

Here is what I experienced, along with what other people have reported, in their own words. This video of what the Jhanas are supposed to feel like roughly matches my [experience](https://x.com/RogerThisdell/status/1882779644635513072).

All the jhanas shared (for me) some properties:

-   They are absorbing. Most of my awareness is dominated by the jhana.
-   They were self-sustaining states. That is, they feel like they could go on forever in the sense that you could be lying down on a bed forever. But you can end them at will.
-   One can shift between them
-   It's possible to have thoughts, hear sounds, imagine stuff, as normal while in a jhana. There's an altered mental state comparable to eg what's reported for MDMA.
-   They seem to be discrete states. I did not experience something I'd call a J1.5.
-   These experiences are roughly aligned with what people have called the "sutta jhanas (as in eg. what [TWIM](https://www.dhammasukha.org/getting-started-with-twim) teaches)" or the "[soft](https://www.mctb.org/mctb2/table-of-contents/part-iii-the-samatha-jhanas/27-the-concentration-states-shamatha-jhanas/) jhanas"; as opposed to the "hard jhanas" or "Visuddhimagga jhanas" which are more engrossing. Jhourney claims to teach these soft jhanas and they do deliver on that.
-   There seems to be some natural ordering to them. The progression happened in the order prescribed, with the lower jhanas happening before the latter formless jhanas.
-   It's possible to pop into a jhana directly from non-jhana, no need to go in order. I in fact first got into J2 and it took me a while to get to J1. Later in the retreat I was popping into J4 and J5 straight away without the warm fuzzies of the lower jhanas.
-   On their own, they do not make one aware of the three Buddhist [marks of existence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_marks_of_existence) (sufering, impermanence, no-self) (As [Ingram](https://www.mctb.org/mctb2/table-of-contents/part-iii-the-samatha-jhanas/28-the-formless-realms/) points out). I still feel I have a self, I don't feel like everything is impermanent.

Some of my description may sound too allegorical but it's quite hard to not be; I hope you agree with me that it's easier to get my meaning across if I say "it's like a rave" than if I say "a sense of euphoria". You can go to a rave and be like "ah yes this is what he meant" whereas "a warm buzzy sensation combined with a higher likelihood of wanting to open your mouth to scream wahooo, a desire to jump and open your arms etc" is a hopeless description by contrast.
