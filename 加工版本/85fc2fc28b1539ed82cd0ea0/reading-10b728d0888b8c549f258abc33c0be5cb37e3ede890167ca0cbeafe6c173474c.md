# The Milanovician Master Argument: You can't care about poverty, only about inequality?

---

## 正文

反对 full egalitarianism 的 standard argument 是 Levelling Down Objection (LDO)。如果有一个支持 egalitarianism 的 argument，能把 LDO 反过来用，那会怎样？

深思熟虑的人通常会 asserted，重要的是 poverty，而不是 inequality。Inequality 只有在它 engenders bad consequences 时才重要，正如一些 researchers 试图 show 的那样。到目前为止，我仍不相信那些最强的 claims（抱歉，Pickett & Wilkinson）。

但 Branko Milanovic 在[这里](https://glineq.blogspot.com/2015/08/all-our-needs-are-social.html) 提出了一个 argument，主张你实际上不可能关心 poverty，只能关心 inequality。Poverty 会是一个 confused idea。Relative poverty 就是 inequality，而 absolute poverty 则说不通。为什么？

关键的 idea：我们的 needs 是 social 的。

LDO 指出，full egalitarianism 要求我们 destroy wealth 来 pursue equality。一个有 1 个 poor 和 1000 个 rich 的世界，会比一个有 1001 个 poor 的世界更糟。

Milanovic 的 argument 会按如下方式展开：

1.  令 absolute poverty 为某些 basic needs 的 existence
2.  令 relative poverty，即 inequality，为 resource distribution 中的任何 differential
3.  Assume needs 完全 depend on 一个 social context。也就是说，人们想要什么，depend 于别人想要什么，或拥有什么。
4.  由 (1,3)，it follows that absolute poverty 是 society-relative 的。（例如，从 Hunter-gatherers 自己的角度看，他们并不 poor。）
5.  Assume 人们的 needs 随 societal wealth 或 reference group 的 societal wealth 而 increase
6.  如果属于 rich 的 societal resources 被 destroyed，那么 inequality 和 societal wealth 就会 decreases
7.  如果是这样，根据 (5)，人们会有更少的 needs，并且根据 (3)，他们会将自身与新的 societal standards for needs 进行 compare，而这些 standards 将被 reduced。
8.  **因此，根据 (4)，通过 destroying rich 的 resources，absolute poverty 必然会 decrease。**
9.  Assume income inequality 与 perceived 和 unmet needs 之间更大的 differences 之间存在 positive correlation
10.  **因此，根据 (1,3,9)，income inquality 会 produces unhappiness（如果 happiness 是通过 need satisfaction 而 obtained 的）**
11.  **因此，根据 (3,4)，needs satisfaction 或 happiness 不会随 increased wealth 而 increase。**
12.  Reducing inequality 会 increases happiness（根据 10），而 reducing absolute poverty 只有在它 reduces inequality 的限度内才会如此（根据 1,3）
13.  **因此，如果你关心 need satisfaction 或 happiness，你就应该只关心 inequality，而不是 poverty。**
14.  **因此，destroying wealth 只要能 reduces inequality，就会 increases needs satisfaction 和 happiness**
15.  **因此，LDO fails。Increasing happiness/needs satisfaction 与 destroying rich 的 wealth 是 consistent with 的**。

我认为这个 argument 有很多漏洞，包括一些必须 checked 的 empirical assertions。我把它放在这里，供你评论，或留待将来某个时候进行 debunking。

补记：这个 argument 已被 debunked。人们的 [absolute income 确实 mater](https://80000hours.org/articles/everything-you-need-to-know-about-whether-money-makes-you-happy/)（relative income 也是如此）。因此，premise 3 似乎是 dubious 的。人们确实有 absolute satisfaction 的感受。poverty 这个 concept 仍然说得通，而主张不关心 inequality、而关心 poverty 的 arguments 仍然 valid。

## 原文

The standard argument against full egalitarianism is the Levelling Down Objection (LDO). What if there is an argument for egalitarianism that turns the LDO on its head?

It is usually asserted by thoughtful people that what matters is poverty, not inequality. Inequality would only matter only as far as it engenders bad consequences, as some researchers have tried to show. So far I'm unconvinced of the strongest claims (Sorry, Pickett & Wilkinson).

But Branko Milanovic presented [here](https://glineq.blogspot.com/2015/08/all-our-needs-are-social.html) an argument defending that you can't actually care about poverty, only inequality. Poverty would be a confused idea. Relative poverty is inequality, and absolute poverty doesn't make sense. Why?

The key idea: our needs are social.

The LDO states that full egalitarianism requires us to destroy wealth to pursue equality. A world with 1 poor and 1000 rich would be worse than a world with 1001 poor people.

Milanovic's would proceed as follows:

1.  Let absolute poverty be the existence of certain basic needs
2.  Let relative poverty, or inequality, be any differential in the resource distribution
3.  Assume needs fully depend on a social context. That is, what peole want depend on what others want, or have.
4.  From (1,3), it follows that absolute poverty is society-relative. (e.g. Hunter-gatherers weren't poor from their point of view.)
5.  Assume people's needs increase with societal wealth, or reference group's societal wealth
6.  If societal resources belonging to the rich are destroyed, inequality and societal wealth decreases
7.  If so, by (5), people will have less needs, and by (3) they will compare themselves to the new societal standards for needs, that will be reduced.
8.  **Therefore, by (4), absolute poverty will necessarily decrease by destroying the rich's resources.**
9.  Assume there is a positive correlation between income inequality and greater differences between perceived and unmet needs
10.  **Therefore, by (1,3,9), income inquality produces unhappiness (if happiness is obtained via need satisfaction)**
11.  **Therefore, by (3,4), needs satisfaction or happiness does not increase with increased wealth.**
12.  Reducing inequality increases happiness (by 10), and reducing absolute poverty does only so insofar as it reduces inequality (by 1,3)
13.  **Therefore, if you care about need satisfaction or happiness, you should only care about inequality, not poverty.**
14.  **Therefore, destroying wealth, as long as doing so reduces inequality, increases needs satisfaction and happiness**
15.  **Therefore, the LDO fails. Increasing happiness/needs satisfaction is consistent with destroying the rich's wealth**.

I think there are plenty of holes in this argument, including some empirical assertions that have to be checked. I leave it here for you to comment on, or for future debunking at some point.

EDIT: The argument, debunked. People's [absolute income does mater](https://80000hours.org/articles/everything-you-need-to-know-about-whether-money-makes-you-happy/) (as does relative income). Therefore, premise 3 seems dubious. People do have a sense of absolute satisfaction. The concept of poverty still makes sense, and the arguments for not caring about inequality, but about poverty remain valid.
