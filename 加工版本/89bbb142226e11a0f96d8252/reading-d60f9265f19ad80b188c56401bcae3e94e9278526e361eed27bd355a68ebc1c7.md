# 我明年的计划

## 正文

今天是我的生日。过去二十年来，每年我都会借这个机会，随心写一篇私人文章，回顾自己的生活，以及对未来的打算。

距离我的 [Foundations](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/myprojects/foundations-project/) project 已经过去两年，我很高兴，在当时投入精力的大多数方面，我都保持得还不错。

我仍然几乎每天都 exercise。虽然今年没有 run a marathon，但我确实完成了一场从 sea level 一路到当地一座山的山顶的 [race](https://www.grousemountain.com/seek-the-peak)（16 km，1400 m 的 elevation gain）。在 eating、sleep、reading 和与朋友保持联系方面，我也一直相当有规律。在 organization 方面，我有些退步，不过考虑到自己在这方面的起点很低，即使还需要多尝试几次才能坚持下来，我也不会感到意外。

今年最大的 project，是筹备我的 course [Everyday Energy](https://join.ingeniumcourses.com/everydayenergy/) 的第一期。为这个 project 做 research 很有趣。我一直对 motivation、productivity、health 和 well-being 之间的 connections 很感兴趣，因此，为这门 course 制作 75+ 节 lessons，给了我一个理由，去深入了解许多原本可能错过的 topics。

## 我明年的计划

我明年最大的 learning project，是尝试为 [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ScottHYoungVid) 制作更多（也更好的）videos。

我一直喜欢 video content，尤其是涉及 educational topics 的内容。[3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown)、[Veritasium](https://www.youtube.com/@veritasium) 和 [World War Two](https://www.youtube.com/@WorldWarTwo) 这样的 Channels，作品都非常出色，既 entertaining，又 intellectually rigorous。   到目前为止，我自己对制作 videos 的投入却一直有些三心二意。Video production 比写作耗时得多，所以我始终没有真正下定决心，去攀登 skill curve，做出让我真正满意的 content。因此，尽管我的 [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ScottHYoungVid/videos) 已经开了十多年，作为 video creator，我的创作却一直断断续续。

不过，online ecosystem 整体向 video 的 shift，让我觉得，今年投入精力去 learning 这种 medium，制作我想做的 videos，是值得的。

你可以在这里看看我最近制作的两个 videos：

-   [如何每周读一本书（即使你读得很慢）](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q6p2aghAAms)
-   [你 Procrastinate 的真正原因（Scientific Explanation，即科学解释）](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RNBuuEyse8g)

如果比起 videos，你更喜欢读文章，也不用担心，我不会停止在这里写作。不过，我会尝试通过 newsletter 分享一些自己制作的 videos。

## 个人目标

工作之外，我主要想继续深化最初那个 project 中着手建立的一些 foundations。  Health 和 fitness 方面进展顺利，不过我可能还想再参加一场 race。我不确定今年是否有时间为 full marathon 进行 train，但参加一场 half-distance race 或 triathlon 可能会很有趣。

在 reading 方面，如果时间允许，我打算尝试涉猎更广一些。我经常需要为某个 project 做 research，这就限制了我的 reading list，所以，如果今年能多读一些不打算直接用于写作的书，会是一件好事。不过，考虑到我整体上会专注于 video creation，我（当然）也买了几本介绍 film 的综合性书籍，来帮助 learning。

Outreach 是另一个我在最初的 Foundations project 中意识到自己做得比较薄弱的方面。明年的一个优先事项，是为朋友腾出时间，即使这有时让人觉得不那么 productive。AI 的出现提醒了我，真实的人际关系能带来多大的价值——不应该忽视它们。

而且，一如既往，正是我与你这样的读者之间的关系，让我能够以此为生。我写作至今已经二十多年了，能有机会与你分享自己学到的东西，我深怀感激。

谢谢你又陪伴了我一年！

## 原文

Today is my birthday. Every year, for the last two decades, I’ve used this opportunity to indulge in a personal post reflecting on my life and plans for the future.

Two years out from my [Foundations](https://www.scotthyoung.com/blog/myprojects/foundations-project/) project, I’m happy that, for most of the areas I worked on, things have stayed relatively strong.

I still exercise nearly every day. And while I didn’t run a marathon this year, I did finish a [race](https://www.grousemountain.com/seek-the-peak) going from sea level to the top of a local mountain (16 km, 1400 m of elevation gain). I’ve also been fairly consistent with eating, sleep, reading and keeping up with friends. I’ve had some backsliding on organization, but given my low starting point there, I wouldn’t be surprised if it takes a few more attempts before that one sticks.

The big project of the year was putting together the first session of my course, [Everyday Energy](https://join.ingeniumcourses.com/everydayenergy/). This was a fun project to research. I’ve always had a deep interest in the connections between motivation, productivity, health and well-being, so creating 75+ lessons for the course gave me an excuse to dive deep into a lot of topics I might have otherwise missed.

## My Plans for Next Year

The big learning project I have for next year is trying to create more (and better) videos for [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ScottHYoungVid).

I’ve always enjoyed video content, especially on educational topics. Channels like [3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown), [Veritasium](https://www.youtube.com/@veritasium) and [World War Two](https://www.youtube.com/@WorldWarTwo) do amazing work that manages to be both entertaining and intellectually rigorous.   So far, my own commitment to creating videos has always been somewhat half-hearted. Video production is a lot more time-consuming than writing, and so I never committed myself to climbing the skill curve to make content I was really happy with. So, even though I’ve had a [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ScottHYoungVid/videos) for over a decade, I’ve been inconsistent as a video creator.

However, the overall shift of the online ecosystem towards video makes me feel like the commitment to learning the medium and creating the videos that I’d like to make this year is justified.

You can check out two of the most recent videos I’ve done here:

-   [How To Read One Book Per Week (Even If You Read Slowly)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q6p2aghAAms)
-   [The Real Reason You Procrastinate (Scientific Explanation)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RNBuuEyse8g)

If you prefer written essays to videos, don’t worry, I’m not going to stop writing here. But I will try to share some of the videos I make on the newsletter.

## Personal Goals

Outside of work, my main focus is continuing to deepen some of the foundations I worked on in my initial project.   Health and fitness are going smoothly, but I might like to enter another race. I’m not sure I’ll have time to train for a full marathon this year, but a half-distance race or a triathlon could be fun.

For reading, I’m going to try to cast a wider net if time allows. Often I’m doing research for a project which narrows my reading list, so it would be nice to use this year to read more books that aren’t intended to make their way into my writing directly. Still, given my overall focus on video creation, I’ve (of course) bought a few books on film in general to help with learning.

Outreach was another area I realized had been somewhat weak during my initial Foundations project. A priority for the next year is to make time for friends, even if it sometimes feels less productive. The emergence of AI has served as a reminder of how much value flows through real relationships—they shouldn’t be neglected.

And, as always, it’s the relationships that I have with readers like you that make what I do for a living possible. I’ve been writing for over twenty years now, and I’m enormously grateful that I have the opportunity to share what I learn with you.

Thanks for sticking with me for another year!
