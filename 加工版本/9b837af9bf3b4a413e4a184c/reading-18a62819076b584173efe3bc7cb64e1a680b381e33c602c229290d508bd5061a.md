## 原文

# 盘点数学里的各种反演

数学学到现在，也见过了好多的反演公式和定理，本文就来总结一下吧。

## 二项反演

最简单的数列反演应该就是二项反演了，即

> 定理：对于数列 ![a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n) 和 ![b\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=b_n) ，若如下两个公式的其中一个成立，则另一个也成立  
> ![\\begin{align} a\_n=\\sum\_{k=0}^nC\_n^k b\_k\\quad b\_n=\\sum\_{k=0}^n(-1)^kC\_n^ka\_k \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+a_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5EnC_n%5Ek+b_k%5Cquad+b_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5En%28-1%29%5EkC_n%5Eka_k+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

（其中 ![C\_n^k](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=C_n%5Ek) 是二项式系数）

证明的话，只需把其中一个式子代入到另一个就行了。然而这种证明并没有给我们带来任何的启发，所以本文将给出一个不太常规的证明。

我们定义多项式空间上的两个线性泛函 ![A](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A) 和 ![B](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=B) 如下

![\\begin{align} A\[x^n\]=a\_n\\quad B\[x^n\]=b\_n  \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+A%5Bx%5En%5D%3Da_n%5Cquad+B%5Bx%5En%5D%3Db_n++%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

那么反演公式中的 ![\\begin{align} a\_n&=\\sum\_{k=0}^nC\_n^k b\_k\\\\  \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+a_n%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5EnC_n%5Ek+b_k%5C%5C++%5Cend%7Balign%7D) 便可重新写作

![\\begin{align} A\[x^n\]&=\\sum\_{k=0}^nC\_n^kB\[x^n\]=B\\left\[\\sum\_{k=0}^nC\_n^kx^n\\right\]\\\\ &=B\[(x+1)^n\]\\\\ \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+A%5Bx%5En%5D%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5EnC_n%5EkB%5Bx%5En%5D%3DB%5Cleft%5B%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5EnC_n%5Ekx%5En%5Cright%5D%5C%5C+%26%3DB%5B%28x%2B1%29%5En%5D%5C%5C+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

于是再定义多项式空间上的线性变换 ![E](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=E) 为 ![E(x^n)=(x+1)^n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=E%28x%5En%29%3D%28x%2B1%29%5En) ，代入到上式

![A\[x^n\]=B\[E(x^n)\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A%5Bx%5En%5D%3DB%5BE%28x%5En%29%5D)

该式说明在多项式空间上，线性泛函 ![A](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A) 和 ![B\\circ E](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=B%5Ccirc+E) 是等价的，于是我们将这两个泛函同时作用到多项式 ![(x-1)^n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28x-1%29%5En) ，就能得到

![\\begin{align} A\[(x-1)^n\]=B\[x^n\] \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+A%5B%28x-1%29%5En%5D%3DB%5Bx%5En%5D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

而该式等价于 ![\\begin{align}  b\_n&=\\sum\_{k=0}^n(-1)^kC\_n^ka\_k \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D++b_n%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5En%28-1%29%5EkC_n%5Eka_k+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，如此便证明了二项反演（其实只证明了一半，但另一半是同理的）

## 斯特林反演

与二项反演类似，斯特林反演是说如下两式是等价的。

![\\begin{align} a\_n=\\sum\_{k=0}^n s(n,k) b\_k  \\quad  b\_n=\\sum\_{k=0}^n S(n,k)  a\_k  \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+a_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5En+s%28n%2Ck%29+b_k++%5Cquad++b_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5En+S%28n%2Ck%29++a_k++%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

其中， ![s(n,k)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=s%28n%2Ck%29) 是有符号第一类斯特林数， ![S(n,k)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=S%28n%2Ck%29) 是无符号第二类斯特林数，它们的生成函数为

![\\begin{align} (x)\_n=\\sum\_{k=0}^ns(n,k)x^k\\quad x^n=\\sum\_{k=0}^nS(n,k)(x)\_k \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%28x%29_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5Ens%28n%2Ck%29x%5Ek%5Cquad+x%5En%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5EnS%28n%2Ck%29%28x%29_k+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

其中 ![(x)\_k=x(x-1)(x-2)\\cdots(x-k+1)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28x%29_k%3Dx%28x-1%29%28x-2%29%5Ccdots%28x-k%2B1%29) 被称为 ![x](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x) 的 ![k](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=k) 次下降幂。

斯特林反演的证明与前文同理，应用线性泛函 ![A](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A) 和 ![B](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=B) ，可将 ![\\begin{align} a\_n&=\\sum\_{k=0}^n s(n,k) b\_k  \\\\   \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+a_n%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5En+s%28n%2Ck%29+b_k++%5C%5C+++%5Cend%7Balign%7D) 写作

![\\begin{align} A\[x^n\]&=\\sum\_{k=0}^n s(n,k) B\[x^k\]\\\\ &=B\\left\[\\sum\_{k=0}^ns(n,k)(x)\_k\\right\]\\\\ &=B\[(x)\_n\] \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+A%5Bx%5En%5D%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5En+s%28n%2Ck%29+B%5Bx%5Ek%5D%5C%5C+%26%3DB%5Cleft%5B%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5Ens%28n%2Ck%29%28x%29_k%5Cright%5D%5C%5C+%26%3DB%5B%28x%29_n%5D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

于是定义多项式空间上的线性变换 ![\\phi](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cphi) 为 ![\\phi(x^n)=(x)\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cphi%28x%5En%29%3D%28x%29_n) ，那么上式可写作

![A\[x^n\]=B\[\\phi(x^n)\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A%5Bx%5En%5D%3DB%5B%5Cphi%28x%5En%29%5D)

这说明线性泛函 ![A](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A) 和 ![B\\circ\\phi](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=B%5Ccirc%5Cphi) 是等价的，我们将它们同时作用到多项式 ![\\begin{align} \\sum\_{k=0}^n S(n,k)x^k \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5En+S%28n%2Ck%29x%5Ek+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，可得

![\\begin{align} A\\left\[\\sum\_{k=0}^nS(n,k)x^k\\right\]&=B\\left\[\\sum\_{k=0}^nS(n,k)\\phi(x^k)\\right\]\\\\ \\sum\_{k=0}^nS(n,k)A\[x^n\]&=B\\left\[\\sum\_{k=0}^nS(n,k)(x)\_k\\right\]=B\[x^n\]\\\\ \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+A%5Cleft%5B%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5EnS%28n%2Ck%29x%5Ek%5Cright%5D%26%3DB%5Cleft%5B%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5EnS%28n%2Ck%29%5Cphi%28x%5Ek%29%5Cright%5D%5C%5C+%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5EnS%28n%2Ck%29A%5Bx%5En%5D%26%3DB%5Cleft%5B%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5EnS%28n%2Ck%29%28x%29_k%5Cright%5D%3DB%5Bx%5En%5D%5C%5C+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

即 ![\\begin{align} \\sum\_{k=0}^n S(n,k)  a\_k =b\_n \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Csum_%7Bk%3D0%7D%5En+S%28n%2Ck%29++a_k+%3Db_n+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，如此便证明了斯特林反演。

证明上述两个反演所用到的技巧常被称为哑运算，关于哑运算的其他妙用可见于

[等幂求和（补）：哑运算的种种妙用](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/713910932)

## 莫比乌斯反演

假设数列 ![a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n) 和 ![b\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=b_n) 满足 ![\\begin{align} a\_n=\\sum\_{k|n}b\_k \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+a_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%7Cn%7Db_k+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，其中 ![k|n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=k%7Cn) 表示 ![k](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=k) 整除 ![n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=n) ，该求和遍历所有 ![n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=n) 的因子 ![k](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=k)

我们该如何从数列 ![a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n) 反演出数列 ![b\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=b_n) 呢？

为解决该问题，我们定义数列的狄利克雷生成函数如下

![\\begin{align} A(s)=\\sum\_{n=1}^\\infty \\frac{a\_n}{n^s} \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+A%28s%29%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%3D1%7D%5E%5Cinfty+%5Cfrac%7Ba_n%7D%7Bn%5Es%7D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

然后再定义两个数列的卷积运算如下

![\\begin{align} (a\*b)\_n=\\sum\_{k|n}a\_kb\_{n/k} \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%28a%2Ab%29_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%7Cn%7Da_kb_%7Bn%2Fk%7D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

设 ![A(s)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A%28s%29) 是数列 ![a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n) 的生成函数， ![B(s)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=B%28s%29) 是数列 ![b\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=b_n) 的生成函数，不难证明

![\\begin{align} A(s)B(s)=\\sum\_{n=1}^\\infty\\frac{(a\*b)\_n}{n^s} \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+A%28s%29B%28s%29%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%3D1%7D%5E%5Cinfty%5Cfrac%7B%28a%2Ab%29_n%7D%7Bn%5Es%7D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

即是说，两个数列做卷积，等价于它们的生成函数相乘。

而前文的 ![\\begin{align} a\_n=\\sum\_{k|n}b\_k \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+a_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%7Cn%7Db_k+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) 可看作数列 ![b\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=b_n) 与常数列 ![1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=1) 做卷积，即

![a\_n=(b\*1)\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n%3D%28b%2A1%29_n)

我们把常数列 ![1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=1) 的生成函数记作 ![\\zeta(s)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Czeta%28s%29) ，则上式等价于

![A(s)=B(s)\\zeta(s)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A%28s%29%3DB%28s%29%5Czeta%28s%29)

其中 ![\\begin{align} \\zeta(s)=\\sum\_{n=1}^\\infty\\frac1{n^s} \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Czeta%28s%29%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%3D1%7D%5E%5Cinfty%5Cfrac1%7Bn%5Es%7D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) 也被称为黎曼函数。

于是有 ![B(s)=A(s)/\\zeta(s)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=B%28s%29%3DA%28s%29%2F%5Czeta%28s%29) ，不妨把 ![1/\\zeta(s)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=1%2F%5Czeta%28s%29) 生成的数列记为 ![\\mu\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu_n) ，则有 ![b\_n=(a\*\\mu)\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=b_n%3D%28a%2A%5Cmu%29_n)

所以我们只需求出 ![\\mu\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu_n) 就能反演出 ![a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n) 了。

为此，我们接着定义积性数列

> 定义：若数列 ![a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n) 满足，当 ![m,n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=m%2Cn) 互素时 ![a\_{mn}=a\_m\\cdot a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_%7Bmn%7D%3Da_m%5Ccdot+a_n) ，则称 ![a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n) 是积性的。

若 ![a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n) 是一个积性数列，则其生成函数具有如下形式

![\\begin{align} A(s)=\\prod\_p\\left(1+\\frac{a\_p}{p^s}+ \\frac{a\_{p^2}}{p^{2s}} +\\cdots \\right) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+A%28s%29%3D%5Cprod_p%5Cleft%281%2B%5Cfrac%7Ba_p%7D%7Bp%5Es%7D%2B+%5Cfrac%7Ba_%7Bp%5E2%7D%7D%7Bp%5E%7B2s%7D%7D+%2B%5Ccdots+%5Cright%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

上述无穷乘积遍历所有素数 ![p](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=p)

反过来，若一个数列的生成函数具有上述形式，则其必为积性数列，这并不难证明。

刚好常数列 ![1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=1) 就是一个积性数列，故其生成函数 ![\\zeta(s)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Czeta%28s%29) 可写作

![\\begin{align} \\zeta(s)&=\\prod\_p\\left(1+\\frac{1}{p^s}+ \\frac{1}{p^{2s}} +\\cdots\\right)\\\\ &=\\prod\_p\\frac{1}{1-p^s} \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Czeta%28s%29%26%3D%5Cprod_p%5Cleft%281%2B%5Cfrac%7B1%7D%7Bp%5Es%7D%2B+%5Cfrac%7B1%7D%7Bp%5E%7B2s%7D%7D+%2B%5Ccdots%5Cright%29%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Cprod_p%5Cfrac%7B1%7D%7B1-p%5Es%7D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

该式也被称为黎曼函数的欧拉无穷乘积公式。

对上式取倒数可得

![\\begin{align} \\frac1{\\zeta(s)} &=\\prod\_p(1-p^s) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cfrac1%7B%5Czeta%28s%29%7D+%26%3D%5Cprod_p%281-p%5Es%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

由此可知 ![\\mu\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu_n) 也是一个积性数列，并且

![\\mu\_1=1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu_1%3D1)

![\\mu\_p=-1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu_p%3D-1) ，其中 ![p](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=p) 为素数

![\\mu\_{p^n}=0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu_%7Bp%5En%7D%3D0) ，其中 ![p](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=p) 为素数且 ![n>1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=n%3E1)

如此我们便得到了莫比乌斯反演

> 定理：若 ![\\begin{align} a\_n=\\sum\_{k|n}b\_k \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+a_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%7Cn%7Db_k+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，则 ![\\begin{align} b\_n=\\sum\_{k|n}\\mu\_{k}a\_{n/k} \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+b_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%7Cn%7D%5Cmu_%7Bk%7Da_%7Bn%2Fk%7D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，其中数列 ![\\mu\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu_n) 的定义如上。

更一般地，若两个数列 ![\\alpha\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Calpha_n) 和 ![\\beta\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbeta_n) 的狄利克雷生成函数互为倒数，则如下两式是等价的

![\\begin{align} a\_n=\\sum\_{k|n}\\alpha\_{k}b\_{n/k}\\quad b\_n=\\sum\_{k|n}\\beta\_{k}a\_{n/k}\\\\  \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+a_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%7Cn%7D%5Calpha_%7Bk%7Db_%7Bn%2Fk%7D%5Cquad+b_n%3D%5Csum_%7Bk%7Cn%7D%5Cbeta_%7Bk%7Da_%7Bn%2Fk%7D%5C%5C++%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

将上述结论中的狄利克雷生成函数换成指数型生成函数即可得到前文的二项反演，读者自行尝试。

## 广义莫比乌斯反演

我们还可以将上述反演从数列推广到函数。

和前文同理，先来定义函数的“生成函数”，即

![\\begin{align} F(s)=\\int\_0^\\infty\\frac{f(x)}{x^{s+1}}{\\rm d}s \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+F%28s%29%3D%5Cint_0%5E%5Cinfty%5Cfrac%7Bf%28x%29%7D%7Bx%5E%7Bs%2B1%7D%7D%7B%5Crm+d%7Ds+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

然后将数列的生成函数与函数的生成函数相乘，得到

![\\begin{align} A(s)F(s)&=\\sum\_{n=1}^\\infty\\frac{a\_n}{n^s}\\int\_0^\\infty\\frac{f(x)}{x^{s+1}}{\\rm d}s\\\\ &=\\int\_0^\\infty\\sum\_{n=1}^\\infty\\frac{a\_nf(x)}{(nx)^{s+1}}{\\rm d}(nx)\\\\ &=\\int\_0^\\infty\\sum\_{n=1}^\\infty a\_nf( x/n)\\frac{{\\rm d}x\\ \\ \\ }{x^{s+1}} \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+A%28s%29F%28s%29%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%3D1%7D%5E%5Cinfty%5Cfrac%7Ba_n%7D%7Bn%5Es%7D%5Cint_0%5E%5Cinfty%5Cfrac%7Bf%28x%29%7D%7Bx%5E%7Bs%2B1%7D%7D%7B%5Crm+d%7Ds%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Cint_0%5E%5Cinfty%5Csum_%7Bn%3D1%7D%5E%5Cinfty%5Cfrac%7Ba_nf%28x%29%7D%7B%28nx%29%5E%7Bs%2B1%7D%7D%7B%5Crm+d%7D%28nx%29%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Cint_0%5E%5Cinfty%5Csum_%7Bn%3D1%7D%5E%5Cinfty+a_nf%28+x%2Fn%29%5Cfrac%7B%7B%5Crm+d%7Dx%5C+%5C+%5C+%7D%7Bx%5E%7Bs%2B1%7D%7D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

那么自然地，我们可将数列 ![a\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=a_n) 和函数 ![f(x)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28x%29) 的卷积定义为

![\\begin{align} (a\*f)(x)=\\sum\_{n=1}^\\infty a\_nf(x/n) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%28a%2Af%29%28x%29%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%3D1%7D%5E%5Cinfty+a_nf%28x%2Fn%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

然而这是个无穷级数，级数要比有限求和难处理得多，所以我们为 ![f](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f) 添加一个限制： ![f](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f) 在区间 ![(0,1)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%280%2C1%29) 上等于零。

有了该限制后，上述卷积便可写作

![\\begin{align} (a\*f)(x)=\\sum\_{n\\leqslant x} a\_nf(x/n) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%28a%2Af%29%28x%29%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%5Cleqslant+x%7D+a_nf%28x%2Fn%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

这样就得到一个有限求和了。同时可知，一个数列与一个函数做卷积，等价于它们的生成函数相乘。

于是我们能够得到函数的莫比乌斯反演定理

> 定理：对于定义在 ![\[1,\\infty)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5B1%2C%5Cinfty%29) 上的函数 ![f](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f) 和 ![g](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=g) ，若它们满足 ![\\begin{align} g(x)=\\sum\_{n\\leqslant x} f(x/n) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+g%28x%29%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%5Cleqslant+x%7D+f%28x%2Fn%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，则有  
> ![\\begin{align} f(x)=\\sum\_{n\\leqslant x}\\mu\_n g(x/n) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+f%28x%29%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%5Cleqslant+x%7D%5Cmu_n+g%28x%2Fn%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

更一般地，若数列 ![\\alpha\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Calpha_n) 和 ![\\beta\_n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbeta_n) 的生成函数互为倒数，则如下两式是等价的

![\\begin{align} g(x)=\\sum\_{n\\leqslant x}\\alpha\_n f(x/n)\\quad f(x)=\\sum\_{n\\leqslant x}\\beta\_n g(x/n) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+g%28x%29%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%5Cleqslant+x%7D%5Calpha_n+f%28x%2Fn%29%5Cquad+f%28x%29%3D%5Csum_%7Bn%5Cleqslant+x%7D%5Cbeta_n+g%28x%2Fn%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

还可以把莫比乌斯反演推广到偏序集上。

## 偏序集反演

我们甚至还能把莫比乌斯反演推广到偏序集上。

接下来会用到很多抽象代数的内容，如果读者没学过抽象代数的话，不放看看我写的抽象代数教程

[抽象代数（一）：抽象代数有多抽象？](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1901961789706568090)

对于一个偏序集 ![(P,\\leqslant)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28P%2C%5Cleqslant%29) ，我们将集合 ![\[x,y\]=\\{z\\in P:x\\leqslant z\\leqslant y\\}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Bx%2Cy%5D%3D%5C%7Bz%5Cin+P%3Ax%5Cleqslant+z%5Cleqslant+y%5C%7D) 称为 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上的一个区间。

若 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上的所有区间都是有限集，则称偏序集 ![(P,\\leqslant)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28P%2C%5Cleqslant%29) 是局部有限的。

设函数 ![f](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f) 将 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上的区间映射到一个数，我们称这样的函数为 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上的区间函数，并把 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上所有的区间函数组成的集合记作 ![I(P)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=I%28P%29) ，我们可以定义 ![I(P)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=I%28P%29) 上的加法和乘法如下

![\\begin{align} (f+g)(x,y)&=\\ f(x,y)+g(x,y)\\\\ (f\*g)(x,y)&=\\sum\_{x\\leqslant z\\leqslant y}f(x,z)g(z,y) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%28f%2Bg%29%28x%2Cy%29%26%3D%5C+f%28x%2Cy%29%2Bg%28x%2Cy%29%5C%5C+%28f%2Ag%29%28x%2Cy%29%26%3D%5Csum_%7Bx%5Cleqslant+z%5Cleqslant+y%7Df%28x%2Cz%29g%28z%2Cy%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

不难证明，上述定义的加法和乘法使得 ![I(P)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=I%28P%29) 构成一个环，且该环的乘法单位元为

![\\delta(x,y)=\\begin{cases} 1&x=y\\\\ 0&x\\ne y \\end{cases}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cdelta%28x%2Cy%29%3D%5Cbegin%7Bcases%7D+1%26x%3Dy%5C%5C+0%26x%5Cne+y+%5Cend%7Bcases%7D)

我们接着定义 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上的 ![\\zeta](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Czeta) 函数，即对任意 ![x\\leqslant y](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x%5Cleqslant+y) 都有 ![\\zeta(x,y)=1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Czeta%28x%2Cy%29%3D1)

我们将 ![\\zeta](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Czeta) 在 ![I(P)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=I%28P%29) 中的乘法逆元称为 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上的莫比乌斯函数，记作 ![\\mu](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu) ，于是有

![(g=f\*\\zeta)\\Leftrightarrow( f=g\*\\mu)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28g%3Df%2A%5Czeta%29%5CLeftrightarrow%28+f%3Dg%2A%5Cmu%29)

有莫比乌斯函数 ![\\mu](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu) 的定义可知，其应满足如下递推式

![\\begin{align} \\mu(y,y)&=1/{\\zeta(y,y)}=1\\\\  \\mu(x,y)&=-\\sum\_{x\\leqslant z<y} \\frac{\\zeta(z,y)}{\\zeta(y,y)} \\mu(x,z) =-\\sum\_{x\\leqslant z<y}\\mu(x,z) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cmu%28y%2Cy%29%26%3D1%2F%7B%5Czeta%28y%2Cy%29%7D%3D1%5C%5C++%5Cmu%28x%2Cy%29%26%3D-%5Csum_%7Bx%5Cleqslant+z%3Cy%7D+%5Cfrac%7B%5Czeta%28z%2Cy%29%7D%7B%5Czeta%28y%2Cy%29%7D+%5Cmu%28x%2Cz%29+%3D-%5Csum_%7Bx%5Cleqslant+z%3Cy%7D%5Cmu%28x%2Cz%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

然后再定义偏序集 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上的无穷区间 ![(-\\infty,x\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28-%5Cinfty%2Cx%5D) 为

![(-\\infty,x\]=\\{y\\in P:y\\leqslant x\\}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28-%5Cinfty%2Cx%5D%3D%5C%7By%5Cin+P%3Ay%5Cleqslant+x%5C%7D)

假设 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上的每个无穷区间 ![(-\\infty,x\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28-%5Cinfty%2Cx%5D) 都是有限的，则 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 上的区间函数可延拓到无穷区间，并且延拓之后 ![I(P)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=I%28P%29) 仍构成一个环。我们将 ![f(-\\infty,x)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28-%5Cinfty%2Cx%29) 简记为 ![f(x)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%28x%29) ，于是便能得到偏序集的莫比乌斯反演公式

> 定理：若 ![f,g](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=f%2Cg) 满足 ![\\begin{align} g(x)=\\sum\_{y\\leqslant x}f(y) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+g%28x%29%3D%5Csum_%7By%5Cleqslant+x%7Df%28y%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，则有 ![\\begin{align} f(x)=\\sum\_{y\\leqslant x}  g(y)\\mu(y,x) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+f%28x%29%3D%5Csum_%7By%5Cleqslant+x%7D++g%28y%29%5Cmu%28y%2Cx%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

正整数集在整除关系下构成一个偏序集，然而其莫比乌斯函数难以计算。所以我们接着扩展，对于偏序集 ![P\_1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P_1) 和 ![P\_2](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P_2) ，我们可在它们的笛卡尔积 ![P\_1\\times P\_2](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P_1%5Ctimes+P_2) 上定义一个偏序关系为

![(x\_1,x\_2)\\leqslant(y\_1,y\_2)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28x_1%2Cx_2%29%5Cleqslant%28y_1%2Cy_2%29) 当且仅当 ![x\_1\\leqslant y\_1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x_1%5Cleqslant+y_1) 且 ![x\_2\\leqslant y\_2](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x_2%5Cleqslant+y_2)

同理可定义多个偏序集的积。

显然有

![\\begin{align} \\delta((x\_1,y\_1),(x\_2,y\_2))&=\\delta\_1(x\_1,x\_2)\\delta\_2(y\_1,y\_2)\\\\ \\zeta((x\_1,y\_1),(x\_2,y\_2))&=\\zeta\_1(x\_1,x\_2)\\zeta\_2(y\_1,y\_2)\\\\ \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cdelta%28%28x_1%2Cy_1%29%2C%28x_2%2Cy_2%29%29%26%3D%5Cdelta_1%28x_1%2Cx_2%29%5Cdelta_2%28y_1%2Cy_2%29%5C%5C+%5Czeta%28%28x_1%2Cy_1%29%2C%28x_2%2Cy_2%29%29%26%3D%5Czeta_1%28x_1%2Cx_2%29%5Czeta_2%28y_1%2Cy_2%29%5C%5C+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

那么由此不难证明 ![\\mu=\\mu\_1\\mu\_2](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu%3D%5Cmu_1%5Cmu_2)

对于多个偏序集的积，也有相同的结论。

然而按上述定义，无限个局部有限偏序集的积并不是局部有限的，所以我们接着定义无限个偏序集的受限积。

对于一族偏序集 ![P\_i\\ (i\\in I)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P_i%5C+%28i%5Cin+I%29) ，我们从每个 ![P\_i](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P_i) 中都选取一个元素作为零元，记作 ![0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=0)

将 ![P\_i](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P_i) 们的受限积记作 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) ，对于 ![\\begin{align} \\prod\_{i\\in I} P\_i \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cprod_%7Bi%5Cin+I%7D+P_i+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) 中的元素 ![(x\_i)\_i](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28x_i%29_i) ， ![(x\_i)\_i\\in P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%28x_i%29_i%5Cin+P) 当且仅当除有限个 ![i](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=i) 之外，都有 ![x\_i=0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x_i%3D0)

我们让 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 继承 ![\\begin{align} \\prod\_{i\\in I} P\_i \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cprod_%7Bi%5Cin+I%7D+P_i+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) 的偏序结构，则 ![P](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=P) 是局部有限的。

对于受限积，同样有 ![\\begin{align} \\mu =\\prod\_{i\\in I} \\mu\_i \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cmu+%3D%5Cprod_%7Bi%5Cin+I%7D+%5Cmu_i+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，该式右侧的无穷乘积是良定义的。

## 子集反演

子集反演是偏序集反演的一个特例。

对于一个 ![n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=n) 元集合 ![S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=S) ，它的幂集 ![2^S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=2%5ES) 上的包含关系显然是一个偏序关系。

我们将 ![n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=n) 个二元偏序集 ![\\{0,1\\}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5C%7B0%2C1%5C%7D) 的积记作 ![\\{0,1\\}^n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5C%7B0%2C1%5C%7D%5En) ，我们可建立一个 ![2^S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=2%5ES) 和 ![\\{0,1\\}^n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5C%7B0%2C1%5C%7D%5En) 之间的同构 ![\\varphi](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cvarphi) 如下

设 ![S=\\{s\_1,s\_2,\\cdots,s\_n\\}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=S%3D%5C%7Bs_1%2Cs_2%2C%5Ccdots%2Cs_n%5C%7D) ，则对于 ![S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=S) 的一个子集 ![G](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=G) ，有 ![\\varphi(G)=(x\_1,x\_2,\\cdots,x\_n)](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cvarphi%28G%29%3D%28x_1%2Cx_2%2C%5Ccdots%2Cx_n%29)

其中，若 ![s\_i\\in G](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=s_i%5Cin+G) 则 ![x\_i=1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x_i%3D1) ，若 ![s\_i\\notin G](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=s_i%5Cnotin+G) 则 ![x\_i=0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x_i%3D0)

不难验证，这确实是一个偏序集的同构。

通过简单计算不难得到 ![\\{0,1\\}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5C%7B0%2C1%5C%7D) 的莫比乌斯函数满足 ![\\mu(0,0)=\\mu(1,1)=0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu%280%2C0%29%3D%5Cmu%281%2C1%29%3D0) ， ![\\mu(0,1)=-1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu%280%2C1%29%3D-1) ，即 ![\\mu(x,y)=(-1)^{y-x}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu%28x%2Cy%29%3D%28-1%29%5E%7By-x%7D) ，所以 ![\\{0,1\\}^n](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5C%7B0%2C1%5C%7D%5En) 的莫比乌斯函数为

![\\begin{align} \\mu((x\_1,x\_2,\\cdots,x\_n),(y\_1,y\_2,\\cdots,y\_n)) =\\prod\_{i=1}^n(-1)^{y\_i-x\_i} \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cmu%28%28x_1%2Cx_2%2C%5Ccdots%2Cx_n%29%2C%28y_1%2Cy_2%2C%5Ccdots%2Cy_n%29%29+%3D%5Cprod_%7Bi%3D1%7D%5En%28-1%29%5E%7By_i-x_i%7D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

根据上文得到的同构， ![2^S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=2%5ES) 的莫比乌斯函数为

![\\mu(A,B)=(-1)^{|B|-|A|}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmu%28A%2CB%29%3D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CB%7C-%7CA%7C%7D)

其中，用 ![|A|](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%7CA%7C) 表示子集 ![A](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A) 的元素个数。

由此，我们便可得到子集反演公式

> 定理：若 ![\\begin{align} g(B)=\\sum\_{A\\subseteq B}f(A) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+g%28B%29%3D%5Csum_%7BA%5Csubseteq+B%7Df%28A%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，则 ![\\begin{align} (-1)^{|B|}f(B)=\\sum\_{A\\subseteq B}(-1)^{|A|}g(A) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CB%7C%7Df%28B%29%3D%5Csum_%7BA%5Csubseteq+B%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CA%7C%7Dg%28A%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

## 最大最小反演

本文最后，我们来证明有限集上的 ![\\text{max-min}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctext%7Bmax-min%7D) 反演。

我们定义 ![\[-\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5B-%5D) 运算为，当条件 ![A](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A) 满足时，则 ![\[A\]=1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5BA%5D%3D1) ，当条件 ![A](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=A) 不被满足时， ![\[A\]=0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5BA%5D%3D0)

由该定义可得 ![\\begin{align} \[\\max S\\leqslant t\]=\\prod\_{x\\in S}\[x\\leqslant t\] \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5B%5Cmax+S%5Cleqslant+t%5D%3D%5Cprod_%7Bx%5Cin+S%7D%5Bx%5Cleqslant+t%5D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

又有 ![\[x\\leqslant t\]=1-\[x>t\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Bx%5Cleqslant+t%5D%3D1-%5Bx%3Et%5D) ，可得

![\\begin{align} \[\\max S\\leqslant t\]&=\\prod\_{x\\in S} (1-\[x>t\])\\\\ &=\\sum\_{G\\subseteq S}(-1)^{|G|}\\prod\_{x\\in G}\[x>t\] \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5B%5Cmax+S%5Cleqslant+t%5D%26%3D%5Cprod_%7Bx%5Cin+S%7D+%281-%5Bx%3Et%5D%29%5C%5C+%26%3D%5Csum_%7BG%5Csubseteq+S%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CG%7C%7D%5Cprod_%7Bx%5Cin+G%7D%5Bx%3Et%5D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

注意到 ![\\begin{align} \\prod\_{x\\in G}\[x> t\]=\[\\min G>t\] \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cprod_%7Bx%5Cin+G%7D%5Bx%3E+t%5D%3D%5B%5Cmin+G%3Et%5D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，代入上式

![\\begin{align} \[\\max S\\leqslant t\] &=1+\\sum\_{G\\subseteq S}(-1)^{|G|}\[\\min G>t\] \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5B%5Cmax+S%5Cleqslant+t%5D+%26%3D1%2B%5Csum_%7BG%5Csubseteq+S%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CG%7C%7D%5B%5Cmin+G%3Et%5D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

式子中的 ![1](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=1) 对应的是空集。

再一次利用 ![\[x\\leqslant t\]=1-\[x>t\]](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Bx%5Cleqslant+t%5D%3D1-%5Bx%3Et%5D) ，得到

![\\begin{align} \[\\max S> t\] &=-\\sum\_{G\\subseteq S}(-1)^{|G|}\[\\min G>t\] \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5B%5Cmax+S%3E+t%5D+%26%3D-%5Csum_%7BG%5Csubseteq+S%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CG%7C%7D%5B%5Cmin+G%3Et%5D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

又注意到，对于一个非负实数 ![x](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=x) ，有 ![\\begin{align} \\int\_0^\\infty\[x>t\]\\ {\\rm dt}=x \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cint_0%5E%5Cinfty%5Bx%3Et%5D%5C+%7B%5Crm+dt%7D%3Dx+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，于是对上式两侧积分。

我们先假设集合 ![S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=S) 中的元素都是非负的，于是有

![\\begin{align} \\max S &=-\\sum\_{G\\subseteq S}(-1)^{|G|}\\min G \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cmax+S+%26%3D-%5Csum_%7BG%5Csubseteq+S%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CG%7C%7D%5Cmin+G+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

即 ![\\text{max-min}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Ctext%7Bmax-min%7D) 反演公式。

如果集合 ![S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=S) 中有负数，则只需为 ![S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=S) 中所有元素都加上一个足够大的正数 ![s\_0](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=s_0) 即可，此时，积分结果为

![\\begin{align} \\max S+s\_0 &=-\\sum\_{G\\subseteq S}(-1)^{|G|}(\\min G+s\_0) \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cmax+S%2Bs_0+%26%3D-%5Csum_%7BG%5Csubseteq+S%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CG%7C%7D%28%5Cmin+G%2Bs_0%29+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

于是需要求出 ![\\begin{align} \\sum\_{G\\subseteq S}(-1)^{|G|} \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Csum_%7BG%5Csubseteq+S%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CG%7C%7D+%5Cend%7Balign%7D) ，只需注意到

![\\begin{align} \\sum\_{G\\subseteq S}(-1)^{|G|}=\\prod\_{x\\in G}(1-1)=0 \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Csum_%7BG%5Csubseteq+S%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CG%7C%7D%3D%5Cprod_%7Bx%5Cin+G%7D%281-1%29%3D0+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)

另外， ![S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=S) 中的最大值就是 ![\-S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=-S) 的最小值，所以可将反演公式中的 ![\\max](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmax) 和 ![\\min](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cmin) 互换。总之，我们证明了

> 定理：设 ![S](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=S) 是实数集的一个有限子集，则有  
> ![\\begin{align} \\max S&=-\\sum\_{G\\subseteq S}(-1)^{|G|}\\min G\\\\ \\min S&=-\\sum\_{G\\subseteq S}(-1)^{|G|}\\max G\\\\ \\end{align}](https://www.zhihu.com/equation?tex=%5Cbegin%7Balign%7D+%5Cmax+S%26%3D-%5Csum_%7BG%5Csubseteq+S%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CG%7C%7D%5Cmin+G%5C%5C+%5Cmin+S%26%3D-%5Csum_%7BG%5Csubseteq+S%7D%28-1%29%5E%7B%7CG%7C%7D%5Cmax+G%5C%5C+%5Cend%7Balign%7D)
