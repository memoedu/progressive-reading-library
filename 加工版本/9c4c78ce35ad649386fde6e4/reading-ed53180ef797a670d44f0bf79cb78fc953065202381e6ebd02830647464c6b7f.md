A first person report of [participating](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/your-review-participation-in-phase) in a Phase 1 clinical trial

In defense of the [Amyloid](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/in-defense-of-the-amyloid-hypothesis) hypothesis (See previous Nintil posts on [Alzheimer's](https://nintil.com/categories/alzheimer-s-disease/) too, though they may be out of date by now). The linked post in Astral Codex Ten is I think mistaken, and one can point to Alnylam's recent [failure](https://capella.alnylam.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/AAIC-Phase-1_OP_SUBMITTED28Jul.pdf) (No NfL change->no neurodegeneration slowdown) as a more solid test than perhaps the antibodies. It is nonetheless a good presentation of the amyloid cascade hypothesis and why it makes so much sense that amyloid beta is in some way in the etiology of Alzheimer's. The idea that very early prevention could slow down the appearence of AD does make sense to me, but once the disease starts one has to intervene at a different place to stop it. The company I work [at](https://www.retro.bio/pipeline) is testing a drug for AD later this year in Phase 1, we shall see how that goes!

Epigenetic proxies for health [biomarkers](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/acel.70224) like LDL fail to pick up changes in a randomized control trial

It is argued [here](https://hereticalinsights.substack.com/p/age-and-cognitive-ability) that IQ decline happens much slower than commonly assumed.
