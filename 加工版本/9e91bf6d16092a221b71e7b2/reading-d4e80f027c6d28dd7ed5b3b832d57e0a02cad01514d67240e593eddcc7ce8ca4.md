最后，我本来打算为 Pinker 的上一本书写一篇 review，但没有写成。这里我只想说，我发现自己赞同他所说的大部分内容，唯独关于 existential risks 的那部分除外，他在那里还不够 Bayesian（不过，也许他是的，这取决于你如何理解他的话。他是在否认向 rare events assigning credences 的 possibility 吗？）。他对于 superintelligent AI 相关问题的 view 比他此前对此的看法有所改善，但仍然有点傻。举例来说，他在某处声称，如果人类足够聪明，能够 build superintelligent AI，那么我们也足够聪明，能够避免用它杀死自己，却忽略了我们的灭亡曾是 Cold War 的一个 possible outcome。没错，我们活了下来，但我们原本也可能没有活下来。有趣的是，他说，如果一个 AI 足够聪明，能够 take over the world，那么它也会足够聪明，能够理解我们说“给我做一些 paperclips”是什么意思（做 4 或 5 个，而不是为了以防万一，take over the universe 来做很多）。乍看之下，这可能又像是一个糟糕的看法：一个由几个 modules composed 的 AI system，可以有一个用于 interpreting orders 的 deficient input module，却依然擅长把事情办成；但在这里也可以 argued 说，拥有关于世界的 good priors（知道 humans 说“给我做一堆 paperclips”是什么意思），正是 take over 它所需条件的一部分。但如果他想提出一个 argument，反对担忧 AI，也许更好的一个是 Talebian argument：世界实在太 random 了。在 coin tossing exercise 中，一个 superintelligent AI 不会比你表现得更好。一个 superintelligent AI 能比 stock market 表现得更好吗？诸如此类。我没有在其他地方见过对此进行很深入的探讨，但如果它是 valid 的，就能化解这个问题。

Pinker 的书还有一个问题，是 data quality 和 comparability。他的书声称——[间接地](https://twitter.com/NoamJStein/status/965705903956811776)——60s 的 Montreal 与 2005 的 Middle East 一样 liberal。这并不 plausible。这是那个 plot 的 constructed 方式（通过 extrapolation）及其 measures 的对象——subjective responses——造成的 artifact。如果有人问：“being gay 是件坏事吗？请用 Likert scale 对你的答案进行 Rank”，Montreal 的一个 5，可能意味着某人对他们有一些隐约的不自在，但也仅此而已；而 Iraq 的一个 5，则可能意味着某人一见到他们，就会用石头砸死他们。

还有一个问题是 [data quality](https://medium.com/@UnlearningEcon/seeing-like-a-neoliberal-part-1-blinded-by-the-data-a134a7026d87)。不过，即使这些 critiques 部分地 undermine 了其中一些 points，所有 evidence 的 conjunction 仍然是 mutually reinforcing 的，那个 point 依然站得住脚。

Last, I meant to write a review of Pinker's last book, but I didn't. Instead, I'll just say that I find myself in agreement with most of what he says, except in the bit around existential risks, where he is not sufficiently Bayesian (But maybe he is, depending on how your read his words. Is he denying the possibility of assigning credences to rare events?) His view of the problems around superintelligent AI are better than his previous takes on the matter, but still a bit silly. As an example, at some point he claims that if mankind is smart enough to build superintelligent AI, we are smart enough to avoid killing ourselves with it, ignoring that our end was a possible outcome of the Cold War. Yes, we survived, but we could have not. Interestingly, he says that if an AI is smart enough to take over the world, it will be smart enough to understand what we mean by "Make me some paperclips" (Make 4 or 5 vs take over the universe to make lots, just in case). Initially, this may seem like another bad take: An AI system composed of several modules can have a deficient input module for interpreting orders and yet be good at getting things done, but here it could be argued that having good priors about the world (knowing what humans mean by "make me a bunch of paperclips") is part of what is needed to take over it. But if he wants to offer an argument against worrying about AI, perhaps a better one is the Talebian argument: the world is just too random. On a coin tossing exercise, a superintelligent AI won't do better than you. Can a superintelligent AI do better than the stock market? Etc. I haven't seen this explored in much depth elsewhere, but if valid, this defuses this issue.

Another problem with Pinker's is data quality and comparability. His book claims that - [indirectly](https://twitter.com/NoamJStein/status/965705903956811776) - Montreal in the 60s is as liberal as the Middle East in 2005. This is not plausible. It is an artifact of the way that plot is constructed (by extrapolation) and the thing it measures: subjective responses. If one asks "Is being gay a bad thing? Rank your answer in a Likert scale", a 5 in Montreal might mean that one has some vague squirm about them, but otherwise that's it, while a 5 in Iraq may mean that one will stone them on sight.

And another is [data quality](https://medium.com/@UnlearningEcon/seeing-like-a-neoliberal-part-1-blinded-by-the-data-a134a7026d87). However, even if these critiques undermine partially some of the points, the conjunction of all the evidence is mutually reinforcing, and the point still stands strong.
