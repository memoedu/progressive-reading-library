## 正文

有一个网站，曾在 Smart Internet™ 中相当一部分人之间颇受欢迎，那就是 [Less Wrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LessWrong)。它由 Overcoming Bias 的共同博主 Eliezer Yudkowsky 于 2009 年创办，如今基本已死（说的是网站，不是 Yudkowsky，哈哈），不过它催生了好几个彼此独立、至今仍然活跃的 communities。

[Shibboleths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shibboleth) 是一些 words 或 customs，用来将 ingroup 的 members 与 outgroup 的 members 区分开来。在这里，Less Wrong shibboleths 指的是这样一些东西：如果你读到了它们，就知道作者很可能与 Less Wrong 有某种联系。如果你看到许多个一起出现，就提高你的 confidence。

这些 shibboleths 似乎包括：

-   谈论 large numbers 时使用 3^^^3
-   Pascal's Mugging（有趣的是，这个 concept 并不像人们普遍以为的那样 originated 于 Less Wrong [本身，](http://lesswrong.com/lw/kd/pascals_mugging_tiny_probabilities_of_vast/)而是 originated 于 Alex Tabarrok 的[一篇文章](https://mason.gmu.edu/%7Eatabarro/Pascal'sWager.pdf)，比 Less Wrong 的那篇早七年）
-   将自己称为 aspiring rationalist
-   把 Bayesianism 说成一种无所不能的 tool
-   用 'Dark arts' 指代为了 persuade 某人而对 biases 进行的 systematic exploitation
-   FOOM：一个用来表示 intelligence explosion 的有趣 onomatopoetic term
-   用 Utilon 而不是 util（说真的，为什么？）
-   map 不是 territory
-   Politics 是 mind-killer
-   对 Harry Potter and the Methods of Rationality 的 Knowledge
-   Paperclip maximizer
-   闭嘴，然后 multiply。在 decision theory 或 ethics 中会出现。
-   Solomonoff induction
-   Kolmogorov complexity
-   Friendly AI 和 AI risk
-   谈论 'updating beliefs'
-   Wireheading
-   Fake X
-   Mere Y
-   Polyamory

现在你可能在期待一些讨论之类的内容，不过别期待了 :)。

## 原文

There's a site that commanded certain popularity among a sizable fraction of the Smart Internet™, [Less Wrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LessWrong). Founded by Overcoming Bias co-blogger Eliezer Yudkowsky in 2009, and now mostly dead (The site, not Yudkowsky, lol) , it has spawned a number of separate communities that remain active.

[Shibboleths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shibboleth)are words or customs that serve to identify members of an ingroup from members of an outgroup. In this context, Less Wrong shibboleths are things that, if you read them, you know the writer probably has some connection to Less Wrong. If you see many together, increase your confidence.

These shibboleths seem to be:

-   Using 3^^^3 when talking about large numbers
-   Pascal's Mugging (Interestingly, the concept originated not in Less Wrong [itself,](http://lesswrong.com/lw/kd/pascals_mugging_tiny_probabilities_of_vast/) as commonly believed, but in [an article](https://mason.gmu.edu/%7Eatabarro/Pascal'sWager.pdf) by Alex Tabarrok,that predates Less Wrong's by seven years)
-   Refering to oneself as an aspiring rationalist
-   Talking about Bayesianism as an all-powerful tool
-   Using 'Dark arts' to refer to systematic exploitation of biases to persuade someone
-   FOOM: A funny onomatopoetic term for intelligence explosion
-   Utilon instead of util (Seriously, WHY?)
-   The map is not the territory
-   Politics is the mind-killer
-   Knowledge of Harry Potter and the Methods of Rationality
-   Paperclip maximizer
-   Shut up and multiply. Comes up in decision theory or ethics.
-   Solomonoff induction
-   Kolmogorov complexity
-   Friendly AI and AI risk
-   Talking about 'updating beliefs'
-   Wireheading
-   Fake X
-   Mere Y
-   Polyamory

Now you may be expecting some discussion or something, but stop expecting :).
