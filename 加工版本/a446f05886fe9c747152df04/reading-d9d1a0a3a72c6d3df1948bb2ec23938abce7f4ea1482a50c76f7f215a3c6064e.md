# Nintil - Why so many charts?!

---

## 正文

这个博客的读者应该已经注意到，我往往会在几篇文章里发布大量 charts。这是有原因的。

对于我们提出的大多数 questions，都有许多 interpretations。例如，从我最近的文章说起，我们可以问：“communism 能 work 吗？”然后我们就会进入“但我们说 communism 时是什么意思？”“我们说 work 时是什么意思？”这些问题。

接着我们又会进入“哪些 countries count as communist？”“我们应该用哪些 measures 来思考 ‘working’？”“哪个 time period？”诸如此类。

之所以会这样，是因为对于任何 concept\[1\]，都没有 clear-cut definition（[Huemer, 2015](http://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007%2F978-1-137-34455-7_3)）。事物具有 particular qualities，而 concepts 是围绕它们的 fuzzily defined clouds，使我们能够 generalise。Sketching 它们的 boundaries 以及 working with concepts，实在是一门艺术。

对于 Soviet Union，如果把 ‘worked’ defined 为 raising 其 population 的 standards of living，那么它确实 worked。如果把它 defined 为 ‘better than the average country’，它也 worked。如果把它 defined 为以 ‘sustaining’ 的方式，那么它可能没有，因为它最终 crashing 了。如果把它 defined 为 ‘worked better than capitalism’，我们就会陷入 defining capitalism 的麻烦（Hong Kong？USA？Morocco？）。不过，随后我们可以把它与 ‘best plausible alternative’ 进行 compare，进入 counterfactual game。

我们也可以 analyse ‘standards of living’。谁的？poorest 的？average citizen 的？Median 呢？某个 weighted average 的？我们 count 什么？Basic foodstuff？Colour televisions？Private jets？一种 Maslowlike assessment，其中更 basic 的 needs 被赋予更大的 weight？

所以，我的做法是尝试找出 meanings 的不同 interpretations，看看我们是否能说些什么是“对于大多数 plausible interpretations”都成立的，还是不得不说“在这个 sense 上，发生了这件事；在另一个 sense 上，发生了另外这些事”。

\[1\] 或许 very simple concepts，或自身包含一套 rules 的 concepts 除外（例如 GDP，它被 defined 为 GDP=C+I+G+(X-M)，以及类似的 definitions）。但即使在这里也并非如此，因为 concepts 是用其他 concepts 来 defined 的，而我们无法通过 introducing 一些 100% clear concepts\[1.1\] 来 fully close 这个 net of concepts。所以，即使 ‘kg of potatoes produced’ 比 ‘GDP’ 更 clearer，我们仍然面临这些问题：precisely defining 什么 counts as 一个 potatoe，应该考虑 potatoe 的什么 minimum quality，是否应该 assume reported data 就是 real data，还是需要 correct 它，等等。

\[1.1\] 祝你好运，试着用 quarks 和最 basic 的 laws of Physics 来 producing explanations 吧。

## 原文

Readers of this blog will have noticed that I tend to post lots of charts in a few posts. There's a reason for that.

For most questions we ask there are many interpretations. For example, drawing from my recent posts, we could ask "Does communism work?". And there we go into "But what do we mean by communism?", "What do we mean by work?".

Then we get into "Which countries count as communist?", "Which measures should we used to think about 'working'?", "Which time period?". Etc.

This happens because for any concept\[1\], there is no clear-cut definition for it ([Huemer, 2015](http://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007%2F978-1-137-34455-7_3)). Things have particular qualities and concepts are fuzzily defined clouds around them so that we can generalise. Sketching their boundaries and working with concepts is a true art.

For the Soviet Union, if 'worked' is defined as raising the standards of living of the population, it worked. If it is defined as 'better than the average country' it also worked. If it is defined as 'in a sustaining way' it may not, as it ended up crashing. If it is defined as 'worked better than capitalism' we get into the trouble of defining capitalism (Hong Kong? USA? Morocco?) But then we can compare it to the 'best plausible alternative' and enter the counterfactual game.

We can also analyse 'standards of living'. Of whom? Of the poorest? Of the average citizen? Median? Of a weighted average? What do we count? Basic foodstuff? Colour televisions? Private jets? A Maslowlike assessment in which more basic needs are given a greater weight?

So what I do is try to get different interpretations of the meanings and see if we can say something 'For most plausible interpretations' or if we have to resort to 'In this sense, this happened, in that other sense, these other things happened'.

\[1\] Except perhaps for very simple concepts or concepts that have a set of rules as part of them (like GDP, defined as GDP=C+I+G+(X-M) and similar definitions). But not even here, as concepts are defined using other concepts, and we cannot fully close the net of concepts by introducing some 100% clear concepts\[1.1\]. So even when 'kg of potatoes produced' is something clearer than 'GDP', it still leaves us with the problem of defining precisely what counts as a potatoe, what minimum quality of potatoe should we consider, whether we should assume that reported data is the real data or we need to correct it, and so on.

\[1.1\] Good luck producing explanations in term of quarks and the most basic laws of Physics
