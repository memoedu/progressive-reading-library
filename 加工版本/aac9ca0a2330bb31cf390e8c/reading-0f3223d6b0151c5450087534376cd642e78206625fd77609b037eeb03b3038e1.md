# 如何像 strategic genius 一样思考（5d thinking）

你的 thinking ability 决定你人生的 outcome。

这并非夸张，而 learning to think 从未像今天这样重要。

尤其是当你认为自己是 smart person 时，因为你最有可能像 idiot 一样 thinking。

我的意思是，social media 上到处都是这种情况。没有哪一天你不会看到一个 IQ 可验证地很高的人落入明显的陷阱。

有很多 smart people 住在妈妈的地下室里，离 heart attack 只差一根 french fry；也有很多 dumb people 异常地 happy、healthy、wealthy。

我不打算谈 first-principles thinking、systems thinking、meta cognition，或其他任何你可以让 ChatGPT 教你的内容；我想给你一些不同的东西。

我们将从 one-dimensional thinking 开始，慢慢朝着 5d thinking 前进。

如果你仔细阅读，就会体验到在一个新 level 上 thinking 是什么感觉。

### **如果你这么 smart，为什么还 broke？**

> Faith 远胜于 belief。Belief 是别人替你 thinking。
>
> – Buckminster Fuller

Thinking tip number one：

如果你想理解某件事是什么，比如 genius level thinking，那么理解它不是什么也会有所帮助。

这样，我们就能识别并 _avoid_ stupid thinking。

当我们观察 stupid thinking 是什么样子时，会得到一些 insights：

*   **Stupid thinking 是 one-dimensional。**人们试图把一切都塞进自己的 perspective，很难看到它之外的东西。

*   **Stupid thinking 是 reductionistic。**例如，一个 domain 的 Experts，比如 business 领域的 Experts，会试图把一切都简化成一个“strategy” problem。

*   **Stupid thinking 是 tribal。**你只信任自己的 group、party 或 tribe，并认为其他人都是错的，因为他们不 conform。

*   **Stupid thinking 不 question。**你的 justification 是“事情本来就是这么做的”。

总的来说，stupid thinking 就是在你达到所知边界后，_把自己的 mind 封闭起来_。

Stupid thinking 就是过早地 _stop_ thinking。到某个时候，你会用预先编好的 thoughts 做出反应，无法获得任何 novel insight。

有了这些，我们就可以开始尝试为 genius thinking 推测一个合适的 definition，或许可以这样定义：

在可能性的范围内容纳具有威胁性的 ideas，同时意在理解而不只是知道。genius thinking 的标志，体现在你 thinking 的 width、depth 和 height 上：你能在多大程度上继续探索，而不被阻断；这种阻断往往是因为你把某个 idea 当作绝对真理。它是你穿行于完整、普遍的 ideas 网络（reality、所有潜在 knowledge）的 ability，并将它们汇集成连贯或有用的东西，甚至是全新的东西。

那么，knowing 和 understanding 有什么区别？你如何发展 thought 的 width、depth 和 height？你还能把它推进得更远吗？

Knowing 是 horizontal。你学习很多，并记住某个特定 domain 的 facts，直到自己变得 competent。知道很多的人是“experts”，但在当今世界，我们不需要更多 experts。

Understanding 是 vertical。它关乎你的整个 cognitive operating system 有多 sophisticated。一个人可以理解很多却知道很少，并运用这种 insight 采取行动，从而带来更有价值的 result。

“smart but dumb” phenomenon 源于 horizontal 上 advanced、但 vertical 上停滞不前。一个 businessman 拥有世上所有的钱，却发现自己并不 happy。一个 creative 有出色的作品，却无法谋生。一个 meathead 无法维持稳定的 relationship。他们在各自的 domain 知道很多，却看不到自己的 knowledge bubble 之外；而这个 knowledge bubble 提供的 surface area 不够，无法识别并解决那些导致 suffering 的 problems。

这就是 learning how to think 如此重要的原因。

因为你的 mind 是你与 reality 互动的方式。

你 process information → 理解它的意义（thinking）→ 做出 choice → 收到 information 作为 feedback → 对 feedback 做出回应并重复这个 cycle。

因此，thinking 决定你人生的 outcome。

每一个你进行 stupid thinking 的瞬间都会产生累积效应，把你越陷越深，深到你甚至无法触及那个能带你脱身的 idea。

### **Thinking 的 altitudes、levels 和 lines**

> 你无法从创造某个 problem 的同一 consciousness level 解决它。
>
> – Albert Einstein

让我们从基础开始。

Thinking 的 **Lines** 是你可以了解很多的 domains。

Lines 代表 horizontal development（width）。

这些可以是任何东西：Marketing、astrophysics、politics、social dynamics、religion 等等。当你试图学习 domain specific knowledge 时，你就在增加那条 thinking line 的 width。这就像在 video game 中积累经验。

（顺便说一句，如果你研究过 Ken Wilber，就能看出我在这里想表达什么，只不过我是把它应用于 thinking。）

Thinking 的 **Levels** 是你 thinking about each line 的方式。

Levels 代表 vertical development（depth）。

当我们观察 cognitive development 如何随着时间在 collectives 和 individuals 中演进时，可以发现 5 种核心 patterns：

*   **Instinctual (Level 0)** – 你出生时会出于纯粹 survival 的需要而行动。你对 stimuli 做出反应，其间几乎没有 thinking。

*   **Conformist (Level 1)** – Black and white thinking。你遵循 rules、服从 authority，不加质疑。_你采纳他人的 perspective。_

*   **Individualist (Level 2)** – Critical thinking 开始出现，你构建自己的 model。_你建立自己的 perspective。_

*   **Synthesist (Level 3)** – 你把自己的 model 看作众多 model 之一。你容纳 contradictions，并把 perspectives 当作 tools，而非 law 或某种不容质疑的 truth。

*   **Generative (Level 4)** – 你创造出此前不存在的 original perspectives，或者不受外界影响地得出 ideas。

Levels 1 和 2 可以被视为“first tier” thinking，它过于 dogmatic：我是对的，你是错的。

Levels 3 和 4 可以被视为“second tier” thinking，它们 reject dogma，并寻求处于中间的 ultimate truth。

在 meaning 这个 domain 中，你通常先被灌输进某个 belief system，之后质疑那个 belief system 并得出自己的 conclusions（通常会 reject 先前的 belief system），接着开始看到所有 belief systems 中的 truths，最后有能力创造一个更 expansive 的 belief system。

在 politics 中，我们看到的大量 fighting 和 violence，都是由于 general population 在 levels 1 和 2 内运作。我的 group 对你的 group。双方都无法超越自己的 bubble 去 thinking。

再说一次，我要把这点说得非常清楚，因为这是一个极容易掉进去的 trap：

Genius thinking 是持续 thinking 的 ability。

在 thinking 的每一个 level，你驾驭已知和未知 ideas 空间的 ability 都在不断扩展。你出生时，并没有 _真正_ thinking。作为 conformist，你会一直 thinking，直到得到“那个”answer，然后开始 _defending_ 那个 answer。作为 individualist，你做的也是同一件事，只不过 defending 的是 _你自己的_ answer。达到 synthesist 时，你可以进行广泛而深入的 thinking，但你 _create new lines of thought_ 的 ability 尚未完全发展。

要达到你最高的 thinking ability，从而为你此生的最高 potential 提供 runway，归根结底是一个简单的 practice：_留意 mind 感到受威胁的时刻，对自己诚实，并至少保持对 new perspectives 开放。_

Thinking 的 **Altitude** 是你所有 levels 的平均值。

Altitude 代表 cross-dimensional development（height）。

为了最好地理解这一点，并 _几乎_ 完成 thinking puzzle，把 thinking 想象成 skill tree 会有所帮助。

在 video game 中，你可以把“points”投入某些 traits，从而在游戏中做更多事情。你可以挑战更高难度、玩得更开心，并继续玩下去。mind 也是如此。

关键在于，只有达到较低 level traits 的特定 requirement，才能 unlock 更高 level 的 traits。比如，不达到“level 4”的 strength，你就无法获得“level 3”的 dexterity。

在现实世界中，设想你开办了一家 business。你学会了关于 marketing、sales 和 product 的一切。你的 smart 是可验证的。你赚了不少钱。但后来你想更进一步，却遇到了一个看不见的 road block。你找不出问题所在，接着把它归咎于自己知道的东西：“market 就是不够大”，或类似的说法。你的 mind 封闭了，前面也没有路可走。

此外，显然 business 并不是 life 的全部。如果 life 的其他部分正在分崩离析，作为 business person 的你会试图把圆形塞进方形的洞里。你也许能暂时缓解问题，却始终无法理解 root cause。

> 这大体上就是 politics、religion 以及 nutrition 等其他 domains 如此敌对、如此两极化的原因。一群 smart but dumb people 互相称对方为 idiots，因为他们已经停止 thinking，只会捍卫自己所相信的唯一正确道路。

回到 business，问题在于你看不到真正的 problem。可能是你不知道如何 lead a team，也可能是 time management issue；甚至可能是 spiritual issue，但你太 business minded，连这种可能性都不愿考虑。至少，这种 perspective 可能催生出带来进一步 progress 的 insight。这就是 generalists 和 polymaths 能胜出的原因。

换句话说，你无法达到 business 的 Level 3，因为你还没有把“points”投入其他必需的 domains，而那些 domains 才能 unlock 那个 level。

### **如何解锁 4-dimensional thinking**

思考有第四个 dimension。

嗯，它并不是真正像 time 那样的第四个 dimension，但它确实能让你的 thinking ability 提升到四倍。

你看，reality 有 4 个“dimensions”。

这些 dimensions 是 perspectives。你可以用 4 种主要方式改变自己看待 belief、idea、situation 或 problem 的方式。

[

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!iIt8!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe03a144f-9228-4dcc-80f2-48d0cfc99f8f_1000x1000.png)

这里既有 inner world，也有 outer world；两者又都包含 collective 和 individual 的部分。

**inner world** 是 mental。我指的是 psychological 和 cultural。你的 _individual_ inner psychology 包含你的 thoughts、emotions、beliefs 和 consciousness。_collective_ inner world，也就是 culture，包含 group beliefs、value systems 和 ideologies。

**outer world** 是 physical，听起来没那么玄乎，但你不能否认自己可以思考 mental 的内容，而这正是我们在这里要讨论的。你的 _individual_ outer world 是你可见的 appearance、behaviors，以及 physical brain states 或 measurements。_collective_ outer world 由 systems、structures 和 social institutions 构成。

为了说明理解这一点的力量，我们来看看 thinking progression。

*   I 学习很多关于 marketing 的知识，并运用这些 knowledge（line of thinking）

*   I 进行 practice、失败并积累足够的经验，从而理解并不存在“一个 proven framework”；但我已经有了足够的 understanding，能够创造出表现更出色的 marketing campaigns（levels of thinking）

*   I 培养对 psychology、fitness 和 personal development 等方面的兴趣，这让我获得更深入的 pattern recognition，进一步摆脱 dogmatic frameworks（altitude of thinking）

从这里开始，事情变得有趣起来，而且可以朝许多不同方向发展。

有一点我们忘了提：thinking 通常是为了 solve a problem。人类就是这么做的。我们就是这样成长的。我们也从中获得 fulfillment。而你一旦停止成长（也就停止了 thinking），problems 就会不断增多，生活也会变得越来越 chaotic。

所以，如果我们识别出一个像“世界正变得腐败且毫无意义”这样的 problem，就可以沿着这个方向继续 thinking。

社会如何控制 attention，并向大众推销他们的 ideas？（collective inner world）

在这一过程中，你的 behavior 还能如何改变，才能对社会产生积极影响？要达到足以留下个人印记的 influence，需要采取哪些 steps？（individual outer world）

当前的 job market 是什么样的？一份工作真的是实现这种目标的途径吗？AI 呢？我能否利用现有的 technology 来产生这种影响？（collective outer world）

我这样 thinking 对吗？还是还有更多？我漏掉了什么？这让我有什么感受？Motivated？Inspired？Hateful？（individual inner world）

我们还可以继续说下去，不过我想你已经明白了。

Genius thinking 是把 attention 引向有用方向的 ability。你逐渐引导自己的 mind 不断向外 zoom out，从那里获得更 enlightened 的 vantage point，再 zoom in 到你最初想要 solve 的 problem 上。

### **如何触及 5th dimension**

> 适应一个病入膏肓的社会，不能作为健康的衡量标准。
>
> – Krishnamurti

是的，我知道，这实际上并不是 5th dimension。

它是第四个 dimension（time），但我在这里试着通过编造一些东西来 practice my thinking；当你把 time 应用到我们刚才谈过的 reality 的全部四个 dimensions 上时，你的 ability to think 就会继续飞速提升。

在之前的所有 examples 中，我们都没有触及 history 的力量。如果你理解 history，尤其是 evolutionary patterns，而不是死记硬背 events，就能把 thinking _aim_ 得更加准确。

这说明了 knowing 和 understanding 的区别。你可以知道许多关于 history 中特定时刻的 facts，这很好；但如果你不能 zoom out，看到那个时刻所代表的 essence，就可能无法发现这一 pattern 如何贯穿不同的 domains。

要开始有效地思考 history，有 5 件事需要知道。这些并不全面，还有更多内容，但足以作为一个好的起点。

**1) master pattern 是 Transcend and Include**

materialist 会说 reality 由 atoms 构成，他们说得没错。

mentalist 会说 reality 由 qualia 构成，他们说得没错。

但如果为了 _understanding_ 而 zoom out，就会清楚地看到，reality 由 whole parts 构成（既是 wholes、又是更大 whole 的 parts）。

Matter → Life → Mind

Word → Sentence → Paragraph

Machine → Computer → Artificial Intelligence

Human → House → City → State → Country → Continent → Planet → Solar system → Galaxy

每一个都是一个 whole，它会 transcend 并 include 前一个 whole。也就是说，如果移除让另一事物得以存在的事物，整条 chain 就会自我毁灭（这就是 environmentalists 如此担心 climate change 的原因。如果 biosphere 崩溃，人类也会随之覆灭）。

通过 examples 就能明白这一点。

**2) individuals 如何在 physical 上 evolve**

这一点很简单，因为大多数人都能看见、触摸、闻到它，等等。

Atoms → Cells → Molecules → Organs → Organism

你可以根据 individual things 如何 develop 来思考 physical world，而不只是关注 humans。

**3) individuals 如何在 mental 上 evolve**

Humans 通过不断扩大的 circle of care 发展。

首先，我们关心自己以及自己的 survival（egocentric）。

然后，我们关心自己的 tribe、group 或 culture（ethnocentric）。

接着，我们开始关心所有 groups，不论彼此有何差异（worldcentric）；但由于 master pattern 是 _transcend and include_，我们仍然关心自己和自己的 tribe，这一点必须纳入我们的 thinking。我们不会仅仅因为关心他人听起来很 virtuous，就牺牲自己或自己的 values。大多数 groups 之间的 arguments 通常就在这里产生，因为有些人把“关心所有人的 equality”当作 moral high ground，而这往往会导致 self-destructive，反而不利于关心所有人。

在发展程度最高的 levels，我们关心 reality，将其视为终极 whole。显然，要从 vast amounts of complexity 中理清头绪并得出任何 practical 的东西，需要具备相应的 thinking ability。

**4) societies 如何在 physical 上 evolve**

Social structures 随着 technology 发展。

随着 hoe 的发明让小规模 farming 成为可能，我们从 tribes 发展到 villages。

随着 horse-drawn plow 的发明，我们从 villages 发展到 empires（因为 food surplus 让人们得以减少 farming，更多地探索、发现和征服。）

随着 machine agriculture 的发明，我们从 empires 发展到 nation-states。

如今，computers 就在我们口袋里，AI 也近在眼前，接下来可能会怎样，就留给你去思考了。

**5) societies 如何在 mental 上 evolve**

由于 individuals 构成 collective，societies 也会按照类似的 egocentric、ethnocentric 和 worldcentric pattern 发展。

Collectives 也有 identity。

Tribe 只会关心自身及其 survival。之后，它可以扩展成更大的 empire 或 nation-state，将多个 sub-groups 纳入其中。

在这种情况下，我们把这些 worldviews 称为 pre-rational、rational 和 post-rational。起初我们遵从 authority，随后通过 science（the enlightenment）寻求 progress，接着开始认识到两者的盲点，同时整合其中好的部分。

但你究竟会如何运用这些 knowledge 来“think”呢？

下面是 _many_ 种方法中的几种：

1.  遇到某种 idea、movement 或 conflict 时，判断 individuals 处于什么发展阶段。进入 AI 时代后，你希望谁来担任 politician？

2.  参与 debate（political、business、personal）时，留意人们是从哪个 level 出发的。处于较高 levels of development 的 individuals 往往需要设身处地地理解他人。

3.  观察 societies 和 organizations 的 technology。仍在使用 industrial era methods、又过于 dogmatic 而不愿适应的 business，可能会失败。

最有用的应用，是找出你自己的 center of gravity 所在。我建议你过段时间再读一遍这封信，同时思考自己的 development。

### **我们漏掉了一件重要的事**

Stupid thinking 就是停止 thinking。

这一点我们知道。

但是什么会让一个人停止 thinking？是什么让他们从 open 转为 defensive，把自己封闭起来，不再继续 learning 和 growth？

答案是 identity。

具体来说，就是执着于 ideas、beliefs 或 constructs，并把它们变成自我的一部分。

因为如果你是坚定的 republican 或 democrat，就永远无法跳出那个 bubble 来 thinking。因此，你永远无法找到事情的 truth。这并不意味着你不能采纳任一方的某些 values，只是说，当别人不同意你时，你不必感到受威胁。

同样的道理也适用于认同某个特定 religion、group、vocation，或我们之前讨论过的任何其他事物。

你还必须考虑自己是如何形成那些 beliefs 的。

在当今世界，大多数人一生中从未有哪怕一天真正独立 thinking。

他们出生在世界上的某个特定地区。

那个地区处于某个特定的 development level。

父母告诉他们该 believe 什么，而那些父母很可能从未质疑过自己的 beliefs。

那些 beliefs 塑造了你的 thinking 能走多远，而那很可能并不远。

还要记住，你的 mind 是你与 reality 互动的方式。

你处理 information → 理解它（thinking）→ 做出选择 → 接收作为 feedback 的 information → 对 feedback 作出回应并重复这个 cycle。

你能够 thinking 的空间决定了你人生的 outcome。

如果你只在父母、老师、雇主和社交媒体上的同伴所设定的界限内 thinking，你人生的 outcome 可能不太美好。只要看看普通人最终走向何处，就不难明白。

所以，下次你感到受威胁时，不要向内退缩。

和这种感受待在一起。质疑它。意识到你的 mind 想要感到安全，因为它就是这样 wired 的。

我们的大脑仍然像 hunter-gatherer ancestors 那样 wired，但我们已经不再生活在那个世界里，而且这个世界只会变化得更快。

那些 practice thinking 的人会赢。

– Dan

> *   **Stupid thinking is tribal.** You only trust your group, party, or tribe and consider everyone else wrong because they don’t conform.
>
> *   **Stupid thinking does not question.** Your justification is “that’s just how it’s done.”
>
> As a whole, stupid thinking is about _closing your mind off once you’ve reached the limits of what you know._
>
> Stupid thinking is to _stop_ thinking too early. To reach a point where you react with your pre-programmed thoughts and fail to reach any novel insight.
>
> With that, we can start to guess at a worthy definition of genius thinking, which could be:
>
> The ability to hold threatening ideas in the realm of possibility, paired with the intention to understand rather than just know. The mark of genius thinking is illustrated by the width, depth, and height at which you can think without being cut off from venturing further, often due to holding an idea as absolute. It’s your ability to traverse the full, universal web of ideas (reality, all potential knowledge) and pull them together into something coherent or useful, if not something entirely new.
>
> The question then is, what’s the difference between knowing and understanding? And how do you develop width, depth, and height of thought? What if you can take it even further than that?
>
> Knowing is horizontal. You learn a lot and memorize facts about a specific domain until you become competent. People who know a lot are “experts,” but we don’t need more experts in today’s world.
>
> Understanding is vertical. It’s about how sophisticated your entire cognitive operating system is. One can understand a lot while knowing very little and use that insight to take an action that leads to a more worthwhile result.
>
> The “smart but dumb” phenomenon stems from being horizontally advanced but vertically stuck. A businessman who has all the money in the world but finds himself unhappy. A creative with beautiful work that can’t make a living. A meathead that can’t hold a steady relationship. They know a lot in their respective domain, but can’t see outside of their bubble of knowledge, and that bubble of knowledge does not provide enough surface area to identify and solve problems that lead to suffering.
>
> That’s why learning how to think is so important.
>
> Because your mind is how you interact with reality.
>
> You process information → make sense of it (thinking) → make a choice → receive information as feedback → respond to that feedback and repeat the cycle.
>
> Thinking, then, determines the outcome of your life.
>
> Every passing moment where you engage in stupid thinking contributes to a compounding effect, digging you into a rut so deep you can’t even access the idea that would lead you out.
>
> ### **Altitudes, levels, and lines of thinking**
>
> > You can’t solve a problem from the same level of consciousness that created it.
> >
> > – Albert Einstein
>
> Let’s work from the ground up.
>
> **Lines** of thinking are domains you can know a lot about.
>
> Lines represent horizontal development (width).
>
> These can be anything. Marketing, astrophysics, politics, social dynamics, religion, etc. When you attempt to learn domain specific knowledge, you are increasing the width of that line of thinking. It’s like gaining experience in a video game.
>
> (By the way, if you’ve studied Ken Wilber at all, you can see what I’m getting at here, just applied to thinking).

> **Levels** of thinking are how you think about each line.
>
> Levels represent vertical development (depth).
>
> When we observe how cognitive development evolves in collectives and individuals over time, we can notice 5 core patterns:
>
> *   **Instinctual (Level 0)** – You are born act out of the need for pure survival. You react to stimuli with little thinking in between.
>
> *   **Conformist (Level 1)** – Black and white thinking. You follow rules and obey authority without questioning. _You adopt others perspective._
>
> *   **Individualist (Level 2)** – Critical thinking emerges and you construct your own model. _You build your own perspective._
>
> *   **Synthesist (Level 3)** – You see your model as one among many. You hold contradictions and use perspectives as tools rather than law or some unquestionable truth.
>
> *   **Generative (Level 4)** – You create original perspectives that didn’t exist before, or you come to ideas without outside influence.
>
> Levels 1 and 2 can be considered “first tier” thinking, which is overly dogmatic. I’m right you’re wrong.
>
> Levels 3 and 4 can be considered “second tier” thinking, which reject dogma and seek the ultimate truth that lies in the middle.
>
> In the domain of meaning, you are often indoctrinated into one belief system, then you question that belief system and come to your own conclusions (often rejecting the prior), then you start to see truths in all belief systems, and finally you have the ability to create a more expansive one.
>
> In politics, much of the fighting and violence we see is do to the general population operating within levels 1 and 2. My group vs yours. Neither side can think beyond their bubble.
>
> Again, and I want to make this exceedingly clear because it is such an easy trap to fall into:
>
> Genius thinking is the ability to continue thinking.
>
> At each level of thinking, your ability to navigate that space of both known and unknown ideas continues to expand. When you are born, you don’t _really_ think. When you are a conformist, you think until you reach a point where you have “the” answer, then you start _defending_ that answer. When you are an individualist, you do the same thing, but with _your_ answer. When you reach synthesist, you can think far and wide, but your ability to _create new lines of thought_ is not fully developed yet.
>
> Reaching your highest ability to think, and thus providing the runway for your highest potential in this life, boils down to the simple practice of _noticing when your mind feels threatened, being honest with yourself, and at minimum, staying open to new perspectives._
>
> **Altitude** of thinking is the average of all your levels.
>
> Altitude represents cross-dimensional development (height).

> To best understand this, and to _almost_ complete the thinking puzzle, it helps to visualize thinking as a skill tree.
>
> In a video game, you can put “points” into certain traits that allow you to do more inside the game. You can take on higher challenges, have more fun, and continue playing. The same holds true with the mind.
>
> The thing is, higher level traits can only be unlocked once you meet a specific requirement of lower level traits. You can’t access “level 3” of dexterity without reaching “level 4” of strength, as an example.
>
> In the real world, imagine you started a business. You learned everything about marketing, sales, and product. You’re verifiably smart. You make a good amount of money. But then you try to take it further, and you hit an invisible road block. You can’t identify what’s wrong and then blame it on what you know: “the market just isn’t big enough,” or some permutation of that. Your mind is closed and there is no way forward.
>
> Further, business isn’t all there is to life, obviously. If the rest of your life is falling apart, you, as a business person, will try to jam a circle into a square hole. You may relieve the issue temporarily, but you never understand the root cause.
>
> > This is mostly why the domains of politics, religion, and others like nutrition are so hostile and polarizing. It’s a bunch of smart but dumb people calling each other idiots because they’ve stopped thinking and only defend what they believe is the one right way.
>
> Back to business, the problem is that you can’t see the actual problem. It could be that you don’t know how to lead a team. It could be a time management issue. It could even be a spiritual issue, but you’re too business minded to even entertain that possibility. At a minimum, that perspective could birth the insight that leads to further progress. This is why generalists and polymaths win.
>
> In other words, you can’t reach Level 3 of business because you haven’t “put points” into the other required domains that would unlock that level.

> ### **How to unlock 4-dimensional thinking**
>
> There is a fourth dimension to thinking.
>
> Well, it’s not really a fourth dimension like time, but it does increase your ability to think by the power of four.
>
> You see, there are 4 “dimensions” of reality.
>
> These dimensions are perspectives. You can change how you look at a belief, idea, situation, or problem in 4 major ways.
>
> [
>
> ![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!iIt8!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe03a144f-9228-4dcc-80f2-48d0cfc99f8f_1000x1000.png)

>
> There’s the inner world and the outer world, and there’s collective and individual segments of both.
>
> The **inner world** is mental. By that I mean psychological and cultural. Your _individual_ inner psychology contains your thoughts, emotions, beliefs, and consciousness. The _collective_ inner world, or culture, contains group beliefs, value systems, and ideologies.
>
> The **outer world** is physical, which sounds less woo-woo, but you can’t deny that you can think about the mental, which is what we’re here to talk about. Your _individual_ outer world is your visible appearance, behaviors, and physical brain states or measurements. The _collective_ outer world is composed of systems, structures, and social institutions.
>
> To illustrate the power of understanding this, let’s walk through a thinking progression.
>
> *   I learn a lot about marketing and use that knowledge (line of thinking)
>
> *   I practice, fail, and experience enough to understand that there isn’t “one proven framework,” but I have such an understanding that I can create outperforming marketing campaigns of my own (levels of thinking)
>
> *   I pursue interests like psychology, fitness, and personal development which gives me deeper pattern recognition that further removes me from dogmatic frameworks (altitude of thinking)
>
> From there, things start to get interesting, and it can go in so many different ways.
>
> One thing we forgot to mention is that thinking is usually done for the sake of solving a problem. That’s what humans do. That’s how we grow. That’s what we find fulfillment in. And the minute you stop growing (and thus thinking), problems multiply and life becomes ever more chaotic.
>
> So, if we identify a problem like “the world is becoming corrupt and meaningless,” we can continue our thinking in that direction.
>
> How does society control attention and market their ideas to the masses? (collective inner world)
>
> How can your behavior change, more than it already has during this process, to have a positive impact on society? What would be the steps to reaching such a point of influence that you can leave your mark? (individual outer world)
>
> What does the current job market look like? Is a job an actual path to achieving such a thing? What about AI? Can I utilize the technology available to me to have that impact? (collective outer world)
>
> Am I thinking about this the right way? Or is there more? What am I missing, and how does this make me feel? Motivated? Inspired? Hateful? (individual inner world)
>
> We could go on and on, but I think you get the point.
>
> Genius thinking is the ability to direct attention in a useful direction. You gradually encourage your mind to zoom further and further out, and from there you have a more enlightened vantage point to zoom in on the original problem you sought to solve.
>
> ### **How to tap into the 5th dimension**
>
> > It is no measure of health to be well‑adjusted to a profoundly sick society.
> >
> > – Krishnamurti
>
> Yes, I know, this isn’t actually the 5th dimension.
>
> It’s the fourth dimension (time), but I’m trying to practice my thinking here by making things up, and when you apply time to all four dimensions of reality we just talked about, your ability to think continues to rocket upward.
>
> In all previous examples, we never tapped into the power of history. And if you understand history, specifically the evolutionary patterns rather than memorizing the events, you can _aim_ your thinking in a more accurate direction.
>
> This illustrates the difference between knowing and understanding. You can know a lot of facts about specific moments in history, and that’s a great thing, but if you can’t zoom out to see the essence of what that moment represents, you may fail to spot that pattern across different domains.
>
> There are 5 things you need to know to begin thinking effectively about history. These aren’t exhaustive, and there is far more to it, but this is a good enough starting point.
>
> **1) The master pattern is Transcend and Include**
>
> A materialist would say that reality is composed of atoms, and they’d be correct.
>
> A mentalist would say that reality is composed of qualia, and they’d be correct.
>
> But if we zoom out for the sake of _understanding_, the pattern is clear that reality is composed of whole parts (wholes that are also parts of a greater whole).
>
> Matter → Life → Mind
>
> Word → Sentence → Paragraph
>
> Machine → Computer → Artificial Intelligence
>
> Human → House → City → State → Country → Continent → Planet → Solar system → Galaxy
>
> Each is a whole that transcends and includes the one before it. Meaning, if you remove the thing that allows another thing to exist, the entire chain self-destructs (which is why environmentalists are so worried about climate change. If the biosphere collapses, humans go down with it).
>
> This will make sense with examples.
>
> **2) How individuals evolve physically**
>
> This one is simple since most people can see it, touch it, smell it, etc.
>
> Atoms → Cells → Molecules → Organs → Organism
>
> You can think about the physical world by how individual things develop, not just humans.
>
> **3) How individuals evolve mentally**
>
> Humans develop through an increasing circle of care.
>
> First, we care about ourselves and our survival (egocentric).
>
> Then, we care about our tribe, group, or culture (ethnocentric).
>
> Next, we care about all groups regardless of differences (worldcentric), but since the master pattern is _transcend and include,_ we still care about ourselves and our tribe, and that must be accounted for in our thinking. We do not sacrifice ourselves or our values simply because caring about others sounds virtuous. This is usually where the arguments between most groups happen, because some use “caring about the equality of everyone” as a moral high ground that can often be self-destructive, which is counterproductive to caring about everyone.
>
> At the most developed levels, we care about reality as a the ultimate whole. This clearly requires one to be able to think through vast amounts of complexity to come away with anything practical.
>
> **4) How societies evolve physically**
>
> Social structures follow technology.
>
> We went from tribes to villages with the invention of the hoe that enabled small scale farming.
>
> We went from villages to empires with the invention of the horse-drawn plow (because a surplus of food allowed people to focus less on farming and more on exploring, discovering and conquering.)
>
> We went from empires to nation-states with the invention of machine agriculture.
>
> Now, with computers in our pockets and AI at our doorstep, I’ll let you think about where things could go.
>
> **5) How societies evolve mentally**
>
> Since individuals compose the collective, societies evolve through a similar pattern of egocentric, ethnocentric, and worldcentric.
>
> Collectives have an identity too.
>
> The tribe can only care about itself and it’s survival. Then, it can expand into a larger empire or nation-state, holding multiple sub-groups within it.
>
> In this case, we call these worldviews pre-rational, rational, and post-rational. First we conformed to authority, then we sought progress through science (the enlightenment), then we began to realize the blind spots of both while integrating the good parts.
>
> But how would you actually use this knowledge to “think?”
>
> Here’s a few ways out of _many:_
>
> 1.  Identify where individuals are when you encounter an idea, movement, or conflict. Who do you want as a politician when entering the age of AI?
>
> 2.  When engaging in debate (political, business, personal) notice the level people are operating from. Individuals at higher levels of development often need to meet people where they are.
>
> 3.  Look at the technology of societies and organizations. A business still operating on industrial era methods (and are too dogmatic to adapt) will probably fail.
>
> The most useful application is to locate where your own center of gravity is. I would encourage you to reread this letter at another time while thinking about your own development.
>
> ### **We missed something important**
>
> Stupid thinking is when you stop thinking.
>
> We know that.
>
> But what makes a person stop thinking? What makes them turn from open to defensive, closing themselves off to further learning and growth?
>
> The answer is identity.
>
> Specifically, latching onto ideas, beliefs, or constructs and making them a part of who you are.
>
> Because if you are a diehard republican or democrat, you will never be able to think outside of that bubble. Therefore, you will never be able to find the truth of the situation. That doesn’t mean that you can’t adopt certain values from either side, it simply means that you don’t need to feel threatened when someone else disagrees with you.
>
> The same applies to identifying with a specific religion, group, vocation, or anything else we’ve discussed prior.
>
> You must also consider how you came to those beliefs.
>
> Most people, in today’s world, have never thought for themselves a single day in their life.
>
> They were born into a specific part of the world.
>
> That part of the world was at a specific level of development.
>
> They were told what to believe by parents who probably hadn’t questioned their own beliefs.
>
> Those beliefs shaped how far you could think, which probably wasn’t very far.
>
> And remember, your mind is how you interact with reality.
>
> You process information → make sense of it (thinking) → make a choice → receive information as feedback → respond to that feedback and repeat the cycle.
>
> The space in which you can think determines the outcome of your life.
>
> If you only think within the confines set by your parents, teachers, employer, and peers on social media, the outcome of your life may not be that pretty. Just observe where the average person ends up and it’s not hard to see.
>
> So, the next time you feel threatened, do not collapse in on yourself.
>
> Sit with it. Question it. Realize that your mind wants to feel safe, because that’s how it’s wired.
>
> Our brain is still wired like our hunter-gatherer ancestors, but we do not live in that world anymore, and the world will only change faster.
>
> Those who practice thinking will win.
