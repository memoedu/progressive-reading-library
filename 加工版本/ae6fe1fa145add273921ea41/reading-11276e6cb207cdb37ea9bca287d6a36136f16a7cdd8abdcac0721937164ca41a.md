# Boston should begin aggressive coronavirus mitigation

---

## 正文

_TL;DR：有限的 public evidence 表明，Boston 很可能已经未能 containing the coronavirus。州政府官员应该开始更透明地沟通 outbreak 的当前 status，而市政府应该取消大型活动，尤其是 St. Patrick’s Day 游行。如果你同意，请致电你选出的官员，要求更好的沟通和 aggressive mitigation measures。_

（[Epistemic status](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=Epistemic%20Status)：我不是 epidemiologist，我只是喜欢 extrapolating exponential growth。）

* * *

今天，Mass. Department of Public Health [披露](https://www.mass.gov/news/15-new-presumptive-positive-cases-of-covid-19-identified-by-massachusetts-state-public-health)了新增的 _fifteen_ 个 COVID-19 presumptive positive cases，这些 cases 全都与“2 月下旬的 Biogen 员工会议有 direct connection”，使 Massachusetts “与 Biogen 会议 associated”的 cases 总数达到 23。

一些观察：![](https://imgs.xkcd.com/comics/business_greetings.png)

-   最初的说法是，三名从州外前来参会的人带入了 coronavirus。如果这是真的，而且另外全部 20 个 cases 都是他们亲自 infected 的，那么这种 rapid transmission 就快得极其出人意料——R0 为 7，而其他来源 estimated 的是 1.5-3。（难道他们在互相舔眼球？）Naively，更好的 explained 方式是，此后还发生了单独的第二轮（或更多轮）transmission。

-   与会议有“direct connection”究竟是什么意思，目前并不清楚。想必它并不只包括参会者，否则他们就会说“参加过”。相反，其中一些人似乎很可能与会议有其他联系；最有可能的是，他们是在会议举办地 Mariott Long Wharf 工作的人。Biogen 会议于 2 月 24-27 日举行，但[3 月 6 日有 60 人从 Marriott 被送往 Brigham and Women’s](https://www.reddit.com/r/boston/comments/fejbe1/60_people_sent_to_brigham_and_womens_from_marriot/)——所以这 60 人很可能是 Marriott 员工。

-   3 月 6 日也是 Biogen [宣布](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/biogen-reports-3-covid-19-cases-following-boston-management-meeting-will-other-pharmas-crack)已要求全体员工“居家工作两周”的那一天，当天还公布了最初三个 conference-related cases。居家工作期何时开始并不清楚，但如果远早于那一天，我会感到意外，因为 Marriott 的 testing 就发生在同一天。这也表明，Marriott 员工在 3 月 6 日之前并未被 isolated。

-   Mariott Long Wharf 在 [LinkedIn 上有 348 名员工](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=mariott%20long%20wharf&origin=TYPEAHEAD_ESCAPE_HATCH)。（实际人数可能高得多，这取决于诸如清洁人员使用 LinkedIn 的可能性有多大。）Massachusetts 目前正在 testing [每天 50 人](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/boston-hospitals-expect-coronavirus-test-results-saturday/2087227/)。考虑到我住酒店时会与多少员工发生 close contact，目前接受 tested 的人数足够的可能性似乎实在很小。

-   [截至 3 月 3 日，Massachusetts 正在被 actively quarantined 的人 _总共_ 只有 249 名](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/covid-19-cases-quarantine-and-monitoring)。这个数字似乎低得太多，不足以包括所有可能在酒店 exposed 的人。现在被 quarantined 的人可能更多了；这些数字每周才更新一次。

* * *

在没有其他信息的情况下，这看起来确实令人担忧：在一个有非常非常多人互相 contact 的场所，发生了多轮 transmission，而充当 mediated 环节的人[很可能薪酬低到](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Marriott_Hotels_strike)有强烈动机在 symptomatic 时仍然来上班。再加上 testing throughput 太低，_实在_ 太低，根本无法 screen 截至目前 virus 可能已经波及的所有人。

当然，DPH／市政府／等等可能掌握更多信息，让他们觉得这件事没那么令人担忧。也许 Marriott Long Wharf 员工在会议结束后立即就被 quarantined 了。也许 testing throughput 已经大幅提高。也许 investigators 正在勤勉地 tracking down 并 testing 每一个曾在 Marriott 工作的人（而不只是那些已经有 symptoms 的人），以及他们接触过的每一个人。

但如果确实有什么尚未公开、足以让人安心的理由，那么 DPH 官员就应该公布它。到目前为止，他们对 outbreak 的 status 一直极其含糊——如果我已经沦落到要引用 Reddit 来获取信息，你就能看出来。有多少 Marriott Long Wharf 员工是 confirmed positive？还有多少人在 experiencing symptoms？有多少其他 Marriott 住客曾与 potentially-symptomatic 员工发生 contact？这些住客是否正在被通知，或被要求 isolate？如果 DPH 对这些问题有令人安心的答案，他们就应该告诉我们。

但更有可能的是，Massachusetts 官员与从 [CDC](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/h4vWsBBjASgiQ2pn6/credibility-of-the-cdc-on-sars-cov-2) 到 [Iran](https://www.axios.com/iran-coronavirus-deaths-cases-information-blocked-f82db0e7-da70-4e9c-b72d-54e7f7f6ed9b.html) 的官员一样，只是非常想告诉大家一切安好——因此，他们正在 underreacting，以便让别人，也可能让他们自己，相信这一点。其他地方就是这样应对 outbreaks 的；如果 Mass DPH 应对这次 outbreak 的方式有所不同，那就有责任证明这一点。

* * *

如果 _并没有_ 什么尚未公开、足以让人安心的理由，那么 Boston 就已经离开 epidemic 的 containment phase，进入 mitigation phase。幸运的是，我们可以看看那些 outbreaks 进程领先于我们的国家，从而窥见未来。要看 worst case，可以看 Italy：16 天前，它的 known cases 还少于 MA。现在，Lombardy 的 medical system 已经 over capacity，16 million 人被 quarantined，350 人已经 died——其中超过一半是在过去一天内（[来源](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2020/03/italy-poised-seal-north-coronavirus-live-updates-200307234942500.html)）。

为了防止这里发生同样的事，我们需要采取尽可能多的措施，以 [flatten the epidemic curve](https://ourworldindata.org/coronavirus#flattening-the-curve)：

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/preview_hu3ca1d928c88bd0bc1be8d7b5997bde2b_152367_1000x414_resize_q75_box.jpg)](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/preview.jpg "full size")

而我们目前[下周日举行百万人游行](https://boston.cbslocal.com/2019/03/17/south-boston-st-patricks-day-parade-2019-2/)的计划，呃，_简直就是在完全反其道而行之_。

到目前为止，卫生官员似乎一直不太擅长将 exponential trends extrapolating 到未来几天以外。这可以理解——那需要想象自己从未经历过的事情，比如 hospital beds 或 ventilators 耗尽。幸运的是，这一次我们不必 extrapolate；我们可以从过去吸取教训，因为以前就有人在 uncontained epidemic 期间尝试过举行游行这一 _完全相同的 strategy_。

那是 1918 年的 Philadelphia，下面是当时的 death rate，并附上一个没那么疯狂的城市作 comparison（[来源](https://www.pnas.org/content/104/18/7582)）：

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/parade_hu38b5ca52c55269a193a05c9df176fdae_239264_1000x628_resize_box_3.png)](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/parade.png "full size")

没错，death rate 就这样立即在一周内 going up 了大约 5x（目测）。在 Philadelphia，这立即 overwhelmed 了 healthcare system（[来源](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/philadelphia-threw-wwi-parade-gave-thousands-onlookers-flu-180970372/)）；COVID-19 的 incubates more slowly，因此我们应该预期一个 more gradual ramp-up，但两者总体上约为 equally infectious（两者的 R0 都在 2 左右），这 suggesting 它们会有 similar overall effect size。

* * *

事实上，Boston _自己_ 也在 1918 年早些时候举办过一场大型军事集会，这很可能帮助 disease 从最初在军队中的 foothold spread 到 civilian population（[来源](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/features/influenza-boston/)）——几天后，平民就开始 dying 了（[图片来源](http://www.medicalheritage.org/2018/10/10/guest-posts-iv-the-situation-has-become-serious-the-1918-influenza-epidemic-in-the-boston-medical-and-surgical-journal/)）：

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/boston-parade_hue9aa86959197810178966a4ab32ab45c_162344_1000x582_resize_box_3.png)](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/boston-parade.png "full size")

同一个城市如果无视 1918 年的教训，任由一场百万人游行 accelerate the epidemic，那将是可悲的愚蠢。如果你同意，我建议打电话给当地政界人士，告诉他们，开始 aggressive mitigation measures 的时候本该是昨天。

（补充：不要把这篇文章当作你个人应该恐慌的 evidence——即使在糟糕的情况下，任何 individual 的 risk 似乎仍然相当 low。你确实应该为州政府那软弱得不可思议的应对而愤怒；但不要彻底变成末日生存准备狂。）

## 原文

_TL;DR: The limited public evidence suggests Boston has likely failed at containing the coronavirus. State officials should start communicating more transparently about the current status of the outbreak, and the city should cancel large events, in particular the St. Patrick’s Day parade. If you agree, call your elected officials to ask for better communication and aggressive mitigation measures._

([Epistemic status](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=Epistemic%20Status): I’m not an epidemiologist, I just like extrapolating exponential growth.)

* * *

Today, the Mass. Department of Public Health [disclosed](https://www.mass.gov/news/15-new-presumptive-positive-cases-of-covid-19-identified-by-massachusetts-state-public-health) an additional _fifteen_ presumptive positive cases of COVID-19, all of which had a “direct connection to the Biogen employee conference in late February,” bringing the total number of Massachusetts cases “associated with the Biogen conference” to 23.

Some observations: ![](https://imgs.xkcd.com/comics/business_greetings.png)

-   The original story was that three people who attended the conference from out of state brought in the coronavirus. If that’s true, and they personally infected all 20 other cases, that’s extremely surprisingly rapid transmission—an R0 of 7, vs the 1.5-3 estimated from other sources. (Maybe they were licking each others’ eyeballs?) Naively, this would be better explained by there being a separate, second (or more) round of transmission afterwards.

-   It’s unclear what a “direct connection” to the conference means. Presumably, it doesn’t only include conference attendees, or they would have said “attended.” Instead, it seems likely that some of these people had other connections; most plausibly, they were workers at the Mariott Long Wharf where the conference was held. The Biogen conference was held on February 24-27, but [60 people were transported from the Marriott to Brigham and Women’s on March 6](https://www.reddit.com/r/boston/comments/fejbe1/60_people_sent_to_brigham_and_womens_from_marriot/)—so those 60 were likely Marriott staff.

-   March 6th was the same day Biogen [announced](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/biogen-reports-3-covid-19-cases-following-boston-management-meeting-will-other-pharmas-crack) they’d requested all employees to “work from home for two weeks,” when the first three conference-related cases were announced. It’s unclear when the work-from-home period started, but I’d be surprised if it was well before that, since the Marriott testing happened the same day. That also suggests that the Marriott staff weren’t isolated before March 6th.

-   The Mariott Long Wharf has [348 employees on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=mariott%20long%20wharf&origin=TYPEAHEAD_ESCAPE_HATCH). (The real number could be much higher depending on how likely e.g. cleaning staff are to use LinkedIn.) Massachusetts is currently testing [50 people per day](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/boston-hospitals-expect-coronavirus-test-results-saturday/2087227/). Given the number of employees that I come into close contact with when I stay at hotels, it seems really unlikely that sufficient numbers of people are being tested.

-   [As of March 3, only 249 individuals _total_ were actively quarantined in Massachusetts](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/covid-19-cases-quarantine-and-monitoring). That seems way too low to include everyone who could have been exposed at the hotel. It’s possible that more are being quarantined now; the numbers are only updated once weekly.

* * *

By default, this seems really worrying: multiple rounds of transmission, in a space where lots and lots of people come into contact, mediated by people who are [likely underpaid enough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Marriott_Hotels_strike) that they have a strong incentive to show up for work while symptomatic. Plus testing throughput that’s way, _way_ too low to screen everyone the virus could have gotten to by now.

Of course, it’s possible that the DPH/city administration/etc. has more information that would make this seem less worrying to them. Maybe Marriott Long Wharf staff were quarantined immediately after the conference. Maybe testing throughput has gone up a lot. Maybe investigators are diligently tracking down and testing everyone who worked at the Marriott (not just people who already have symptoms) and everyone they interacted with.

But if there’s a hidden reason to be reassured, then DPH officials should be reporting it. So far, they’ve been extremely vague about the status of the outbreak—as you can tell if I’m reduced to citing Reddit for information. How many Marriott Long Wharf staff are confirmed positive? How many others are experiencing symptoms? How many other Marriott guests had contact with potentially-symptomatic staff? Are those guests being notified or asked to isolate? If the DPH has reassuring answers to these questions, they should tell us.

But it seems more likely that Massachusetts officials, like officials from the [CDC](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/h4vWsBBjASgiQ2pn6/credibility-of-the-cdc-on-sars-cov-2) to [Iran](https://www.axios.com/iran-coronavirus-deaths-cases-information-blocked-f82db0e7-da70-4e9c-b72d-54e7f7f6ed9b.html), just really want to tell people everything is fine—and so they’re underreacting in order to convince others, and perhaps themselves, of that. That’s how outbreaks have been handled elsewhere, and if the Mass DPH is handling this one differently, the burden is on them to prove it.

* * *

If there _isn’t_ any hidden reason to be reassured, then Boston is out of the containment phase of the epidemic and into the mitigation phase. Fortunately, we can glimpse the future by looking at other countries whose outbreaks are ahead of ours. For a worst case, there’s Italy, which 16 days ago had fewer known cases than MA. Now, Lombardy’s medical system is over capacity, 16 million people are quarantined, and 350 have died—more than half in the last day ([source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2020/03/italy-poised-seal-north-coronavirus-live-updates-200307234942500.html)).

To prevent that from happening here, we need to be taking as many measures as possible to [flatten the epidemic curve](https://ourworldindata.org/coronavirus#flattening-the-curve):

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/preview_hu3ca1d928c88bd0bc1be8d7b5997bde2b_152367_1000x414_resize_q75_box.jpg)](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/preview.jpg "full size")

And our current plan of [holding a million-person parade next Sunday](https://boston.cbslocal.com/2019/03/17/south-boston-st-patricks-day-parade-2019-2/), is, uh, _literally the exact opposite of that_.

So far, health officials haven’t seemed very good at extrapolating exponential trends more than a couple days into the future. It’s understandable—that requires imagining something that you’ve never experienced before, like running out of hospital beds or ventilators. Fortunately, in this case we don’t have to extrapolate; we can learn from the past, because someone tried the _exact strategy_ of having a parade during an uncontained epidemic before.

It was Philadelphia in 1918, and here’s what the death rate looked like, with a less insane city for comparison ([source](https://www.pnas.org/content/104/18/7582)):

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/parade_hu38b5ca52c55269a193a05c9df176fdae_239264_1000x628_resize_box_3.png)](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/parade.png "full size")

Yup, that’s the death rate immediately going up by (eyeballing) around 5x in one week. In Philadelphia this immediately overwhelmed the healthcare system ([source](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/philadelphia-threw-wwi-parade-gave-thousands-onlookers-flu-180970372/)); COVID-19 incubates more slowly, so we should expect a more gradual ramp-up, but they’re about equally infectious overall (both with an R0 of around 2) suggesting a similar overall effect size.

* * *

In fact, Boston _itself_ also threw a large military rally earlier in 1918 which likely played helped the disease spread from its initial, military, foothold into the civilian population([source](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/features/influenza-boston/))—civilians started dying a few days after it ([image source](http://www.medicalheritage.org/2018/10/10/guest-posts-iv-the-situation-has-become-serious-the-1918-influenza-epidemic-in-the-boston-medical-and-surgical-journal/)):

[![](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/boston-parade_hue9aa86959197810178966a4ab32ab45c_162344_1000x582_resize_box_3.png)](https://www.benkuhn.net/corona/boston-parade.png "full size")

It would be tragically dumb for the same city to ignore the lessons from 1918 and allow a million-person parade to accelerate the epidemic. If you agree, I’d suggest calling your local politicians and telling them that yesterday is the time to start aggressive mitigation measures.

(Addendum: don’t take this post as evidence that you should panic personally—risk to any individual still seems fairly low even in bad cases. Do be angry at the incredibly anemic state response; don’t go full-on prepper.)
