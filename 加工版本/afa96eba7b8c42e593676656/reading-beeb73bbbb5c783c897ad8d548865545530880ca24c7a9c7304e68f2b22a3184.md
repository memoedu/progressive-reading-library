我接下来要发表的文章将讨论 Mormons，尝试看看哪些因素与成为其中一员 correlates，并希望能看出哪些因素与此 causally related。我将首次公开所有用于 analysing 这些 data 以及 producing 众多 figures 和 charts 的 code，这些 figures 和 charts 构成了 Nintil 标志性风格的一部分。这也将适用于今后所有的 code，而且它已经放在 OpenNintil 的 [github repo](https://github.com/jlricon/open_nintil) 中。目前我只有下一篇文章的 working code（距离 complete 还很远），但原则上，它会让任何人都能直接从 sources 开始 replicate 我所做的一切。

另一篇正在筹备的文章是关于 blockchain 的，我会尝试解释为什么人们对此的兴奋程度超过了应有的程度。

最后，明年二月初（1 日至 9 日），我会去华盛顿（DC）、纽约和旧金山。如果有人想在见到自己最喜欢的博客作者时失望一下的话。

现在，来看链接文章：

关于[为 finance 辩护](https://putanumonit.com/2018/12/14/defense-of-finance/)这篇文章，我总体上赞同，但有一些 caveats：我确实认为，finance industry 目前的形态过于 bloated，需要大力 reform，才能更好地为所有人服务，并 reduce 它促成 financial crises 的倾向。一个 healthy 的 finance system 不会存在 large scale maturity transformation，而这种 function 如今被广泛认为是 banks 所做之事的本质。但这其实是近来才出现的东西，只有在 banks 被赋予 incentivises 它们承担更大 risks 的特权之后才产生：Bank deposits 用于 financing mortgages 或 industrial loans，是一种 aberration。当然，可以用 regulations 来改善这一点，但为什么不 address 其 root cause 呢？让 banks fail（对它们 Apply bail-in，而不是 bail-out），以及 abolishing deposit insurance，会是不错的起点。\[Universal brain\]

来一点 cryptonihilism：[blockchain applications 的 public success rate 为 0%](https://www.theregister.co.uk/2018/11/30/blockchain_study_finds_0_per_cent_success_rate/)，这来自一项针对几家自称正在使用 distributed ledgers 的 firms 的 survey。几乎没有 evidence 表明它在实际环境中有 successful uses，当然也有通常那些 caveats：如果愿意算上的话，有 Cryptokitties，或者 [Santander OneFX](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FZv1W0bNrlY) payments platform。这是迄今最 successful 的 Blockchain application，却没有人听说过它。

美国的 [Inequality](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2018/12/increases-inequality-overestimated.html) 被 overestimated 了，同样，non-rich 的 income stagnation 也被 overstated 了。MR 文章中链接的 report 解释了为什么会这样。从某种意义上说，归根结底还是那些老问题：definitions，以及针对不同 factors 以不同方式进行的 adjustments。

[《The mind illuminated》](http://slatestarcodex.com/2018/11/28/book-review-the-mind-illuminated/)，一篇 SSC 的 book review。这至少是 Scott 写的第二篇，而我觉得它确实很有意思。我绝对应该试试 meditation。

[一篇关于 Radical Markets 的好 review](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3163831)——迄今最好的 review——。和此前的其他 review 一样，作者发现，Radical Markets 的作者们有所涉猎（但并未 endorse）的那个“markets in human capital” idea 存在问题。聚焦于这个特定 case 可能显得不公平，而且书中并未详细讨论它，作者们也不 endorse 它。不过，考虑到它似乎是这本书的 philosophical commitments 的 logical extension，我对此持不同意见。这就像 organ transplant cases 与 utilitarianism，或者 liar 与 Kant 的关系一样。Weyl 和一位 coauthor（Zoë Hitzig\_\_）作出了[回应](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3306738&download=yes)。

women 和 single men 的 Wages 在他们一生中都是 similar 的。[是 married](https://www.stlouisfed.org/on-the-economy/2018/december/married-men-outearn-single-men) 的 men 才会 outearn 其他所有人。

study 发现，Feelings[对你有害](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4050437/)（r reasoning）。感谢 [Alex Tabarrok](https://twitter.com/ATabarrok/status/1076223910448955392) 提供线索

science 中的 Bias：[没有](https://psyarxiv.com/r2xvb)。一项 study 将一批署有虚构 authors（不同 race 和/或 gender）的 papers 送交 412 位 scientists 进行 review，没有在那里发现 bias。请注意，这只是一项 study（记得 Moss-Racusin study），所以，和往常一样，这又增加了一点 evidence，但并非 definitive 的 evidence。

labour market 中的 Bias：有 evidence 表明，过去可能存在[针对 gays 和 lesbians 的 bias](https://blogs.worldbank.org/impactevaluations/same-sex-marriage-employment-and-discrimination-guest-post-dario-sansone)。

DeepMind 的 10 人 teams 证明比 protein structure prediction 这一 field 的所有人合在一起的脑力还要出色，介绍文章在[这里](https://moalquraishi.wordpress.com/2018/12/09/alphafold-casp13-what-just-happened/)。如果这意味着 science 中还存在某个 scholars 的 community 尚未发现的 substantial unrealised gains，那看来是件好事。

一场关于 Friston 的 free energy principle 的 merits 的 debate。[Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/karl-friston-free-energy-principle-artificial-intelligence/) 上有一篇介绍，而 [SSC 上](http://slatestarcodex.com/2018/03/04/god-help-us-lets-try-to-understand-friston-on-free-energy/)有一篇好得多的、讨论相关 ideas 的 review。[Nostalgebraist](https://nostalgebraist.tumblr.com/post/171651247714/comments-on-the-friston-free-energy-stuff-that) 提出了一篇 critique。

一篇针对 Prum 关于 sexual selection 的书的 [Critical review（批判性书评）](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/evo.13629)。Prum argued，sexual selection 仅由 beauty 所 driven，而非 good genes（也就是说，其中几乎没有或完全没有涉及 fitness 的 signaling）。这篇 review argues，Prum 的 theory 并不像他所声称的那样是被遗忘的——我同意这样一种 claim：good-genes theory 很 widespread，而且往往在没有 enough evidence 的情况下就被 assumed；我也同意，确实几乎没有支持它的 evidence，但 Prum 的 theory 也不应该被 accepted by default。

Collection of papers and articles that I’ve spotted since my previous links post that seem interesting.

![Screenshot from 2018-12-26 19-22-52.png](https://nintil.com/old_assets/2018/12/28-1.png)

Only one post I've written this year is among the most read: On the constancy of the rate of GDP growth, the rest are basically the Soviet Series and some very old posts in spanish. Visits this year (not counting visits that came from search engines) came mostly from twitter, followed by Marginal Revolution, Facebook, and Reddit, and then SSC, Patrick Collison's blog, and Alexey Guzey's.

Next post I'll be posting will be about Mormons, and trying to see what correlates, and hopefully, what is causally related with, being one. For the first time, I'm going to release all the code that goes into analysing the data and producing the many figures and charts that forms part of Nintil's signature style. This will apply to all future code and it is up in the OpenNintil  [github repo](https://github.com/jlricon/open_nintil) already. Currently I only have the working code for the next post (still far from complete), but in principle it would allow anyone to replicate all I do straight from the sources.

Another one in the pipeline is about blockchain, with an attempted explanation for why are people more excited than they should.

Finally, next year in early February (1st to 9th) I'll be visiting Washington(DC), New York, and San Francisco. In case anyone wanted to be disappointed when meeting the author of their favourite blog.

Now, the links post:

In [defense of finance](https://putanumonit.com/2018/12/14/defense-of-finance/) , I agree with the post in general with some caveats: I do think the finance industry is bloated in its current form, and that it is in need of heavy reform to make it work better for everyone, and reduce how prone it is to contribute to financial crises. A healthy finance system is one where there is no large scale maturity transformation, a function now widely believed to be the essence of what banks do. But this is a recent invention that arose only after banks were granted privileged that incentivises them to take on greater risks: Bank deposits financing mortgages or industrial loans is an aberration. Sure, that can be improved with regulations, but why not address the root cause? Letting banks fail (Apply a bail-in to them instead of a bail-out), and abolishing deposit insurance would be good starting points. \[Universal brain\]

Some cryptonihilism: [0% public success rate in blockchain applications](https://www.theregister.co.uk/2018/11/30/blockchain_study_finds_0_per_cent_success_rate/), from a survey of a few firms that said they were using distributed ledgers.. There is little evidence of successful uses of it in the wild,w ith the usual caveats, Cryptokitties if one is willing to consider that, or the [Santander OneFX](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FZv1W0bNrlY) payments platform. This is the most successful Blockchain application so far and no one has heard of it.

[Inequality](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2018/12/increases-inequality-overestimated.html) in the US has been overestimated, and likewise income stagnation for the non-rich has been overstated. The report linked in the MR post explains why this is so. In a way it comes down to the usual: definitions and adjustments for different factors in different ways.

[The mind illuminated](http://slatestarcodex.com/2018/11/28/book-review-the-mind-illuminated/), a SSC book review. At least the second Scott does, and I find it really interesting. I definitely should try meditation.

[Good review of Radical Markets](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3163831) - the best review so far-. Like the others before it, the author finds issues with the "markets in human capital" idea that the the authors of Radical Markets flirt with (but do not endorse). It may seem unfair to focus on this particular case, and it is not discussed at length in the book and the authors do not endorse it. Yet, given that it seems to be a logical extension of the philosophical commitments of the book, I differ. This is just like the organ transplant cases and utilitarianism, or the liar and Kant. Weyl and a coauthor (Zoë Hitzig\_\_) [replies](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3306738&download=yes).

Wages of women and single men are similar throughout their lives. [It is married](https://www.stlouisfed.org/on-the-economy/2018/december/married-men-outearn-single-men)men who outearn everyone else.

Feelings[are bad for you](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4050437/)(r reasoning), study finds. ht/ to [Alex Tabarrok](https://twitter.com/ATabarrok/status/1076223910448955392)

Sam Bowman [criticises](https://twitter.com/s8mb/status/1073599175756013568) a relatively bad Vox article that supports an increase in the alcohol tax in the US.

Bias in science: [not](https://psyarxiv.com/r2xvb). A study sends a bunch of papers with faked authors (different race and/or gender) for review by 412 scientists, no bias found there. Note that this is one study (remember the Moss-Racusin study), so this is one more bit, but not definitive, evidence, as usual.

Bias in the labour market: evidence that there might have been, [against gays and lesbians](https://blogs.worldbank.org/impactevaluations/same-sex-marriage-employment-and-discrimination-guest-post-dario-sansone).

DeepMind teams of 10 people proves better than the combined brainpower of the field of protein structure prediction, writeup [here](https://moalquraishi.wordpress.com/2018/12/09/alphafold-casp13-what-just-happened/). This seems good if it's a pointer that there are substantial unrealised gains in science that a given community of scholars has not seen yet.

A debate over the merits of Friston's free energy principle. A presentation at [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/karl-friston-free-energy-principle-artificial-intelligence/) and a far better review of the relevant ideas [at SSC](http://slatestarcodex.com/2018/03/04/god-help-us-lets-try-to-understand-friston-on-free-energy/) . [Nostalgebraist](https://nostalgebraist.tumblr.com/post/171651247714/comments-on-the-friston-free-energy-stuff-that)offers a critique.

[Critical review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/evo.13629) of Prum's book on sexual selection. Prum argued that sexual selection is driven by beauty alone, not good genes (that is, there is little or no signaling of fitness involved). The review argues that Prum's theory is not a forgotten one (as he claimed), -agree with the claim that the good-genes theory is widespread and often assumed without enough evidence, and agree that indeed there is little evidence for it, but that Prum's theory should not be accepted by default either.

A [lengthy](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1078682801954799617.html) twitter thread, criticising Ethereum. A debate [here](https://www.reddit.com/r/ethereum/comments/aac4hr/tuurs_criticism_discussion_thread/).
