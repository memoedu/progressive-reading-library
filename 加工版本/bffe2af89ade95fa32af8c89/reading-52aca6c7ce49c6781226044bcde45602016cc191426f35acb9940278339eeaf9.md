A paper claimed to be able to regrow limbs in various organisms. A lab tried to [replicate](https://twitter.com/casa_tuthill/status/1585703476948258816) their experiment in flies and failed. Note that the original paper tried across a range of organisms whereas the replication only challenges the results in one of them.
