## 原文

December 2014

Reminder: if you live in the US and are planning to donate substantially (at least $3,000) to charity this year, consider bunching your donations by donating at the beginning of next year instead!

In some circumstances, this will give you a substantially larger average tax deduction (up to $6,000), allowing you to donate much more than you could otherwise. For details, see [the previous post I wrote on the subject](http://www.benkuhn.net/bunching).
