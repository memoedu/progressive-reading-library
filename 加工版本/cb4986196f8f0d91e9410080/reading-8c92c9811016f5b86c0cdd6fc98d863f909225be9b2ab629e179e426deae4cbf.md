Readers of this blog will know that I'm not a fan of Mariana Mazzucato's Entrepreneurial State.

But suppose I had to write that book, arguing the same thing Mazzucato argues. How would it look like? It would be something like a mixture of two existing sources:

-   The first is Josh Lerner's [Boulevard of Broken Dreams: Why public efforts to boost entrepreneurship and venture capital have failed, and what do to about it (2009)](http://press.princeton.edu/titles/8984.html).
-   NESTA report [How Innovation Agencies Work (2016)](http://www.nesta.org.uk/blog/art-and-craft-running-innovation-agency)
