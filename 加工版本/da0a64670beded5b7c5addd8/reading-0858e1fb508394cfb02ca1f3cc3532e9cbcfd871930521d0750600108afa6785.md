### Bodily redistribution（身体再分配）

Robin Hanson 就向 sex-deprived individuals（“incels”）进行 redistributing sex 发表了一些言论，随后“sex redistribution”这个 question 便引爆了互联网。互联网的反应相当可预料，也没什么意思。更有意思的是，将这看作一个 general trend 的又一个 case：人们一碰到某些令自己 emotionally charged 的 topics，rationality 就被抛到了九霄云外。

写作时，需要在 socially acceptable 与说出自己的想法之间作出 tradeoff。私下里，与思想开放的同伴交流时，第一个顾虑不成问题；但在 open fora 中，对 loss of social reputation 的恐惧可能会 stiffle discussion。Hanson 是 tenured professor，因此他其实不必担心；但那些没有受到如此保护、无法抵挡 public outrage 的人，就不得不再三考虑该如何表达自己的想法。被人称作“那个 sexist 的猥琐家伙”，甚至可能 hamper 自己的 career prospects，即便自己怀有任何类似 sexist beliefs 的想法。

我不认为这有什么解决办法。感情用事的人总会感情用事，所以我们很可能会看到，对 unusual ideas 的 discussion 逐渐离开公众视野，转向 anonymous blogs 和私人的 Slack 群聊；或许我们还会看到某种 shibboleth 的兴起，用来在公开场合 identify，别人究竟是可以与你开诚布公交谈的人，还是恰恰相反，你必须在他们面前恪守规定的 canon。

不过，对 Nintil 来说，sex redistribution 作为一个 abstract hypothetical 还不够刺激，所以让我们谈谈取走你的 kidneys，再把它们送给 kidneyless 的人。

在我看来，那些相当 widespread、会使人 endorsing income redistribution 的 moral views，并不会显然地在 body 开始的地方止步。[Rawlsians](https://nintil.com/2017/08/06/rise-and-fall-of-rawlsianism/) 尤其应该担心，所有持有“凡是不 deserved 的东西，都可以 unproblematically 地被 redistributed away”这一 view 的人也一样。我们的 friends 也不是我们 deserve 的，难道我们应该 redistribute friends 吗？

最近，moral philosopher [Cécile Fabre](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v2YczpQPPtM) 确实 made the case——而不只是随便想想——认为在这种情况下，income redistribution 的确 imply organ redistribution。Eric Mack 是她在这场 debate 中的 opponent，他将这看作对支持 income redistribution 的 arguments 的 reductio。

### Bodily redistribution

The "sex redistribution" question has gotten internet on fire, after Robin Hanson made some remarks regarding redistributing sex towards sex-deprived individuals ("incels"). Internet's reaction was quite predictable, an uninteresting. More interesting is seeing this as one more case in the general trend that is people's rationality going out the window around certain topics that are emotionally charged for them.

When writing, there is a tradeoff to be made between being socially acceptable and saying what you think. In private and among openminded peers, the first concern is not an issue, but in open fora the fear of loss of social reputation can stiffle discussion. Hanson is a tenured professor so he doesn't really have to worry, but those that are not so shield from public outrage have to think twice about how to say what they say. Being known as "that sexist creepy dude" could conceivably even hamper one's own career prospects, even when one harbours anything resembling sexist beliefs.

I don't think there is a solution to this. Feelers gonna feel, so we would probably see the discussion of unusual ideas moving away from public view and into anonymous blogs and private Slack group chats, and we will perhaps also see the rise of some sort of shibboleth to identify in public if someone else is the sort of person with whom you can talk openly, or if on the contrary you have to stick to the mandated canon.

But sex redistribution as an abstract hypothetical is not spicy enough for Nintil, so let's talk about taking your kidneys and giving them to the kidneyless.

It is my view that relatively widespread moral views - that lead to endorsing income redistribution - do not obviously end where the body begins. [Rawlsians](https://nintil.com/2017/08/06/rise-and-fall-of-rawlsianism/) should be particularly worried, and likewise for all those that hold the view that "That which is not deserved can be redistributed away unproblematically". We do not deserve our friends either, should we redistribute friends?

Recently, moral philosopher [Cécile Fabre](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v2YczpQPPtM) actually made the case -not just mused around- that income redistribution does indeed imply, in that case, organ redistribution. Eric Mack, her opponent in the debate sees this as a reductio of the arguments for income redistribution.
