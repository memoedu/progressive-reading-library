# A brief argument with apparently informed global warming denialists

---

## 正文

我浪费了一些时间，与一群这样的 climate change skeptics 争论；我想，把这里发生的事情分享出来，供日后参考，应该会很有意思。

先交代一下背景：这件事发生在一个 political debate FB group 里。有人发了一条评论，赞扬 Trump 退出 Paris Agreement。我立即评论说，global warming 是 real，而且是 man-caused。

对此，我收到了以下回应：

-   有人贴出了一张展示几千年来 CO2 的 varying levels 的图片（从 600 million years ago 开始），讽刺地说：“哈哈，看看 CO2 如何 affects life”，还附了一张 dinosaurs 的图片。
-   这些 models 只是被 designed 来 fit 过去，而且它们的 predictions 总是 fail。
-   有人说，global warming 是 real，但还有其他 factors（sun、cosmic radiation...）才是 main drivers，而且 States 有意利用 climate science 来 justify 更多 state intervention。
-   [这个](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Smhn1gL6Xg&feature=youtu.be)视频
-   不需要了解 climate science，也能知道 politicians 有 incentives，而且已经据此采取行动（climate scientists 也是如此），以 twist the facts。

我随后说，除非一个人确实 studied climatology，而且真正 understands 这场 debate，否则他 disbelieving 一个 scientific consensus 就没有正当理由。毕竟，这个 consensus 代表着数千人为 finding the truth 而付出的努力。接着，这又招来了……

-   Scientific consensus 以前也曾是 wrong！人们起初不同意 Louis Pasteur，scientific consensus 曾 agreed on geocentrism，scientific consensus 也曾 agreed on lobotomy。
-   economics 中的 Scientific consensus 也 rejects the Austrian School！
-   Truth 不是由 democracy 决定的。global warming 的 Believers 就是一群绵羊，追随 ecohisteric propaganda，并盲目相信它。甚至连 ex-UN officials 都已 proven，global warming 是一场 political farce。
-   你没有读过 relevant literature，就在一个 scientific issue 上 taking a stand。

 我随后 argued，人不必时时都做 literature reviews。例如，我说 homeopathy 不 work，却没有读过 homeopaths 的说法。我在[这里](https://nintil.com/2016/04/10/on-the-express-acceptance-and-rejection-of-beliefs/)和[这里](https://nintil.com/2016/04/12/on-the-express-acceptance-and-rejection-of-beliefs-ii/)谈过这件事。有人问我，是否 understand homeopathy 据称是如何 work 的；我的确 understand，就像我 understand climate science 的 basics 一样，但我没有读过那些 papers，其中 homeopaths specify 了 homeopathy 据称借以 work 的 molecular-level mechanism（没错，这些 papers 确实存在）。

-   可你这样怎么能 reject homeopathy！如果你没读过他们的 papers，就不能对 homeopathy 说三道四！
-   但 homeopaths 之间存在 consensus，认为 homeopathy work

我只是说，我并非在为自己的任何 conclusion 进行 arguing，只是在 pointing to the consensus；对方却告诉我，没有足够的 formation，就不能 make claims。接着，对方让我去看一份[相当长的文档](http://www.herbogeminis.com/IMG/pdf/historia_del_clima_de_la_tierra_anton_uriarte.pdf)，说我在开口之前应该先读完。这篇文字出自一位知名 climate skeptic，长达 200 多页，并坚持说，不能凭 consensus 就让某件事成为 true。（这不是我说过的话，而且在我看来，这通常是不言而喻的。）此外，他还说根本不存在 consensus。

我问，这是怎么说的？

随后，对方给了我一份[scientists 名单](http://www.petitionproject.org/)的链接，这些人签署了一封 2007 年的信，声称 climate warming 是一场 hoax，自 1998 年以来就不存在 warming，等等。这被用来 show 不存在 consensus，还有一些人赞扬那条评论，讽刺地说：“哦，我们不该相信这些 scientists，只能相信 UN 的 public employees 和 bureaucrats……”

随后，我给出了 skepticalScience 对那份名单的 debunking 链接，在[这里](https://skepticalscience.com/OISM-Petition-Project-intermediate.htm)，以及对自 1998 年以来不存在 warming 这一 claim 的 debunking 链接，在[这里](https://skepticalscience.com/global-warming-stopped-in-1998-intermediate.htm)。但对方相当激烈地提醒我，consensus 并不存在，还给出了[这个视频](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSrjAXK5pGw&feature=youtu.be)的链接，声称它能 prove 不存在 consensus。我再次用[这个](https://niskanencenter.org/blog/should-skeptics-concede-the-climate-consensus/)和[这个](https://niskanencenter.org/blog/study-humans-behind-recent-climate-change/)回应。基本上，关于 global warming 的 consensus 并不 depend on John Cook 的某一项 study；考虑到其余 evidence，仅仅因为只有 papers 总数的一部分 express an opinion，就说不存在 consensus，是 nonsense；一个认为某个 theory 已经 settled 的人，不会去 explicitly endorsing 它；而如果只看那些确实 express an opinion 的 papers，仍有 97% endorse the consensus。Absence of evidence 并不是 evidence of absence。

迎接这些回应的是嘲笑。

* * *

大多数人在讨论与自身 identity 密切相关的话题时，会变得比平常更加 irrational。Climate change 在某些人看来，是“由 leftists defended 的东西”“由 governments pushed 的东西”“会 imply massive state intervention 的东西”。它完全可能同时具有这些特点。的确，leftists 对 climate change 的 reality 的 agreement 程度更高，governments 也确实 fund climate science，并 implement measures 来 fight 它；但对此，正确的态度是：“那又怎样？”要通过 postulating 一场横跨整个 field 的 conspiracy 来 reject 一个 consensus，一个人得是极端的 cynic，而且必须拿出 substantial evidence，表明确实存在这样的 conspiracy。

* * *

这个案例很有意思，因为这里的 skeptic 似乎确实读过一些东西，而我承认自己读得很少；我只是在把 global warming 是 manmade 且 real 这一 belief 接受为 true 之前，确认这个 consensus 确实如表面所见那样存在。从这位 skeptic 的角度看，这会是什么样子？

或许会让他觉得受到了侮辱。这位 skeptic 认为，自己已经做过 going through the evidence 的工作，仔细 weighing 了 official truth 和 contrarian dissenters，思考过 policymakers 和 scientists 的 incentive structure，并在此基础上 concluded 这个 consensus 是 wrong。然后，一个对这个话题没读过多少东西的人走过来，毫不在意地略过他付出的努力，直接指出他是 wrong，而 consensus 是 right。从他的角度看，我可能显得无知，或者差不多是那样。

我们该如何解决这些 disputes？当然，一个人可以去读 climatology，读遍所有 skeptics 的论述，再回到 debate 中，with precision 地 argue 所有需要 argued 的问题。但这需要时间。正如我上面给出的链接所说，practical rationality 的一项 key skill，是通过挑选好的 second hand sources，能够迅速地 reject 或 accepts beliefs，而无需 extensive research。那么，如果我不打算读这位 skeptic 的说法，我们又该如何解决这些 disputes？我当然尝试过给出 skeptical science 的链接，那里已经有人替我完成了所有这些 analysis，但那些链接大多被无视了。

另一种做法是，只 analyse 一个 argument，看看会发生什么。在这个案例中，我 analysed 了 PragerU 的视频，并 explained 它为什么是 wrong，以及为什么即使经过 Alex Epstein 的 nitpicking，consensus 仍然 holds。这很重要，因为这位 skeptic 一直在说不存在 consensus。在 showing 一个 core argument 是 wrong 之后（这只花了几分钟），这位 skeptic 难道不应该 reconsider 吗？嗯，这次并没有。但对方也没有提出 counterargument，只是在嘲笑我。这个案例已经没有希望了，所以我当时就停止了争论。我也略微 updated my views，更倾向于认为“只付出很少的努力，确实很难改变别人的想法”。

* * *

这件事有没有可能处理得更好？假设我是一位 climatologist。我就不会被认为无知了，可是，要如何 counteract 针对某个 scientific inquiry 的 field 的 generalised cynism？也许根本做不到。也许有些 skeptics 真的无可救药，但希望一些读过这场交锋、原本尚未决定的人，会觉得我是对的，而他不是。

我很久以前就不再在 twitter 上争论了，因为那完全没有意义。也许我应该把这一做法推广到所有地方，只在自己确实做过 relevant research 的 fields 中进行 debates。在这一点上，我 endorse Michael Huemer 的[这篇文章](https://www.facebook.com/mike.huemer/posts/10156206523549115)。

## 原文

I lost some time arguing with a bunch of these climate change skeptics, and I thought it would be interesting to share what happened here for future reference.

To give you some context, this happened in a political debate FB group. Someone posted a comment praising Trump for walking out the Paris Agreement. I immediately commented that global warming is real, and that it is man-caused.

This was met by the following:

-   Someone posted a picture of varying levels of CO2 throughout the millenia (Starting 600 million years ago), saying ironically that "Haha, see how CO2 affects life", plus a picture of some dinosaurs.
-   That the models are just designed to fit the past and always fail in their predictions.
-   Someone saying that global warming is real, but that there are other factors (sun, cosmic radiation...) that are the main drivers, and that States are interested in using climate science to justify more state intervention.
-   [This](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Smhn1gL6Xg&feature=youtu.be)video
-   That there is no need to know climate science to know that politicians have incentives, and have acted on them (and same for climate scientists) to twist the facts.

I then said that unless one has really studied climatology and actually understands the debate, one is not justifying in disbelieving a scientific consensus. After all, the consensus represents the efforts of thousands of people dedicated to finding the truth about something. In turn, this got...

-   Scientific consensus was wrong before! People initially disagreed with Louis Pasteur, scientific consensus agreed on geocentrism, scientific consensus agreed on lobotomy.
-   Scientific consensus in economics rejects the Austrian School too!
-   Truth is not dictated by democracy. Believers in global warming are sheeps who follow ecohisteric propaganda and blindly believe in it. It has been proven, even by ex-UN officials that global warming is a political farce.
-   You are taking a stand on a scientific issue without having read the relevant literature.

 I argued then that one need not do literature reviews all the time. For example, I say that homeopathy doesn't work without having read what homeopaths say. I talked about this [here](https://nintil.com/2016/04/10/on-the-express-acceptance-and-rejection-of-beliefs/) and [here](https://nintil.com/2016/04/12/on-the-express-acceptance-and-rejection-of-beliefs-ii/). I was asked if I understand how homeopathy is claimed to work, and I do in the same way I understand the basics of climate science, but I have not read the papers wherein homeopaths specify molecular-level mechanism through which homeopathy is supposed to work (Yes, those papers exist).

-   But how can you then reject homeopathy! You cannot say anything about homeopathy if you haven't read their papers!
-   But there is consensus among homeopaths that homeopathy work

I just said that I am not arguing for any conclusion of my own, just pointing to the consensus, and I was told that one can't make claims without adequate formation. I was then pointed to a [rather long document](http://www.herbogeminis.com/IMG/pdf/historia_del_clima_de_la_tierra_anton_uriarte.pdf)that I was supposed to read before saying anything. The text is written by a known climate skeptic and it is over 200 pages long, and insisted that something cannot be made true by consensus. (Something that I didn't say, and something that I think is generally obvious) Ad furthermore he said that there is no consensus.

How so, I asked?

I was then linked to a[list of scientists](http://www.petitionproject.org/) that signed a 2007 letter saying that climate warming is a hoax, and that there is no warming since 1998, etc. This was supposed to show there is no consensus, and some others praised that comment, saying ironically that "Oh, we shouldn't believe in these scientists, only in public employees and bureaucrats from UN..."

I then linked to a skepticalScience debunking of that list, [here](https://skepticalscience.com/OISM-Petition-Project-intermediate.htm), and the claim that there has been no warming since 1998 [here](https://skepticalscience.com/global-warming-stopped-in-1998-intermediate.htm) but I was reminded quite vehemently that consensus doesn't exist, being linked to [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SSrjAXK5pGw&feature=youtu.be) that purports to prove that there is no consensus. Once again I replied  with [this](https://niskanencenter.org/blog/should-skeptics-concede-the-climate-consensus/) and [this](https://niskanencenter.org/blog/study-humans-behind-recent-climate-change/). Essentially, the consensus on global warming does not depend on  a single study by John Cook, and that saying that there is no consensus because just a fraction of the total number of papers express an opinion, is nonsense given the rest of the evidence, that someone who thinks of a theory as settled won't be explicitly endorsing it, and that if one takes papers that do have an opinion, still 97% endorse the consensus. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.

This was met with laughter.

* * *

Most people get even more irrational than they already are when discussing topics close to their own identity. Climate change look to certain people like something "defended by leftists", "pushed by governments", "something that would imply massive state intervention". It could well be all those things. Indeed, agreement with the reality of climate change is greater among leftists, and governments do fund climate science and implement measures to fight it, but the right attitude to this is "So what". One has to be a tremendous cynic to reject a consensus by postulating a conspiracy across a whole field, and would need substantial evidence that there is.

* * *

This case is interesting because the skeptic in question seems to have done some reading, and I was admitting to having done little, beyond ensuring that the consensus is what it looks like it is before accepting as true the belief that global warming is manmade and real. How would this look like from the skeptic's perspective?

Insulting, perhaps. The skeptic thinks he has done the work of going through the evidence, carefully weighing the official truth and contrarian dissenters, thinking about the incentive structure of policymakers and scientists, and on the basis of that, he has concluded that the consensus is wrong. Then someone who has done little reading on the topic comes and points out, without caring about examining his efforts, that he is wrong and the consensus is right. I might have came across as ignorant or something like that, from his side.

How are we to solve these disputes? Of course, one could read about climatology, read all of the skeptics, and then come back to the debate and argue whatever has to be argued with precision. But that takes time. As I linked above, a key skill in practical rationality is to be able to reject or accepts beliefs swiftly and without extensive research, thanks to picking good second hand sources. How can we solve these disputes then if I am not going to read what the skeptic says? I did of course try to link to skeptical science, where all this analysis has already been done for me, but those links were mostly disregarded.

Instead, what one can do is to just analyse one argument and see what happens. In that case, I analysed the video from PragerU, and explained why it is wrong, and why the consensus still holds even after Alex Epstein's nitpicking. This is important, because the skeptic has been saying that there is no consensus. After showing that one core argument is wrong (it took just some minutes), shouldn't the skeptic reconsider? Well, not in this case. But no counterargument was made, I was merely laughed at. This case is hopeless, and I stopped arguing at that point. I also updated my views slighly towards the "it is really hard to change people's minds with minimal effort"

* * *

Could it have been done better? Let's say I were a climatologist. I wouldn't have been perceived as ignorant, but how do you counteract generalised cynism about about a field of scientific inquiry? Maybe you cannot. Maybe some skeptics are really hopeless, but hopefully some people who read the exchange and were undecided thought I was correct and he wasn't.

I stopped debating on twitter a long time ago, as it is completely pointless. Maybe I should extend that everywhere and limit any debates to fields where I have actually done the relevant research. In that, I endorse [this post](https://www.facebook.com/mike.huemer/posts/10156206523549115) by Michael Huemer.
