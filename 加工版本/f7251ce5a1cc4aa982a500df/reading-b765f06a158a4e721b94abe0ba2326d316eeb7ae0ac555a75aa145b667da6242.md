# My opponents are all biased!

---

## 正文

我时常看到人们在 arguments 中说出这样的话：“我觉得，许多 hold <opposing position> 的人之所以如此，是因为他们是 biased。”

无论其 truth value 如何，我都不认为这是一个值得提出的 argument，原因有几个：

-   它并没有真正 address 那个 opposing position 的 substance。如果你认为某人之所以 holds 一个 opposing position，是因为他们是 biased，而你没有那么 biased，那么你必然认为有更 strong 的 evidence 来支持你的 position。那么，既然你可以直接就 facts 展开 argue，又何必把 argument 提高一个 level，转到人们的 biases 上呢？

-   这听起来像是 _ad hominem_。Accusing 你的 opponents 有 bias，很容易让他们进入 defensive 状态，并 cut off 他们的 line of retreat。这样一来，如果他们 change their mind，也就同时在 implicitly endorsing 你的 claim，即他们之前是 biased。因此，claiming 你的 opponents 是 biased，会让他们更不可能 change their minds。

-   这是在 [epistemic](http://lesswrong.com/lw/9z/the_epistemic_prisoners_dilemma/) prisoner’s [dilemma](http://lesswrong.com/lw/bt/the_true_epistemic_prisoners_dilemma/) 中 defecting。即使你觉得自己 _确实非常确信_ 自己站在正确的一方，而你的 opponents 是 biased，他们大概也同样确信自己站在正确的一方，而你是 biased。而你们 _双方_ 都开始 arguing 谁才是 biased 的那个人，对谁都没有帮助。

---

## 原文

I somewhat often see people, during arguments, make remarks along the lines of: “I think that many people who hold <opposing position> do so because they’re biased.”

Regardless of its truth value, I don’t think this is a useful argument to make, for a couple reasons:

-   It doesn’t really address the substance of the opposing position. If you think that someone holds an opposing position because they’re biased, and you’re not as biased, then you must think that your position has stronger evidence for it. So why take the argument up a level, to people’s biases, when you can just argue about facts?

-   It reads like an _ad hominem_. Accusing your opponents of bias is a great way to get them on the defensive and cut off their line of retreat. Now if they change their mind, they are also implicitly endorsing your claim that they were biased before. So claiming that your opponents are biased makes them less likely to change their minds.

-   It’s defecting on the [epistemic](http://lesswrong.com/lw/9z/the_epistemic_prisoners_dilemma/) prisoner’s [dilemma](http://lesswrong.com/lw/bt/the_true_epistemic_prisoners_dilemma/). Even if you feel _really sure_ that you’re on the right side and your opponents are biased, they’re probably equally sure that they’re on the right side and you’re biased. And it doesn’t help anyone for you _both_ to start arguing over who’s the biased one.
