**AI**:

- ["Mastering The Game of Go without Human Knowledge"](/doc/reinforcement-learning/model/alphago/2017-silver.pdf#deepmind "'Mastering the game of Go without human knowledge', Silver et al 2017"), Silver et al 2017:

    [Detailed commentary](https://www.reddit.com/r/reinforcementlearning/comments/778vbk/mastering_the_game_of_go_without_human_knowledge/); the final hurrah of the now-closed DM research programme, AlphaGo Zero is trained from scratch without any human data using pure self-play to truly superhuman levels of Go play, far surpassing AlphaGo Sedol, using an order of magnitude less computing power; the key ingredient appears to be a breakthrough in stabilizing self-play, by training the NN's value estimates, each move, towards the value estimates as finetuned by a MCTS search. This provides improved estimates, but also (somehow?) manages to prevent catastrophic forgetting and provides incredibly stable self-play training. It may be clearer to read the independent invention of ["Thinking Fast and Slow with Deep Learning and Tree Search"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08439), Anthony et al 2017, as well as ["Learning Generalized Reactive Policies using Deep Neural Networks"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.07280), Groshev et al 2017. Self-play has been underused in deep RL because it is so hard to stabilize despite being a seductively general technique, and despite the limitation of requiring a MCTS-able setting, I think this may turn out to be a big breakthrough for deep RL, and perhaps in the long run more important than the original AlphaGo paper.

    ![AlphaGo Zero: 'just stack moar layers lol!'](/doc/reinforcement-learning/2017-12-24-gwern-meme-nnlayers-alphagozero.jpg "Humorous description of the simplicity of the AlphaGo Zero architecture compared to AlphaGo Master"){.float-right}
- ["ProGAN: Progressive Growing of GANs for Improved Quality, Stability, and Variation"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10196#nvidia "'Progressive Growing of GANs for Improved Quality, Stability, and Variation', Karras et al 2017"), Karras et al 2017 ([source](https://github.com/tkarras/progressive_growing_of_gans); [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XOxxPcy5Gr4); GANs generating photorealistic 1024x1024px faces, better than [StackGAN++](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10916 "'StackGAN++: Realistic Image Synthesis with Stacked Generative Adversarial Networks', Zhang et al 2017") This jumps right over the uncanny valley to, for many of these, I can no longer tell the difference)
