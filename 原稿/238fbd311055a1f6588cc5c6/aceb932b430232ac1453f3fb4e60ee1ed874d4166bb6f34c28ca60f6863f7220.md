### [Infinite Certainty](https://web.archive.org/web/20090203013258/http://www.spaceandgames.com/?p=27)

Filed under: [General](https://web.archive.org/web/20090203013258/http://www.spaceandgames.com/?cat=2 "View all posts in General") — Peter de Blanc @ 9:26 pm

On [Overcoming Bias](https://web.archive.org/web/20090203013258/http://www.overcomingbias.com/2008/01/infinite-certai.html), Eli says:

> I don’t think you could get up to 99.99% confidence for assertions like “53 is a prime number”. Yes, it seems likely, but by the time you tried to set up protocols that would let you assert 10,000 _independent_ statements of this sort - that is, not just a set of statements about prime numbers, but a new protocol each time - you would fail more than once. Peter de Blanc has an amusing anecdote on this point, which he is welcome to retell in the comments.
