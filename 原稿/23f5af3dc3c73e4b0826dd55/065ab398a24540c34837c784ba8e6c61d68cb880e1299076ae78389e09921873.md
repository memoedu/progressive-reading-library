[Preference Learning GPT-2 Music: Null Result](/gpt-2-preference-learning "Experiments with OpenAI's 'preference learning' approach, which trains a NN to predict global quality of datapoints, and then uses reinforcement learning to optimize that directly, rather than proxies.
