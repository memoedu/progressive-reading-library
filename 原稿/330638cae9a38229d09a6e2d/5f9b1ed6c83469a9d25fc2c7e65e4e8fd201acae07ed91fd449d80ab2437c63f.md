**Statistics/Meta-Science**:

- ["'Unbelievable': Heart Stents Fail to Ease Chest Pain"](https://www.nytimes.com/2017/11/02/health/heart-disease-stents.html), of [Al-Lamee et al 2017](/doc/statistics/causality/2017-allamee.pdf "Percutaneous coronary intervention in stable angina (ORBITA): a double-blind, randomized controlled trial")

    500,000+ people annually are operated on to insert stents starting in the 1990s; this surgery was never tested with placebo controls and was based on self-report measurements---rather than hard endpoints like mortality---before being rolled out, despite surgeries failing half the time in placebo-controlled studies ([Wartolowska et al 2014](https://www.bmj.com/content/348/bmj.g3253 "Use of placebo controls in the evaluation of surgery: systematic review"), see also [_Ending Medical Reversal_](https://www.amazon.com/Ending-Medical-Reversal-Improving-Outcomes/dp/1421417723)), as doctors thought stents not working was "unbelievable" and US IRBs would have killed a placebo-controlled study of stents as being 'unethical'. The non-US study found minimal benefit (blinded effect size 1/6th that of non-blinded). Said study is, nevertheless, expected to not affect stent surgeries or IRB standards. As Yudkowsky asks, "Why aren't they rioting?"
