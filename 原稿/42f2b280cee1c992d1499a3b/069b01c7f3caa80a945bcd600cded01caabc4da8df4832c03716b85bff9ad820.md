### Statistics/AI/meta-science

- ["What my deep model doesn't know..."](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/yarin.gal/website/blog_3d801aa532c1ce.html) (Something of a tour de force: isomorphism between [Gaussian processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_process) and deep neural networks showing [dropout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dropout_%28neural_networks%29) is equivalent to using 1 NN to average over a whole family of similar models (explaining why dropout improves results, since we all know the advantages of ensembles) and showing how variation in NN output when wiggled by dropout gives an indication of uncertainty in predictions and from there yields useful results and stuff like usable [Thompson sampling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thompson_sampling) (!) in the famous [deep Q reinforcement-learner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q-learning#Deep_Q-learning) for optimizing exploration and learning faster. Phew.)
