# Books

**Fiction**:

- [_Bone_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bone_(comics)) (Smith 2004; a doorstopper of an omnibus volume, I picked this up at an Anthropic retreat where there was a free library of books contributed by employees.
  I was pleased to see two copies of [_Radiance_](https://gwern.net/doc/radiance/2002-scholz-radiance), and successfully talked people into taking them. _Bone_ stood out for its sheer size and being the only comic book or manga I saw, and I was intrigued by the endorsements on the back, rankings of it being one of the greatest Western comic books, and comments about power and fanaticism---all of which which hinted at greater depth than the first few pages indicated, anyway...
  I felt I could use a break from more serious topics like AI RSI or startup fundraising, so I took it back from the ranch with me.
  I read it over the next few weeks and was ultimately disappointed.
  The characters and humor are in a standard Western Disney juvenile style, barely even adolescent level, with the titular 'Bones' being flat and irritating the entire time; the story can only be described as deeply cliche and derivative---give me a _Redwall_ narrative any day!---while ending relatively abruptly and unsatisfyingly after endless back-and-forth tedious chasing around the countryside and foreboding forested mountains.
  The promised depth never arrived, to the point where I'm still unsure what the primary antagonist's motives even *were*.
  The sole selling point of _Bone_ for me winds up being Smith's artwork: he makes striking use of black-and-white artwork which capitalizes on the settings.
  An example is the deuterprotagonist's dream of being handed over to the dragons at night: as thoroughly silly as the dragon in question looks, Smith nevertheless makes gorgeous use of the dragon's white eyes against a black page.
  Many pages are works of art, once scrubbed of speech bubbles with lame dialogue.
  Overall, this would probably be a good comic omnibus for an elementary school kid, but I didn't much enjoy it.)
