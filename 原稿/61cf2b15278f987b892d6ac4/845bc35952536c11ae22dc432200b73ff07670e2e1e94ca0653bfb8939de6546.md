Subreddit for discussing AI, machine learning, or deep learning approaches involving **big** numbers: billions of parameters, millions of *n*, petaflops, etc. eg. [GPT-3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165#openai "‘Language Models are Few-Shot Learners’, Brown et al 2020"). Most research is conducted at much smaller scale; this subreddit is for research analogous to 'high energy physics', requiring specialized approaches, large investments, consortium, etc.

**Topics**: How? Who? Why do they work? What are they good for? What resources are available? Who will pay & how? What is the future of such approaches? What global consequences will there be?
