February 2024's [Gwern.net](https://gwern.net/newsletter/2024/02) [newsletter](https://gwern.substack.com/ "'Gwern.net newsletter (Substack subscription page)', Gwern 2013") is now out; previous, [January 2024](https://gwern.net/newsletter/2024/01) ([archives](https://gwern.net/doc/newsletter/index)).
This is a collation of links and summary of major changes, overlapping with my [Changelog](https://gwern.net/changelog); brought to you by my donors on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/gwern).

# Writings

- Gwern.net: new **file-transclusion** feature---browse images/videos/documents in the annotations & tag-directories (eg. [Midjourney](https://gwern.net/doc/ai/nn/diffusion/midjourney/index), [DALL·E 3](https://gwern.net/doc/ai/nn/transformer/gpt/dall-e/3/index)); +video thumbnails; new ['GTX' text format](https://github.com/gwern/gwern.net/blob/master/build/GTX.hs) to replace YAML; GPT-4-V demos: [`image-margin-checker.py`](https://github.com/gwern/gwern.net/blob/master/build/old/image-margin-checker.py), [`invertornot.py`](https://github.com/gwern/gwern.net/blob/master/build/invertornot.py)
- Proposals: ["Novelty nets"](https://gwern.net/novelty-net): adapter layers which memorize history of generated samples to encourage unseen samples; [planning in AUNNs](https://gwern.net/aunn#pondering)
