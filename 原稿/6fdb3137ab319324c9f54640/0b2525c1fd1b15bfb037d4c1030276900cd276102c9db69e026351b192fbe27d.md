# Writings

- [Sperm selection as minor enhancement to embryo selection](/embryo-selection#sperm-phenotype-selection "Sperm can be selected on traits such as mobility, which are measures of quality. These may be correlated with genetics for adult traits, and one can select from billions of sperm. Estimating the gain, it is probably worthwhile but small.")
