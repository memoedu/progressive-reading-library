Results don’t come from isolated pieces of advice. They come from having a system.

I was reminded of this by a comment I received on a recent essay. The person liked the ideas I was sharing, but they weren’t sure how to integrate all of it into an actual project they could succeed with.

Understanding that retrieval practice matters for learning doesn’t automatically give you a system to efficiently master everything you need to know for an exam. And understanding the importance of deliberate practice doesn’t mean you’re consistently improving in your work. The difference between advice and an effective system is not just the difference between knowing and doing, although that’s a big part. It’s having a complete picture instead of just isolated fragments.
