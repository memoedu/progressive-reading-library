When I was a high-schooler, I greatly enjoyed [Ryan North’s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_North "Ryan North") formalist _[Dinosaur Comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinosaur_Comics "Dinosaur Comics")_; I was impressed by how much he could do within the strict limit of his template, and I tried my hand at a few comics. Re-reading them, I wish I had been more careful about the spelling & formatting, but they hold up better than most of my juvenilia.
