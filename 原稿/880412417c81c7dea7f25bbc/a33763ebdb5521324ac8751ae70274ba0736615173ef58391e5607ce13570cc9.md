[`ThisWaifuDoesNotExist.net`](https://www.thiswaifudoesnotexist.net/) ([TWDNE](/twdne "‘This Waifu Does Not Exist’, Gwern 2019")) is a static website which uses JS to display random [anime faces generated by StyleGAN](/face "‘Making Anime Faces With StyleGAN’, Gwern 2019") neural networks, along with [GPT-3](/gpt-3 "‘GPT-3 Creative Fiction’, Gwern 2020")-generated anime plot summaries. Followups: ["This Pony Does Not Exist" (TPDNE)](https://thisponydoesnotexist.net/ "‘This Pony Does Not Exist’, Arfafax 2020")/["This Fursona Does Not Exist" (TFDNE)](https://www.thisfursonadoesnotexist.com/ "‘This Fursona Does Not Exist’, Arfafax 2020")/["This Anime Does Not Exist" (TADNE)](https://thisanimedoesnotexist.ai/ "‘This Anime Does Not Exist.ai (TADNE)’, Nearcyan et al 2021").
