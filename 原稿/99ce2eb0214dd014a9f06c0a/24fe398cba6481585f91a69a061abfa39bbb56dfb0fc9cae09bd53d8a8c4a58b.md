> Commercial aviation is a mundane miracle: hundreds of people nap in a pressurized tube and arrive on schedule. My poem, inspired by a June 2025 departure from [SFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SFO "SFO"), praises that calm as an engineered faith in bolts, checklists, and invisible labor.
> 
> The poem rewrites [William Carlos Williams’s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Carlos_Williams "William Carlos Williams") “so much depends” as a structural refrain and stress frame. Each recurrence pivots viewpoint—mechanic, passenger, ground watcher, pilot, engineer—while [Bay Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Bay_Area "San Francisco Bay Area") landmarks trace the widening circle of ascent.
> 
> Technical diction carries the lyric load: torque to spec, witness marks, castellated nuts, Jet-A, autopilot… A century of wreckage and regulation compresses into routine, until the refrain breaks into “So— / much— / depends”, the missing period leaving dependency hanging in the air.

[So much depends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red_Wheelbarrow "The Red Wheelbarrow") / upon

the click of a bolt [torqued](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torque_wrench "Torque wrench")  
to seventy foot-pounds

in the hum of a [Renton hangar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeing_Renton_Factory "Boeing Renton Factory")  
before dawn,

holding wing-root  
to fuselage, metal  
to metal, two hundred souls / to air.

* * *

The jet banks wide past [San Bruno Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Bruno_Mountain "San Bruno Mountain"),  
spiraling over [Pacifica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacifica,_California "Pacifica, California"), seaward  
over darker blue—  
white scratch widening.

Inside: heads back, sleep masks down,  
screens dimmed. But one  
watches [the city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco "San Francisco")  
shrink, [bridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_bridges_in_the_San_Francisco_Bay_Area "List of bridges in the San Francisco Bay Area") thread to silver,  
[salt ponds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Bay_Salt_Ponds "San Francisco Bay Salt Ponds") checkered below.

At [Crissy Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crissy_Field "Crissy Field") a child  
looks up, tracks the flash:  
that [small bone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001:_A_Space_Odyssey#Visual_effects "<em>2001: A Space Odyssey</em> § Visual effects")  
thrown skywards, bearing hundreds.

Someone walking [the headlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marin_Headlands "Marin Headlands")  
sees it climb between  
blues—the sky, the sea, the sky—  
carrying hundreds  
toward gates unseen.

And so much depends / on what awaits.

* * *

Pilots dream in the sky;  
[horizons tilt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatial_disorientation "Spatial disorientation") until down is up,  
the [inner ear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_ear "Inner ear") [betrays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vestibular_system "Vestibular system"),  
[mirages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirage "<em>Mirage</em>") flicker across the glass,  
[clear air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altitude_sickness "Altitude sickness") [chokes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypoxia_\(medicine\) "Hypoxia (medicine)").

So much depends / on instruments over instinct.

* * *

At [Lands End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lands_End_\(San_Francisco\) "Lands End (San Francisco)") where cliffs  
fall to the Pacific, someone  
sees the sun-glint skate the bay:  
so much depends

upon spar bolts, splice plates,  
engineers’ calculations,  
torque to spec, [witness marks](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/witness_mark "witness mark"),  
[castellated nuts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castellated_nuts "Castellated nuts"),  
upon what was checked at dawn—  
hundreds carried on an inch of steel,  
engines inhaling [Jet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_fuel "Jet fuel")\-[A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_fuel#Jet_A/A-1 "Jet fuel § Jet A/A-1"),  
exhaling their debt as [fire](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/SXK87NgEPszhWkvQm/mundane-magic "'Mundane Magic', Yudkowsky 2026").

* * *

So much depends  
on [twelve seconds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wright_Flyer#Flight_trials_at_Kitty_Hawk "<em>Wright Flyer</em> § Flight trials at Kitty Hawk")  
at [Kill Devil Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_Devil_Hills,_North_Carolina "Kill Devil Hills, North Carolina"),  
that century  
a coast away  
from the sleeping passengers—

pilots fighting wings of ice,  
burning in their straps,  
gliding on with empty tanks,  
just vanishing—

they arrive at their gate  
on the appointed date—  
their least likely fate.

[Autopilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autopilot "Autopilot") holds;  
test pilots [auger into](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/auger_in "auger in") desert;  
engineers bend over tombstone books;  
crashes get written into clauses—  
a century of wreckage  
compresses to routine,  
so this morning you could doze / through heaven.

* * *

_So_—

the jet climbs still,  
spiraling through sun glare.  
Two hundred dreamers  
oblivious to their ascent,  
surrendered to autopilot,  
to what was torqued before dawn,  
to checklists humming in the ear—

_much_—

more than watchers can see:  
unstained, the white bird  
between two blues,  
climbing past late gulls,  
past [Farallones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farallon_Islands "Farallon Islands"),  
bone to bird to geometry—

_depends_

**[Strophe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strophe "<em>Strophe</em>") I**

At noon the span burns clean; the Gate’s red ribs keep their measure.  
Past San Bruno Mountain the bank begins—  
seaward across Pacifica’s white surf, then bayward,  
clockwise the radius holds in clear air.  
Offshore, sparing South City the sound.  
Sutro pins the noon; the ponds flare white.  
Container cranes in Oakland bow and hold.  
Someone shades their eyes; a child finds the loop—  
silver ellipse, again, again—  
and thinks: that white bone holds lives.  
_Still rising._

**[Antistrophe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antistrophe "<em>Antistrophe</em>") I**

Belts low and tight; a pressurized hush.  
The push sets in the seat and in the teeth.  
Three hundred sleepers tilt and lift in blue.  
Winglets scythe their arcs; the yaw damper steadies.  
Flaps stepping in; slats stowed; the nose writes circles.  
LNAV holds the lead; VNAV lifts the floor.  
Tablets map the climb; masks already down.  
One awake holds the window, will not blink.  
“Climb and maintain one-five thousand”, dry in the ear.  
The skin takes sun; the rivet lines run true.  
Phones pool small estuaries of blue.  
Inside: quiet thoroughness—checklist, crosscheck, callout.  
_Still rising._

**[Epode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epode "Epode") I**

Years paid this silence:  
Kill Devil Hills—twelve seconds held,  
desert floors where test flights failed,  
regs written in blood; Whitcomb’s tuned wing;  
centuries of wreckage and of math—  
before we learned to sleep through heaven’s door;  
so this noon could be nothing.

**Strophe II**

At Land’s End a walker tracks the chalk  
inscribed on blue—clockwise—by that mote.  
Far side: Tam; far off: the Farallones like coins.  
Down-bay the ponds go pink; the freeway braids.  
Gulls sheer off; the craft climbs past their reach.  
A hand lifts—shade or blessing—for the climb,  
the salt-bright light, the clean exactness—  
a white bone between blue and blue, unstained,  
carrying strangers to meetings, funerals,  
a lover at a gate.  
_Still rising._

**Antistrophe II**

Autothrottle keeps the bargain with the air.  
Hydraulics think in pounds; the pitot speaks in knots.  
Logbooks: o-rings, borescope, dye; night-shift hands.  
ETOPS is not a word here but a trust.  
In rows, the living cargo—errands and griefs;  
homecomings; a lover at a gate—  
lean back, go dark; the crew minds time and fuel.  
A finite engine compresses air, burns fuel,  
spends the heat as thrust, and keeps its covenant.  
_Still rising._

**Epode II**

All this to make a bus with wings—  
miracle compressed to Monday—  
so that a noon can stay a noon.  
_Still rising._  
Climb, then go level. Leave a thin white proof.
