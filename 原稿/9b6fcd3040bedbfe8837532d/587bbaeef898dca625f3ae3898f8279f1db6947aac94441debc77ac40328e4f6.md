::: {.editorial .collapse}
[Colophon.]{.margin-note} What is this and where did it come from? The context here is that Quanticle asked me "what's the vibes on Claude-4?" and I shot back "A struck gong. The crows launch from the trees. Behind them: the moon." (vibes/gongs, crows/commenters, moon/Anthropic-capabilities (despite little PR like OpenAI), get it?), parodying the [vibe coding Rick Rubin book](https://www.thewayofcode.com/ "‘<em>The Way of Code: The Timeless Art of Vibe Coding</em>’, Rubin 2025").

And then I realized that was a neat idea for a *waka*! I spent a while messing with the LLMs to see if they could come up with variations I liked.

Which they did; and so I curated and cleaned them up, tested the LLMs' critical judgment in pruning weaker or redundant entries and then rearranging the surviving variants into a pleasing order, generated some Midjourney/GPT-4o images for illustrations---and here we are.
