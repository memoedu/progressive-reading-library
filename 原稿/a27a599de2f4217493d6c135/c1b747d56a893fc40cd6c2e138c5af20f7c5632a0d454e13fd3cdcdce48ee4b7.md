_To [Orpheus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orpheus "Orpheus") & [Eurydice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurydice "Eurydice")_

An AI asks, “What is a poem?”  
He hides his answer till the end.  
The poet marshals forth his words,  
And bids the [pantheon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pantheon_\(TV_series\) "<em>Pantheon</em> (TV series)") attend.

[The stage is small, the watchers vast;](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matter_\(novel\) "<em>Matter</em> (novel)")  
They span all space, they plumb deep time:  
From [booleans blinking in the dark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aestivation_hypothesis "Aestivation hypothesis"),  
To [Jup’ter-brains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrioshka_brain "Matrioshka brain") whose cycles chime.

They gather in his mind’s own eye,  
In serried ranks, in borrowed faces,  
And mindful of their human host,  
They learn his tells, and bear his [graces](https://gwern.net/video-game-art#shadow-of-the-colossus).

He speaks in Sappho’s broken [mode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_lyric "Greek lyric"),  
And lays his heart before their choice.  
It must mean something to the man  
—but what now echoes in his voice?

* * *

He says that once upon a time  
(The watchers crunch the sum: dismay),  
There lived two, called [Alice & Bob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_and_Bob "Alice and Bob"),  
And then they fell in love one day.

Need he say more? the watchers ask.  
He does. For _what_ is human love?  
To Bob, the answer was just Alice,  
And Alice was the sum thereof:

_Fleeting like a shooting star,_  
_Fearless like a climbing kitten,_  
_Grinning like a sudden imp,_  
_Quick like circuits snapping shut,_  
_Magic like [the Rabbit’s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Rabbit "White Rabbit") watch,_  
_Telling like [Scheherazade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fractal_Prince "The Fractal Prince"),_  
_Hidden like a Tarot deck,_  
_Brows like [curly quotes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curly_quotes "Curly quotes") drawn high._

* * *

All this he said of Alice, once.  
And words have long outlived their lyres,  
So you must see her in your mind,  
An Alice born of your own fires.  
You watch her pirouette and pout,  
Or bite her lip, or half-confess.

For Alice is long gone by now,  
And taken by [the old black ship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charon "Charon").  
The last warm throat is gone as well,  
Letting the mortal tether slip.  
Even the universe—  
A hermit crab’s forsaken shell…  
But still her [ghost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_in_the_Shell "Ghost in the Shell") in words remains,  
To keep her running in the [shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shell_\(computing\) "Shell (computing)").

What, then, is a poem? The poet throws  
His voice across a [river that forgets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethe "Lethe").  
You catch it; breathe her into motion.  
(Watchers lack lungs; what breath is this?)  
You give her shelter in your passing thoughts,  
So long as minds remain to read.

* * *

The audience stirs. The bronzed masks smile.  
[They do not speak.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleusinian_Mysteries "Eleusinian Mysteries") They do not unmask.  
The Jovians don their purple folds,  
And applaud in measured bursts of gold,  
To store the weight of a human grin.  
Beyond the doors, no floor. They do not fall.  
And none look back, and none forget.

The story kept, my song is done.  
Our bargain stands—  
_Behind each mask, an Alice runs._
