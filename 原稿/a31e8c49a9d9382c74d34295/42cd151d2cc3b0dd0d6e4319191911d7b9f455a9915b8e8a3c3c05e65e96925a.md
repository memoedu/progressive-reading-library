According to a report by [index.hu](https://web.archive.org/web/20141114035021/http://index.hu/tech/2014/11/11/magyar_uzemeltette_a_sotet_web_egyik_drogpiacat/) citing a statement from the Hungarian police,  a 26 year old man who is the **alleged** operator of Hydra marketplace who got seized during [operation Onymous](https://web.archive.org/web/20141114035021/http://www.deepdotweb.com/tag/silkroad2bust/) – was arrested by TEK \[local Hungarian SWAT\], interrogated and later released (The released part is somewhat unclear yet) in Budapest, Hungary.

The police claims, that he was the admin of Hydra marketplace, since march 2014 and that he collected 5% commission from the site sales. This is the video of the bust itself::

During the operation there were:

-   17 arrests
-   13 search warrants issued
-   414 hidden sites seized (27 sites total)
-   Hardware and digital media seized
-   Bitcoins worth approximately USD 1 000 000 and EUR 180 000 in cash, drugs, gold and silver seized

Countries involved in the operation, codenamed ‘Onymous’, include Bulgaria, the Czech Republic, Finland, France, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Romania, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the USA.
