Over the more than decade I have worked on the [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Wikipedia "English Wikipedia"), I have written a few things. Some might wonder what, exactly, but not want to dig through >95,133[1](https://gwern.net/wikipedia-resume#fn1) edits (on English only). But even I don’t remember everything I have done!

# [Sources](https://gwern.net/wikipedia-resume#sources "Link to section: § 'Sources'")

I’ve done my best to pull together a list of my major contributions; I’ve based it on the results from the [toolserver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Toolserver "Wikipedia:Toolserver"), specifically 2 of [Soxred’s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:X! "User:X!") tools which return lists of pages created and articles most edited for a user:

2.  Articles created:

    -   [Gwern](https://xtools.wmcloud.org/pages/en.wikipedia.org/Gwern "Pages Created")
        

Those lists are unreliable, however. They are incomplete; ‘created’ may be misleading, as I make many [redirects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Redirect "Wikipedia:Redirect") which may be turned into an actual article by a later user (for example, I am credited with creating the valuable computer science article [green threads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_thread "Green thread") when all I did was redirect that name to the main article on [threads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thread_\(computing\) "Thread (computing)")). The tool tries to filter out redirects, but may be wrong. The most-edited listings are also misleading in the sense that a great many edits on a page may simply be undoing vandalism; I do not list [René Descartes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Descartes "René Descartes") below because my dozens of edits there are just reverts and I do not feel reverts are a particularly valuable service. Article writing is. Some such articles are listed where I have watched them for many years and feel that my supervision has risen to the level of a major contribution. Conversely, sometimes I have written articles offline and then added them as single giant edits with a few followup edits to fix formatting errors; such articles would not show up very highly on the most-edited list.

I am certain that some of the following entries are erroneous, and I have omitted some articles that should be included. But I have done what I can. I am especially proud of the highlighted articles such as the Medici bank, Brethren of Purity articles, and the Fujiwara no Teika article.
