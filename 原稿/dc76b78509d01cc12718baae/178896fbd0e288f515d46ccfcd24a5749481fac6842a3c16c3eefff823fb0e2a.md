# The Wild Calling

<div class="poem">
[**Strophe**](!W): <!-- NOTE: we mark the Pindarian triad structure of strope/antistrophe/epode the first time, but not thereafter, to reduce clutter. -->

<!-- First two opening gnome lines compressed per Pindaric convention (cf. Olympian 1); meter expands throughout the triad -->
Water gives life; || the sun lords over days; <!-- 3+3: WA-TER GIVES LIFE || SUN LORDS DAYS [l-l]; Apollo ref --> \
But gold is the well-wrought word, || god's-gift to the world. <!-- 3+3: GOLD WROUGHT WORD || GOD'S-GIFT WORLD [g-g]; Apollo ref, and echos later 'gift' --> \
Sing of the small saints, || silent, unnamed— <!-- 3+3: SING SMALL SAINTS || SI-LENT UN-NAMED [s-s] --> \
Ghost-mice of the grain-bins, || grown tame for human need, <!-- 3+3: GHOST-MICE GRAIN-BINS || GROWN TAME NEED [g-g] --> \
Who traded frost-field || for filter-cage frame, <!-- 3+3: TRAD-ED FROST-FIELD || FIL-TER-CAGE FRAME [f-f] --> \
And sealed a silent compact || no claw could carve away. <!-- 3+3: SEALED SI-LENT COM-PACT || CLAW CARVE A-WAY [k-k] -->

[**Antistrophe**](!W):

Grain-bins groaned once, gnawed || by grey guests who crept unbidden, <!-- 4+4: GRAIN-BINS GROANED GNAWED || GREY GUESTS CREPT UN-BID-DEN [g-g] --> \
Scuttling from cold fields || to farmer's flame-lit floor, all hunger, <!-- 4+4: SCUT-TLING COLD FIELDS || FAR-MER'S FLAME-LIT FLOOR HUN-GER [f-f] --> \
And farmers cursed the gnawing guest || that fouled the flour, fed on their labor, <!-- 4+4: FAR-MERS CURSED GNAW-ING GUEST || FOULED FLOUR FED LA-BOR [f-f] --> \
But [Lathrop](!W "Abbie Lathrop"), lone and listening, || led them from floor to living loft, <!-- 4+4: LATH-ROP LONE LIS-TEN-ING || LED FLOOR LIV-ING LOFT [l-l] --> \
Where [line bred line](!W "Linebreeding"), locked tight, || law inside blood—till [Jackson labs](!W "Jackson Laboratory") <!-- 4+4: LINE BRED LINE LOCKED TIGHT || LAW BLOOD JACK-SON LABS [l-l] --> <!-- Dense: 5 [l] for inbreeding weight --> \
Would call this pale strain forth || for the kept covenant of cure. <!-- 4+3: CALL PALE STRAIN FORTH || KEPT COVENANT CURE [k-k] -->

<div class="text-center">[**Epode**](!W):

Found cage; || fed need. <!-- 2+2: FOUND CAGE || FED NEED [f-f] --> \
The compact made, || the count began. <!-- 3+2: COM-PACT MADE || COUNT BE-GAN [k-k] -->
</div>
</div>

# The Temple & The Tithe

<div class="poem">
[Laminar wind](!W "Laminar flow cabinet"), unsoiled, || lifts lint from living fur; <!-- 3+3: LAM-I-NAR WIND UN-SOILED || LIFTS LINT LIV-ING FUR [l-l] --> \
Cedar scents the sanctum || where soft feet pace the grate. <!-- 3+3: CE-DAR SCENTS SANC-TUM || SOFT FEET PACE GRATE [s-s] --> \
[C-Fifty-Seven](!W "C57BL/6") bears || black blood-price; <!-- 3+3: C-FIF-TY-SEV-EN BEARS || BLACK BLOOD-PRICE [b-b] --> \
[Wistar](!W "Laboratory rat#Wistar rat"), white Adam, || waits, warehoused, watchful, <!-- 3+3: WIS-TAR WHITE A-DAM || WAITS WARE-HOUSED WATCH-FUL [w-w] --> \
Dawn brought the feeding hand; || dusk brought the snapping thumb— <!-- 3+3: DAWN BROUGHT FEED-ING HAND || DUSK BROUGHT SNAP-PING THUMB [d-d] --> \
The hand that filled the water || felled them with the final sleep. <!-- 3+3: HAND FILLED WA-TER || FELLED FI-NAL SLEEP [f-f] -->

[Ear-punch](!W) prints the witness || in numb cartilage, nailing the named; <!-- 4+4: EAR-PUNCH PRINTS WIT-NESS || NUMB CAR-TI-LAGE NAIL-ING the NAMED [n-n] --> \
[Tail-vein](!W) takes blue river || where [viral vectors](!W) ride to root; <!-- 4+4: TAIL-VEIN TAKES BLUE RIV-ER || VI-RAL VEC-TORS RIDE ROOT - vein/viral [v-v] --> \
[LD-Fifty](!W "Median lethal dose") weighs death in halves || while hush comes heavy, [CO~2~ closing](!W "Carbon dioxide#Minor uses")— <!-- 4+4: LD-FIF-TY WEIGHS DEATH HALVES || HUSH COMES HEA-VY CLOS-ING, halves / hush+heavy [h-h] --> \
Till thumb-snap shuts the spine-cord || and soul-spark, severed, is gone; <!-- 4+4: THUMB-SNAP SHUTS SPINE-CORD || SOUL-SPARK SE-VERED GONE [s-s] --> <!-- Dense: 5 [s] portrays finality --> \
[Sharps Bin](!W) stands red, ready, || sealing researchers' spent refuse; <!-- 4+4: SHARPS BIN RED REA-DY || SEAL-ING RE-SEARCH-ERS' SPENT REF-USE [r-r] --> <!-- Pickup: [s] --> \
[Steam-hell](!W "Autoclave") scrubs the silver || and stacks their souls in data's scroll. <!-- 4+4: STEAM-HELL SCRUBS SIL-VER || STACKS SOULS DA-TA'S SCROLL [s-s] -->

<div class="text-center">Cures come; || corpses count. <!-- 2+2: CURES COME || CORP-SES COUNT [k-k] --> \
Gift endures; || glory stays. <!-- 2+2: GIFT EN-DURES || GLO-RY STAYS [g-g] -->
</div>
</div>

# The Void-Watcher

<div class="poem">
[Stray mutt of Moscow](!W "Laika") || met the mounting moon, <!-- 3+3: STRAY MUTT MOS-COW || MET MOUNT-ING MOON [m-m] --> <!-- Dense: 5 [m] --> \
Caught from cold street || claimed for cosmic climb. <!-- 3+3: CAUGHT COLD STREET || CLAIMED COS-MIC CLIMB [k-k] --> <!-- Dense: 5 [k] for trap’s snap & capture-to-cosmos arc --> \
Bound in the [bolt-sphere](!W "Sputnik 2"), || in the blind black sky, <!-- 3+3: BOUND BOLT-SPHERE || BLIND BLACK SKY [b-b] --> \
Heart hammering hard || in hell's heat and noise— <!-- 3+3: HEART HAM-MER-ING HARD || HELL HEAT NOISE [h-h] --> <!-- Dense: 5×h for panting / hyperthermic haze --> \
She watched the wide world || whirl, a wheel of blue, <!-- 3+3: WATCHED WIDE WORLD || WHIRL WHEEL BLUE [w-w] --> \
A saint sent high || to silent sea of stars. <!-- 3+3: SAINT SENT HIGH || SI-LENT SEA STARS [s-s] -->

She burned as a beacon in the black, || where no hand could reach to bring her home, <!-- 4+4: BURNED BEA-CON BLACK || HAND REACH BRING HOME [b-b] --> \
And the wires that mapped her wild fear || wrote the way for men who followed after, <!-- 4+4: WIRES MAPPED WILD FEAR || WROTE WAY MEN FOL-LOWED [w-w] --> \
For the data won by dying dog || drove the path through endless night, <!-- 4+4: DA-TA WON DY-ING DOG || DROVE PATH END-LESS NIGHT [d-d] --> <!-- note Pindarian emphasis on double-victory in this couplet --> \
And the pilots who walked the waste she won || wore her death as warrant for their way, <!-- 4+4: PI-LOTS WALKED WASTE WON || WORE DEATH WAR-RANT WAY [w-w] --> \
First to fall in the fire of the future, || first to find what flesh could bear in flight— <!-- 4+4: FIRST FALL FIRE FU-TURE || FIRST FIND FLESH BEAR FLIGHT [f-f] --> <!-- Grace: 8 [f] for heroic intensity / future-fire rhetoric, the cumulative, overwhelming force of ascent/death --> \
The void looked back, and the void-watcher || vanished in the vast and lasting dark. <!-- 4+4: VOID LOOKED BACK VOID-WATCH-ER || VAN-ISHED VAST LAST-ING DARK [v-v] -->

<div class="text-center">Lives lost; || light from dark. <!-- 2+2: LIVES LOST || LIGHT DARK [l-l] --> \
She held the door; || dawn drew us through. <!-- 2+2: HELD DOOR || DAWN DREW [d-d] -->
</div>
</div>

# The Named Who Follow

<div class="poem">
[Dolly](!W "Dolly (sheep)"), donor's double, || dawned from an [udder cell](!W "Cloning"). <!-- 3+3: DOL-LY DO-NOR'S DOUB-LE || DAWNED UD-DER CELL [d-d] --> \
First mammal made from memory, || she died remembering none. <!-- 3+3: MAM-MAL MADE MEM-O-RY || DIED RE-MEM-BER-ING NONE [m-m] --> \
[OncoMouse™](!W), patented prophet, || proved the patent's price: <!-- 3+3: ON-CO-MOUSE PA-TEN-TED PRO-PHET || PROVED PA-TENT'S PRICE [p-p] --> \
Lawyers' unloved child— || yet lasting, law writ small. <!-- 3+3: LAW-YERS' UN-LOVED CHILD || LAST-ING LAW SMALL [l-l] --> \
See [the bronze in snow](!W "Monument to the laboratory mouse"), || stitching spiral, spectacled— <!-- 3+3; SEE the BRONZE in SNOW || STITCH-ing SPI-ral SPEC-ta-cled [s-s] --> \
Monument to the mute, || made by hands they healed. <!-- 3+3: MON-U-MENT MUTE || MADE HANDS HEALED [m-m] -->

Through these few the nameless million || lift for one line toward the light, <!-- 4+4: THROUGH FEW NAME-LESS MIL-LION || LIFT ONE LINE LIGHT [l-l] --> \
Breaking blur of billion || to blaze a few with lasting crown; <!-- 4+4: BREAK-ING BLUR BIL-LION || BLAZE FEW LAST-ING CROWN [b-b] --> \
For glory gives the singer's tongue || what silence stole from still and mute, <!-- 4+4: GLO-RY GIVES SING-ER'S TONGUE || SI-LENCE STOLE STILL MUTE [s-s] --> \
A name is a nail, and the nail || pins a soul to the wall of song— <!-- 4+4: NAME NAIL NAIL || PINS SOUL WALL SONG [n-n] --> <!-- Grace: pivot post-caesura [n]→[s] for gnomic force --> \
Yet the named are few, and the nameless mass || waits mute beyond the music's reach, <!-- 4+4: NAMED FEW NAME-LESS MASS || WAITS MUTE MU-SIC'S REACH [m-m] --> \
For each one blazed, a billion burn || in the blank of the bone-ledger's page. <!-- 4+4: EACH ONE BLAZED BIL-LION BURN || BLANK BONE-LED-GER'S PAGE [b-b] -->  <!-- Dense: 5×b for burn/blank/bone bookkeeping -->

<div class="text-center">Fame feeds; || flesh falls. <!-- 2+2: FAME FEEDS || FLESH FALLS [f-f] --> \
Speak them; || they stay. <!-- 2+2: SPEAK THEM || THEY STAY [s-s] -->
</div>
</div>

# The Grass-Gate Opens

<div class="poem">
Grass-gate gaped wide || for the glass-born, deemed surplus; <!-- 3+3: GRASS-GATE GAPED WIDE || GLASS-BORN DEEMED SUR-PLUS [g-g] --> \
Clear plastic paradise || cast out on cold ground. <!-- 3+3: CLEAR PLAS-TIC PA-RA-DISE || CAST COLD GROUND [k-k] --> \
Black mice huddled || where bleak breath bit hard; <!-- 3+3: BLACK MICE HUD-DLED || BLEAK BREATH BIT HARD [b-b] --> \
They nosed the tubs, nudged, || knew no name for earth; <!-- 3+3: NOSED TUBS NUDGED || KNEW NO NAME EARTH [n-n] --> \
[The false sun](!W "Dark-light cycle") sank behind; || fierce frost fell fast; <!-- 3+3: FALSE SUN SANK || FIERCE FROST FELL FAST [f-f] --> <!-- Dense: 5×f for cold’s assault --> \
The grass was a forest; || the cat was a god. <!-- 3+2: GRASS FOR-EST || CAT GOD [g-g] --> <!-- Grace: frame-alliteration; parallelism and scale-shift take precedence -->

They froze fast, at the plastic threshold— || paws pressing the damp earth, <!-- 4+4: FROZE FAST PLAS-TIC THRESH-OLD || PAWS PRESS-ING DAMP EARTH [p-p] --> \
False wind whisked whiskers— || wide world opened, owl-hushed; <!-- 4+4: FALSE WIND WHISKED WHISK-ERS || WIDE WORLD O-PENED OWL-HUSH [w-w] --> <!-- Dense: 5×w for wind-wide-world, the 'landscape of fear' <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology_of_fear> --> \
What covenant did we keep, || casting them into cold we could not follow? <!-- 4+4: CO-VE-NANT KEEP || CAST-ING COLD COULD NOT FOL-LOW [c-c] --> \
No contract signed in blood— || though blood had paid the bond already— <!-- 4+4: CON-TRACT SIGNED BLOOD || BLOOD PAID BOND AL-REA-DY [b-b] --> \
Warmth's bond of trust was broken || bare, black, without breath of warning, <!-- 4+4: WARMTH'S BOND TRUST BRO-KEN || BARE BLACK BREATH WARN-ING [b-b] --> <!-- Dense: 5 [b]; "black" recalls C57 workhorse and strophe's "black mice huddled" --> \
Cast from the engine's care || to the cold of the cat-god's ground. <!-- 4+4: CAST EN-GINE'S CARE || COLD CAT-GOD'S GROUND [k-k] -->

<div class="text-center">Trust broke; || truth stays. <!-- 2+2: TRUST BROKE || TRUTH STAYS [t-t] --> \
Warmth goes with them; || work remains. <!-- 2+2: WARMTH GOES || WORK RE-MAINS [w-w] -->
</div>
</div>

# The Gift of Song

<div class="poem">
[Pasteur](!W "Louis Pasteur") [broke](!W "Rabies vaccine") [rabies](!W); || [boy walked, breathing;](!W "Joseph Meister") <!-- 3+3: PAS-TEUR BROKE RA-BIES || BOY WALKED BREATH-ING [b-b] --> <!-- note: intentionally staccato since this is the strophe: the stark census that the antistrophe must transform. The telegraphic quality creates punch. They're gnomes, not narrative. --> \
[Salk](!W "Jonas Salk") sought the sign; || the swarm supplied. <!-- 3+3: SALK SOUGHT SIGN || SWARM SUP-PLIED [s-s] --> \
We walk on wide walls || of white-fur bone; <!-- 3+3: WALK WIDE WALLS || WHITE-FUR BONE [w-w] --> \
We drink the draft of cure, || distilled from their deaths; <!-- 3+3: DRINK DRAFT CURE || DIS-TILLED DEATHS [d-d] --> \
Their blood binds our bodies; || their breath bought our breath; <!-- 3+3: BLOOD BINDS BOD-IES || BREATH BOUGHT BREATH [b-b] --> \
Your child owes mornings || to the mice that met night. <!-- 3+3: CHILD OWES MORN-INGS || MICE MET NIGHT [m-m] -->

<!-- The last 3 lines of this antistrophe ending in a triple 'And' give us paratactic uplift for liturgical invocation: speech → naming → song → resurrection -->
But the poet brings praise where census || piles only number, count on count, <!-- 4+4: PO-ET BRINGS PRAISE CEN-SUS || PILES NUM-BER COUNT COUNT [p-p] --> \
For a name nailed fast by song || never melts from memory's wall, <!-- 4+4: NAME NAILED FAST SONG || NEV-ER MELTS MEM-O-RY'S WALL [n-n] --> \
So we speak the strains to wind: || Wistar, workhorse, white Adam, host— <!-- 4+4: SPEAK STRAINS WIND || WIS-TAR WORK-HORSE WHITE A-DAM HOST [w-w] --> \
And silent clans that science keeps || spring sunward, named at last. <!-- 4+4: SIL-ent CLANS SCI-ence KEEPS || SPRING SUN-ward NAMED LAST [s-s] --> \
And song speaks to silent souls || the sparks the sharp count stole. <!-- 4+4: SONG SPEAKS SI-LENT SOULS || SPARKS SHARP COUNT STOLE [sp-sp] --> <!-- Note: "Sparks" recalls "soul-spark, severed" from Triad II --> \
And the million souls forgotten rise, || riding this rhythm, borne to the sun. <!-- 4+4: MIL-LION SOULS FOR-GOT-TEN RISE || RI-DING RHY-THM BORNE SUN [r-r] --> <!-- Pindaric self-reference per genre convention: poet claims ode's power; "sun" = Apollo Smintheus, completing the dedication. 3 [r] lifts the line toward Apollo/sun of dedication. -->
