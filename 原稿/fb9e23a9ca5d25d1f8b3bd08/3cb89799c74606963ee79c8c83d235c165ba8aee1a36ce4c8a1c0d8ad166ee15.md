### AI

- ["A Step Toward Quantifying Independently Reproducible Machine Learning Research"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.06674), Raff 2019 ([blog](https://thegradient.pub/independently-reproducible-machine-learning/ "'Quantifying Independently Reproducible Machine Learning', Raff 2020"))
- [Robot startup Covariant.ai releases details](https://www.wired.com/story/ai-helps-warehouse-bots-pick-new-skills/ "AI Helps Warehouse Robots Pick Up New Tricks: Backed by machine learning luminaries, Covariant.ai’s bots can handle jobs previously needing a human touch"): in warehouse deployments, >95% picker success, ~600 items/hour (real-world success of DRL robotics from BAIR alumni & [Abbeel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pieter_Abbeel), using a relatively exotic mix---imitation learning + sim2real + transfer learning + meta-learning + fleet-learning---for high performance in robot arms picking up many kinds of objects)

- **Matters Of Scale**:

    - [Turing-NLG](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/turing-nlg-a-17-billion-parameter-language-model-by-microsoft/ "'Turing-NLG: A 17-billion-parameter language model by Microsoft', Rosset 2020"): a 17b-parameter LM, showing the usual log-linear gains from increasing parameters (1.5× [T5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.10683#google "'T5: Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer', Raffel et al 2019") & 2×  [MegatronLM](https://nv-adlr.github.io/MegatronLM "MegatronLM: Training Billion+ Parameter Language Models Using GPU Model Parallelism")) trained using [ZeRO](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02054#microsoft "'ZeRO: Memory Optimization Towards Training A Trillion Parameter Models', Rajbhandari et al 2019") (user-friendly library for splitting a single model across GPUs & then replicating that across the cluster, scaling to 100b parameters & planning for 1000b; competitor to [Mesh-Tensorflow](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.02084#google "'Mesh-TensorFlow: Deep Learning for Supercomputers', Shazeer et al 2018"))
    - ["A Constructive Prediction of the Generalization Error Across Scales"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12673), Rosenfeld et al 2019 (smooth power-law scaling of NN performance with data & model size across many architectures & datasets; see previously [Hestness et al 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.00409#baidu "Deep Learning Scaling is Predictable, Empirically")/[Shallue et al 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.03600#google "Measuring the Effects of Data Parallelism on Neural Network Training")/[McCandlish et al 2018](https://openai.com/research/how-ai-training-scales "How AI Training Scales: We've discovered that the gradient noise scale, a simple statistical metric, predicts the parallelizability of neural network training on a wide range of tasks. Since complex tasks tend to have noisier gradients, increasingly large batch sizes are likely to become useful in the future, removing one potential limit to further growth of AI systems. More broadly, these results show that neural network training need not be considered a mysterious art, but can be rigorized and systematized. ['An Empirical Model of Large-Batch Training', McCandlish et al 2018]")/[Kaplan et al 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361#openai "Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models"); found via [TR on OA](https://www.technologyreview.com/2020/02/17/844721/ai-openai-moonshot-elon-musk-sam-altman-greg-brockman-messy-secretive-reality/ "The messy, secretive reality behind OpenAI's bid to save the world"), which confirms OA is all-in on the scaling thesis and is working on a multimodal followup to GPT-2, presumably trained with model parallelism on a cluster, like Turing-NLG using ZeRO^[GPT-3 did require model parallelism on a cluster, but actually turned out to be text-only: ["GPT-3: Language Models are Few-Shot Learners"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165#openai), Brown et al 2020.])
    - ["REALM: Retrieval-Augmented Language Model Pre-Training"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.08909#google), Guu et al 2020 ([blog](https://research.google/blog/realm-integrating-retrieval-into-language-representation-models/ "'REALM: Integrating Retrieval into Language Representation Models', Chang & Guu 2020"); learning to query all of WP for question-answering; combo with [T5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.08910#google "'How Much Knowledge Can You Pack Into the Parameters of a Language Model?', Roberts et al 2020") pending)
    - ["Growing Neural Cellular Automata: Differentiable Model of Morphogenesis"](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/#google), Mordvintsev et al 2020 ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22300376); training tiny CNNs to coordinate as cellular automata to create complex damage-resilient global patterns, fun)
