## Tommy Convinces Howard to Come Back

**Howard:** I retired from playing A grade footy when I was twenty-five, twenty-six. When we went up to Croydon and then Evergreen, I didn't play. I was only twenty-six, twenty-seven, twenty-eight, twenty-nine. I retired early.

I said, "Well, actually, no I won't." I went on for another ten minutes and then said to Tommy, "I'm not going to be able to play on the weekend if this keeps up, because I already feel myself tightening up."

I played three or four games and then there were finals. In the fourth game I played fullback and I played really well—exceeded my own expectations. We won. But I pulled a muscle—not a hamstring, but in that region—and couldn't play the next week, which was the first final. The team lost, so I didn't play. I wouldn't have done it for anybody other than Tommy, probably.

And everybody else was happy, because the reserve grade team and the A grade team trained together, so the A grade coach—whom I knew—said, "Are you sure you don't want to play again? You're up to it, just seeing your training." I said, "Yeah, I'm sure." I was thirty, I think, then.
