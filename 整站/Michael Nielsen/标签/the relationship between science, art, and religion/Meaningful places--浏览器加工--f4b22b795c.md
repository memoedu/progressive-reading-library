**I've been:** Sagrada Familia[![World map](https://michaelnotebook.com/assets/places/worldmap.svg)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sagrada%20Fam%C3%ADlia%2C%20Barcelona)![The warm glass on the eastern side](https://michaelnotebook.com/assets/places/sagrada-1.jpg)![The cool green and blue glass opposite](https://michaelnotebook.com/assets/places/sagrada-2.jpg)![The nave, columns branching into the vault](https://michaelnotebook.com/assets/places/sagrada-3.jpg)![Light from the rose window across the vault](https://michaelnotebook.com/assets/places/sagrada-4.jpg)Barcelona. Gaudí worked on the church from 1883 until a tram killed him in 1926; it is still unfinished. He set the geometry with hanging weighted cords, whose curves, inverted, become arches in pure compression, so the columns lean and branch like trees, carrying the nave vaults 45 metres overhead with no buttress. Visited April 2024 and July 2025. Photos by me. The rim is limestone laid down 270 million years ago; a mile below, the Colorado runs over black rock nearly 1.7 billion years old, and between them the Great Unconformity, a single line where more than a billion years of the record is missing. Wide enough that you stand and watch rainstorms cross below you. Visited September 2022 and and again since. Photos by me. **"Travel is the only education":** I don't recall who said this, but it's stayed with me as containing some important insight. Perhaps because different places and different cultures are so incredibly and diversely generative. You stand in the Imperial Garden in Tokyo, and you're just immersed in this incredible depth around you. And then you visit the Strip in Las Vegas, and there's an entirely different type of depth. Even you can't articulate it, you can feel it, and it's reflected all around.

Something I don't like about the phrase is that it has medicinal, do-this-it's-good-for-you overtones. But good travel is refreshing and rejuvenating; it shouldn't be done for puritanical, let's check-off-the-checkboxes reasons.
